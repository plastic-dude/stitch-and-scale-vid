## Concept and distinctness

A blank or zero can look decisive while hiding whether the value was measured, entered, skipped, not applicable, unknown, unavailable, or never checked. The nearest prior angle is `commit-state`, which concerns save acknowledgement and interruption/return. `absence-state` changes the pain to a non-value being silently interpreted as a real value or a single kind of missingness. The hook becomes “Zero is not missing.” The visual metaphor changes from a save-status timeline to one numeric cell branching into `0 / NOT ENTERED / NOT APPLICABLE / UNKNOWN / NOT CHECKED`. The proof surface is `VALUE / STATUS / REASON / ASKED? / APPLIES? / CHECKED? / COUNTS-INCLUDED?`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from save confidence to honest calculation readiness. The final line is “Name the absence before you calculate.” It does not recreate `value-state`, `uncertainty-context`, `data-minimum`, `stale-number`, `availability-status`, `first-record`, `default-state`, `backup-state`, `commit-state`, imported `false-completion`, or imported `release-gate-roster`.
