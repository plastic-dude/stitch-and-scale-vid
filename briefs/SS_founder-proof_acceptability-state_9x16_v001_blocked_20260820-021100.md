## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or tester. He may explain why a visible change still needs intended-use requirements, objective tests, user evidence where relevant, limitations, a decision owner, and disposition before it is accepted for a particular purpose. Calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies a current acceptability surface with intended use, requirements or criteria, observed result, test or user evidence, limitations, decision owner, and disposition. Every card is an original prop labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Do not invent current labels, controls, validation, suitability, usability, project-level classification, accuracy, outcomes, adoption, or capabilities.

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Design |
|---|---|
| Subject | A visible before/after difference beside a blank intended-use acceptance card. |
| Desire | Know whether the observed difference is acceptable for the specific person, project, or human gate that needs it. |
| Obstacle | The effect is visible, but intended use, criteria, test evidence, user evidence, limitations, and decision owner remain unverified. |
| Stakes | A team may treat a visible improvement as fit for every use; the film must not claim a real incident or outcome. |
| Change | The acceptance record separates intended use, requirements, observed result, test/user evidence, limitations, owner, and disposition. |
| Contradiction | Something can change measurably without being acceptable for the use that matters. |
| Escalation | `EFFECT OBSERVED` → intended-use card appears → criteria requested → test/user evidence requested → limitations and decision owner exposed → disposition remains hold. |
| Final image | `ACCEPT A-001 / INTENDED_USE: FOLLOW CHART 3 / REQUIREMENTS_OR_CRITERIA: CLEAR SYMBOLS + EXPECTED RESULT / OBSERVED_RESULT: DIFFERENCE SEEN / TEST_OR_USER_EVIDENCE: PENDING / LIMITATIONS: UNKNOWN / DECISION_OWNER: UNCONFIRMED / DISPOSITION: HOLD`. |

## Treatment

The film opens with an original effect card labeled `EFFECT OBSERVED` beside two before/after cards showing a visible difference. A hand reaches to mark `ACCEPTED FOR USE`, then stops because no intended use, criteria, test or user evidence, limitations, or decision owner is named.

Cards are added for intended use, requirements or criteria, observed result, test or user evidence, limitations, decision owner, and disposition. The acceptance record remains deliberately incomplete: `INTENDED_USE: FOLLOW CHART 3`, `REQUIREMENTS_OR_CRITERIA: CLEAR SYMBOLS + EXPECTED RESULT`, `OBSERVED_RESULT: DIFFERENCE SEEN`, `TEST_OR_USER_EVIDENCE: PENDING`, `LIMITATIONS: UNKNOWN`, `DECISION_OWNER: UNCONFIRMED`, and `DISPOSITION: HOLD`. The film does not claim that any real output is suitable or that any gate passed.

## Visual grammar, coverage, and alternatives

Use overhead and hands-only side-angle coverage, neutral paper, high contrast, sentence-case labels, and dedicated readable inserts for every dense card. Capture a wide effect-and-acceptance master, macro inserts for intended use, criteria, observed result, test/user evidence, limitations, decision owner, and disposition, a clean hold-state card, a caption-free proof pass, and a final disclaimer frame. Never show a fabricated app screen. Captions may not cover hands, cards, labels, numbers, logos, controls, menus, or primary action.

Primary location is a quiet tabletop with neutral light and no private or branded material. Alternatives are a wall-mounted acceptance board, a no-hand version, or text-only original cards. Do not substitute a screenshot, customer record, private message, copyrighted pattern, logo, or current UI capture without approved proof and rights.
