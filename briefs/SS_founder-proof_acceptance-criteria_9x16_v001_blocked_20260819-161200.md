## Concept and distinctness

A status can look decisive while lacking the criterion that makes it interpretable. The nearest prior angle is imported `false-completion`, with imported `release-gate-roster` as its narrower successor. Those concepts concern a complete-looking checklist and a specific release roster. This angle changes the pain to an ownerless or unscoped result; the hook to “Pass is not a criterion”; the visual metaphor to a green status card that loses its meaning when its missing criterion and evidence fields are exposed; the proof surface to `CRITERION / REQUIREMENT / EVIDENCE REF / OBSERVED / SCOPE / EVALUATOR / DATE-VERSION / STATUS / NOTES`; the emotional turn from distrust of blanket completion to disciplined interpretation of a scoped result; and the final line to “Name the criterion before you name the status.” It does not recreate a roster or consume either imported reservation.
