## Concept and claim boundary

> “Assigned is not accountable.”

Two fictional action cards describe one craft finding. A role or owner field is present, but no answerable action, due point, acknowledgment, evidence of action, response, exception path, or disposition is attached. The card becomes bounded only when the accountability chain is explicit. The ending is a follow-through lesson, not legal liability, audit compliance, automatic assignment, authority, approval, or product capability claim. The closing line is **“Name who answers for the next action.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes accountable owners, assignments, due dates, acknowledgment states, evidence-of-action records, exception routing, accountability trails, or automatic responsibility. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence board, two action cards, a separated role/person label, and an accountability chain with arrows from finding to actor to action to due point to evidence to response. Amber marks missing answerability; red marks only `ACCOUNTABILITY INCOMPLETE`; there is no green completion state. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, product claim, or person identity. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers finding/decision, role/person, action, due point, evidence, acknowledgment, response, exception, disposition, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; two-card side-by-side; role-only insert; action/due close hold; evidence/acknowledgment hold; response/exception hold; disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static assigned-versus-accountable comparison with one explicit missing-link callout if motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unreadable proof, and unsupported claims rather than hiding defects with effects.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material.
