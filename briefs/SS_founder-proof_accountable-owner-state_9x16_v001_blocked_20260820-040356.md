## Research-informed distinction

Pass 210 made the conditions attached to a decision explicit. Pass 211 makes the accountable role explicit: identify who is authorized to accept or reject the decision, who supplied or checked the evidence, who must be consulted for a human gate, who maintains the conditions, and what happens when the named owner is unavailable or conditions change. NIST defines an authorizing official as the role with authority to formally assume responsibility. ICO accountability guidance requires demonstrable records and ongoing review, while the page carries a current under-review notice. W3C recommends diverse expertise and collaborative evaluation. Craft Yarn Council distributes responsibility across designer, editor, manufacturer, and recipient requirements. [1] [2] [3] [4]

> **A named decision is not an owned decision until someone can accept responsibility for its scope and next action.**

These sources inform general workflow education only. They do not establish that Stitch & Scale currently assigns accountable roles, routes decisions, escalates ownership, or maintains coverage when a person is unavailable.
