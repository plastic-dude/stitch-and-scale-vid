## Concept and distinctness

A vague note cannot be located, understood, reproduced, owned, or acted on. The nearest prior angle is `response-state`, which concerns the difference between a planned response and an enacted response. `actionability-state` changes the pain to the minimum handoff data needed for one finding to be located and acted upon. The hook becomes “Noted is not actionable.” The visual metaphor changes from plan-versus-action to a vague note expanding through scope, expected/actual, evidence, context, owner, and next action. The proof surface is `FINDING / SCOPE / EXPECTED / ACTUAL / EVIDENCE / CONTEXT / OWNER / NEXT ACTION`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from handoff ambiguity to next-action clarity. The final line is “Name what makes the finding actionable.” It does not recreate `response-state`, `reproducibility-state`, `closure-state`, `acknowledgement-state`, `assumption-register`, `provenance-chain`, or `applicability-scope`.
