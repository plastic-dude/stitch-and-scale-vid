## Concept and claim boundary

> “A rule is not universal.”

A fictional result card is copied into three fictional contexts. Each copy looks plausible until the missing subject, scope, size, unit, intended use, or exception is exposed. The final card limits the statement instead of claiming that a product automatically scopes rules or detects misuse. The closing line is **“Name where this applies.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes applicability rules, scope controls, warnings, exceptions, or automated review. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, numbers, warnings, and behavior require current approved product captures or a proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Treatment and beat sheet

The treatment is an abstract desk-top evidence lesson. A neutral grid holds a single result card. The card is duplicated into three labeled contexts: `SIZE A`, `SIZE B`, and `DIFFERENT INTENDED USE`. The copies remain fictional and do not display real measurements or product UI. A boundary line interrupts the duplication. The result is returned to one subject with explicit scope fields and a visible `REVIEW NEEDED` disposition.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral paper or grid background, one scope accent color, high-contrast typography, no identifiable people, no private records, no copyrighted pattern, no branded UI, and no real customer or product data. The proof card is always placed inside the Meta ad safe-zone guidance for 9:16 contexts, with critical content held away from edges; organic-platform treatment remains subject to platform review. Captions occupy a reserved central-lower lane and never cover the card, numbers, menus, buttons, logos, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked top-down master; medium card push-in; close readable proof holds for each field cluster; overhead duplication move; boundary-line insert; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static two-card layout if motion makes labels unreadable. Do not use effects to hide stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, or unreadable proof.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material. Arrangement is abstract cards, neutral paper/grid, and a single physical boundary line or vector line. Approved assets are limited to original abstract shapes, cleared type, cleared narrator, and original or cleared sound. No final asset is approved yet. The sound motif is one clean card placement per scope reveal, followed by a deliberate pause at `REVIEW NEEDED`; no alarm, urgency bed, or unlicensed music.
