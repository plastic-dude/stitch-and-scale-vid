## Concept and claim boundary

> “Permission is not audience control.”

Two fictional share cards describe one craft record. A consent badge says permission exists, but the audience, purpose, fields shown, fields withheld, channel, expiry, review, revocation/change, and disposition remain undefined. The card becomes bounded only when the disclosure boundary is explicit. The ending is a privacy and disclosure lesson, not a legal-compliance claim, secure-transmission claim, consent mechanism, redaction promise, or product capability claim. The closing line is **“Name who receives what.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes audience controls, purpose fields, field redaction, recipient selection, channel restrictions, expiry, disclosure review, revocation, or privacy controls. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence board, two fictional share cards for `RECORD R-001`, a consent badge separated from an audience-control frame, and arrows from purpose to audience to fields shown/withheld to channel to expiry to review. Amber marks undefined boundaries; red marks only `AUDIENCE CONTROL INCOMPLETE`; there is no green sharing or privacy-safe state. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, product claim, or recipient identity. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers record/purpose/audience/fields shown/fields withheld/channel/retention or expiry/review/revocation/disposition fields, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; two-card side-by-side; consent-only insert; purpose/audience close hold; fields shown/withheld hold; channel/expiry hold; review/revocation/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static consent-versus-audience-control comparison with one explicit leaked-field callout if motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unreadable proof, and unsupported claims rather than hiding defects with effects.
