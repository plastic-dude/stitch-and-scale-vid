## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or tester. He may explain why an internally complete output still needs audience context, intended use, access testing, rights, destination, open limitations, and disposition before it is presented externally. Calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies a current audience-readiness surface with intended user, intended use, required standard, access test, rights, destination, open limitation, and disposition. Every folder and card is an original prop labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Do not invent current labels, controls, user outcomes, public usability, accessibility, standards compliance, or capabilities.

## Treatment

The film opens with an original folder labeled `INTERNAL PACK / COMPLETE FOR TEAM` beside a public-facing sample card. A hand reaches to place the sample in a `PUBLIC` tray, then stops because the external context is not named. Cards are added for intended user, intended use, required standard, access test, rights, destination, open limitation, and disposition.

The final card reads `INTENDED_USER: FOUNDING TESTER`, `INTENDED_USE: REVIEW SAMPLE`, `ACCESS_TEST: PENDING`, `RIGHTS: PENDING`, `DESTINATION: UNSET`, `OPEN_LIMITATION: NOT YET TESTED`, and `DISPOSITION: HOLD`. The output is deliberately not called accessible, audience-fit, consumer-ready, or approved. All props are original and must not resemble current Stitch & Scale UI.
