## Purpose

This brief tests a narrow founder-proof idea: a current measurement, record, or derived result should not be described as a meaningful change until the reference state, reference date/version, measurement context, and allowed-change rule are named. The concept is a planning artifact only. It does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes baseline management, change control, comparison, grading, fit prediction, or any other named surface.

## Hook and close

> “Current is not baseline.”

The closing line is: **“Name the reference before you name the change.”**

## Audience problem

A maker or reviewer can see a current number or output and say that it changed without knowing what it is being compared against. A reference may be stale, informal, unreviewed, measured in another unit, tied to another size or ease choice, or intended for a different use. The video should create disciplined pause, not fear or a promise of automatic correctness.

## Visual plan

Use a fully fictional, abstract record board with two cards. The first card is `BASELINE` and contains a reviewed date, version, context, measure, unit, size, ease, and intended use. The second card is `CURRENT` and contains the same categories with different fictional values. A delta marker appears, followed by an unresolved `ALLOWED CHANGE?` gate. The card is stamped `REVIEW NEEDED`, not approved. Do not show a real application, real customer record, real garment, identifiable person, private data, copyrighted pattern, brand interface, or claimed result.

David must be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. Do not call him a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or user of an unverified feature.

## Proof and claim boundary

The only permitted factual language about the external research is that NIST describes a formally reviewed baseline as a reference for future changes, W3C describes provenance and derivation as records about entities and activities, and the Craft Yarn Council presents body and garment measurements and schematics as context for sizing guidance. None of those sources proves a Stitch & Scale product feature.

The package remains blocked until one current, versioned, non-private, rights-cleared product capture or proof-registry entry verifies the exact baseline/current labels and behavior, including date/version, context, measure/unit/size/ease/intended use, allowed or observed change, review, and disposition. Rights, privacy, captions, audio, platform, and human craft gates must also pass independently.
