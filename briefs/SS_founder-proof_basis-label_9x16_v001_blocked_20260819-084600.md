## Research decision

NIST’s traceability guidance says a measurement result should relate to a reference through a documented chain and that traceability alone does not guarantee fitness for purpose. Government Analysis Function guidance says methods, assumptions, limitations, and uncertainty should be transparent so data is not presented with unwarranted certainty. The craft-business and mobile-maker contracts distinguish recorded, estimated, planned, and derived values and require source notes, units, active context, and validation state. None of this proves Stitch & Scale exposes provenance or uncertainty labels.

## Concept and dramatic engine

| Stakes | A designer may treat a provisional or derived value as a measured fact; no actual product outcome is claimed. |

| Contradiction | A label can increase honesty without making the value fit for purpose. |
| Escalation | Naked number → basis question → four status labels → source note/uncertainty → human judgement. |
| Final image | A value card with `BASIS: ESTIMATED` and `USE: REVIEW`; no accuracy badge. |
