## Concept and distinctness

An owner name and due date can create false confidence while the actual work has no feasible window. The nearest prior angle is `priority-state`, which concerns ranking and urgency. `capacity-state` changes the pain to unverified feasibility of an assigned task. The hook becomes “Assigned is not available.” The visual metaphor changes from multiple review cards separating into impact and urgency axes to an assigned task passing through time-window and conflict checks before acceptance. The proof surface is `TASK / OWNER / REQUIRED TIME / AVAILABLE WINDOW / CONFLICT / DUE / ACCEPTED / FALLBACK`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from defensible triage to feasible commitment. The final line is “Name the capacity before you promise the date.” It does not recreate `priority-state`, `acknowledgement-state`, `review-expiry`, `response-state`, `commit-state`, `dependency-state`, imported `false-completion`, or imported `release-gate-roster`.
