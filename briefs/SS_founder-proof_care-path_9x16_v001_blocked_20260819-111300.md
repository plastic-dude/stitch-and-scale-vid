## Research decision

The FTC says covered garments and piece goods need care instructions or warnings with a reasonable basis, and that reliable evidence may need to address the finished garment as a whole when components differ.[192] Craft Yarn Council identifies care-symbol categories but does not prescribe a finished garment’s care method.[193] Stitch & Scale’s contracts require distinct human test knitting, technical editing, print review, and evidence-status labels.[194] [195] These sources support a care-path checklist, but do not prove Stitch & Scale provides care instructions or wash prediction.

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `prerequisite-state`, which focuses on what must be true before a workflow step. `care-path` changes the pain to **which source and evidence support a washing or blocking decision, and whether yarn-label guidance is being mistaken for finished-garment proof**. The hook changes to **“The yarn label is not the finished garment.”** The visual metaphor changes from readiness state to **`YARN LABEL / FINISHED PIECE / METHOD / WARNING / EVIDENCE`**. The proof surface changes from a prerequisite card to a current care/evidence surface with source, date, method, warning, and test status. The emotional turn changes from step readiness to pausing before irreversible care without a universal method. The final line changes to **“Name the evidence before you wash the work.”**

It is distinct from `finish-state` because it is care evidence rather than object state; from `fit-intent` because it is wash/block basis rather than fit goal; from `price-basis` because it is material evidence rather than economic category; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or evidence-roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.

## Treatment and production bible

A fictional care symbol appears, then is separated into `YARN LABEL`, `FINISHED PIECE`, `METHOD`, `WARNING`, and `EVIDENCE`. Add `SOURCE / DATE / TEST STATUS`. The final card reads `CARE BASIS VISIBLE — REVIEW OPEN`.

A real Stitch & Scale care/evidence surface may replace the fictional card only if a current, approved, non-private capture or proof-registry entry verifies the exact surface, labels, product version/date, and rights to show it. If proof is absent, retain the blocker card and disclaimer. No wash instruction, blocking recommendation, care-symbol interpretation, textile compliance, damage prediction, or safe-care outcome claim is permitted.

Use one narrator family. David may appear only with authorization and remains described as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Captions must not cover care symbols, warnings, evidence cards, tables, buttons, menus, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions. Generated text remains illustrative and cannot substitute for current product proof.
