## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or tester. He may explain why a material change needs an audience, changed artifact/version, impact, effective timing, owner/channel, required action, limitation, and disposition before it is treated as communicated. Calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies a current change-communication surface with affected audience, changed artifact/version, impact, effective time, owner/channel, required action, limitation, and disposition. Every card is an original prop labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Do not invent current labels, controls, public notices, notification routing, alert behavior, errata, change logs, audience delivery, accessibility, or outcomes.

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Design |
|---|---|
| Subject | An internal revision note beside a blank audience notice card. |
| Desire | Ensure the people affected by a craft-production change can understand its scope and know what to do next. |
| Obstacle | The internal note records a change, but affected audience, impact, timing, owner/channel, required action, limitation, and disposition are absent. |
| Stakes | The right people may not see or understand a material change; the film must not claim a real incident or outcome. |
| Change | The notice card is bounded with audience, changed version, impact, timing, owner/channel, action, limitation, and disposition. |
| Contradiction | A change can be documented without being communicated to the people who must act on it. |
| Escalation | `REVISION NOTE EXISTS` → blank notice appears → affected audience missing → impact/timing revealed → owner/channel/action added → limitation and disposition hold the release. |
| Final image | `NOTICE N-001 / AFFECTED_AUDIENCE: TESTERS USING V001 / CHANGED_VERSION: V002 / IMPACT: CHECK CHART 3 / EFFECTIVE_AT: PENDING / OWNER_OR_CHANNEL: PENDING / REQUIRED_ACTION: REVIEW / LIMITATION: DELIVERY UNVERIFIED / DISPOSITION: HOLD`. |

## Treatment

The film opens with an original internal revision note labeled `V002 / CHANGED ROW 18` pinned to a folder. A blank audience notice card sits beside it. A hand reaches to mark the revision as `COMMUNICATED`, then stops because no audience, impact, timing, owner/channel, required action, limitation, or disposition is named.

Cards are added one at a time. The notice becomes specific but remains held because delivery is unverified: `AFFECTED_AUDIENCE: TESTERS USING V001`, `CHANGED_VERSION: V002`, `IMPACT: CHECK CHART 3`, `EFFECTIVE_AT: PENDING`, `OWNER_OR_CHANNEL: PENDING`, `REQUIRED_ACTION: REVIEW`, `LIMITATION: DELIVERY UNVERIFIED`, and `DISPOSITION: HOLD`. The film does not claim that any notice was sent, seen, acted on, or delivered by Stitch & Scale.
