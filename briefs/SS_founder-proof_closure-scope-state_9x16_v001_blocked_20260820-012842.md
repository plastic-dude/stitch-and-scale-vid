## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Design |
|---|---|
| Subject | A single `RESOLVED` card placed over a row of adjacent craft-production tasks. |
| Desire | Know whether the resolution closes one item or the surrounding workflow as well. |
| Obstacle | The resolved item is visible, but scope, adjacent checks, remaining work, owner, next action, and disposition are absent. |
| Stakes | A team may mistake an issue-level resolution for global completion; the film must not claim a specific defect or outcome. |
| Change | The closure record names the resolved item, scope, checks made, remaining open work, owner, next action, and disposition. |
| Contradiction | A valid resolution can coexist with unresolved neighboring work. |
| Escalation | `RESOLVED` card → covers an entire row → uncovered adjacent items remain → scope and remaining work appear → closure claim narrows to what is actually checked. |
| Final image | `CLOSED_SCOPE: SAMPLE A / ADJACENT_CHECKED: NO / REMAINING_OPEN: TECH EDIT + PRINT REVIEW / NEXT: ASSIGN HUMAN GATES / DISPOSITION: PARTIAL`. |

## Treatment

The film opens on a row of original cards: `CALCULATION`, `TECHNICAL EDIT`, `PHYSICAL PRINT REVIEW`, `CHART READABILITY`, `SCHEMATIC SCALE`, and `TEST KNIT`. A single `RESOLVED` card is laid on top of the row. The narrator asks whether the whole row is now closed. A hand reaches for a `DONE` tray, then stops.

The card is rebuilt with a specific resolved item and declared scope. It states `CLOSED_SCOPE: SAMPLE A`, `ADJACENT_CHECKED: NO`, `REMAINING_OPEN: TECH EDIT + PRINT REVIEW`, `OWNER: HUMAN REVIEW`, `NEXT: ASSIGN HUMAN GATES`, and `DISPOSITION: PARTIAL`. The final image deliberately narrows the closure claim rather than displaying a global completion state. All cards are original props and must not resemble current Stitch & Scale UI.
