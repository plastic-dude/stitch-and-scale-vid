## Concept and distinctness

A closed label can hide unfinished follow-up. The nearest prior angle is `outcome-proof`, which concerns whether something completed actually improved the result. `closure-state` changes the pain to closure evidence and unresolved follow-up. The hook becomes “Closed is not resolved.” The visual metaphor changes from a done/improved comparison to one review item passing through outcome, evidence, follow-up, verification, improvement, and closure. The proof surface is `ITEM / OUTCOME / EVIDENCE / OWNER / FOLLOW-UP / VERIFIED / IMPROVEMENT / CLOSED AT`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from false improvement confidence to accountable closure. The final line is “Name what closed before you call it resolved.” It does not recreate `outcome-proof`, `acknowledgement-state`, `response-state`, `review-expiry`, `retention-state`, imported `false-completion`, or imported `release-gate-roster`.
