## Research decision

NIST defines repeatability in terms of agreement under shared measurement conditions and distinguishes accuracy from precision; this supports preserving procedure, observer/instrument, location/time, and uncertainty context before interpreting a comparison.[426] Modern Daily Knitting discusses how swatch size and flat-versus-round method can affect apparent gauge comparison.[427] Internal contracts preserve comparison basis, source/date, measurement context, and separate human evidence gates.[432] Current platform and caption guidance supports a short vertical master with human-reviewed captions.[428] [429] [430] [431] None of this proves current Stitch & Scale comparison behavior.

## Concept and dramatic engine

| Engine element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | Two records that appear comparable but require an explicit shared basis. |
| Desire | To understand a difference without making a false equivalence. |
| Obstacle | Item, field, unit, date, method, conditions, scope, or benchmark basis may differ silently. |
| Stakes | A designer may interpret an invalid comparison as a meaningful result; no real defect or outcome is claimed. |
| Change | The fictional record adds A/B identity, field, values, unit, date/period, method, conditions, scope, basis, result, and status. |
| Contradiction | A visible difference is not automatically an interpretable difference. |
| Escalation | A/B → field → unit → date/period → method → conditions → scope → basis → result/status. |
| Final image | `COMPARISON BASIS VISIBLE — RESULT NOT OVERSTATED`. |

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `comparison-frame`, which focuses on framing a comparison generally. `comparison-basis` changes the pain to **two records looking comparable while item, unit, method, conditions, period, scope, or benchmark basis quietly differ**. The hook changes to **“Compared is not comparable.”** The visual metaphor changes from a general A/B frame to two cards that first reveal identity/units/conditions/basis, then permit a restrained difference card marked `STATUS: UNVERIFIED`. The proof surface changes from comparison framing to comparability prerequisites. The emotional turn changes from seeing a contrast to refusing a false equivalence. The final line changes to **“Name the basis before you compare the numbers.”**

It is distinct from `basis-label` because it requires two-sided alignment; from `conflict-state` because it is not disagreement; from `uncertainty-context` because it is not dispersion around one value; from `conversion-trace` because it is not raw/transformed units; from `locale-number-format` because it is not display; and from `gauge-reality` because it is not gauge evidence. It does not reopen or reuse `false-completion` or `release-gate-roster`.
