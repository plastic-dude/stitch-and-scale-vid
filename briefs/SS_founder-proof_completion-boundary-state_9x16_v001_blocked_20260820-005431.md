## Treatment

The film opens on a tabletop handoff card with a large `COMPLETED` label. A second card shows `NEXT ROLE: REVIEW`. The narrator asks what the next person can actually verify. The camera reveals that the card has no owner, no evidence-required field, no evidence-present field, and no blocker. A hand tries to pass it across the table, then stops.

The card is rebuilt with a restrained state vocabulary: `NOT STARTED`, `IN PROGRESS`, `WAITING`, `BLOCKED`, and `COMPLETED`. `COMPLETED` is permitted only when the evidence slots and human owner are named. The final card keeps `COMPLETED` absent and instead reads `INCOMPLETE / OWNER: HUMAN REVIEW / NEEDS: CHART READABILITY + TEST KNIT / NEXT: OPEN EVIDENCE`. These are illustrative paper props and must not resemble current Stitch & Scale UI.
