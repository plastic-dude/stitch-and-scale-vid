## Concept, distinctness, and research boundary

A green state can feel confident without explaining what is known or unknown. The nearest prior angles are `uncertainty-context` and `tolerance-state`, which concern uncertainty context and in-range status. `confidence-state` asks whether the evidentiary basis and limits of confidence are explicit. Hook: “Confident is not quantified.” Fictional proof surface: `RESULT / EVIDENCE / UNCERTAINTY / RANGE / LIMITS / REVIEW / BASIS / CONFIDENCE / DECISION`. Emotional turn: “It looks right” becomes “I can say how much I know.” Final line: “Name what your confidence is based on.” Distinct from `fitness-state`, `attestation-state`, `audit-state`, `sensitivity-state`, `calibration-state`, and `scenario-state`.

NIST defines measurement uncertainty as a parameter associated with a result that characterizes dispersion and incomplete knowledge. Craft Yarn Council explains that gauge depends on pattern-specified yarn/tools, swatch conditions, and tension, and may require changes before proceeding. These sources support bounded education only; they do not prove that Stitch & Scale currently exposes confidence, uncertainty, ranges, or confidence bases.
