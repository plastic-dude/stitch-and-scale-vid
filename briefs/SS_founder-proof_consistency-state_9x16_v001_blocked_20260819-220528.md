## Concept, distinctness, and research boundary

Each field can look plausible while the record disagrees with itself. The nearest prior angle is `reconciliation-state`, which compares two populated records. `consistency-state` asks whether one record and its derived views agree across source/derived fields, units, sizes, views, expected relations, discrepancies, review, and decision. Hook: “Aligned is not consistent.” Fictional proof surface: `SOURCE FIELD / DERIVED FIELD / UNIT / SIZE / VIEW / EXPECTED RELATION / DISCREPANCY / REVIEWED / DECISION`. Emotional turn: “Each number looks fine” becomes “The record does not agree with itself.” Final line: “Name what the record must agree with.” Distinct from `dependency-state`, `schema-state`, `sequence-state`, `confidence-state`, `tolerance-state`, and `context-state`.

W3C Data on the Web Best Practices treats data quality, metadata, provenance, versioning, identifiers, formats, and preservation as distinct areas and says origin/history provide interpretive context for judging quality. The Craft Yarn Council official result describes schematics as representing size and shape and indicating relevant measurements; the target page did not return readable content in this browser session, so no exact labels were inferred. These sources support bounded data-quality and craft-context education only; they do not prove that Stitch & Scale currently checks internal consistency.
