## Concept and claim boundary

> “Visible is not contained.”

One fictional suspect-output card is labeled `VISIBLE` and begins producing copies. The copies stop only when the card expands with a reason for hold, evidence snapshot, propagation block, review queue, owner, review window, release condition, and `RELEASED OR RETAINED`. The ending is a hold boundary, not a product promise, quarantine claim, or automatic approval. The closing line is **“Hold it until the evidence can travel.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes hold, freeze, quarantine, propagation-block, release-condition, retained/released, or evidence-preservation controls. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Treatment and beat sheet

The treatment is an abstract evidence-preservation lesson. A neutral evidence board holds one fictional output card. It duplicates into three lanes labeled `COPY A`, `COPY B`, and `EXPORT PREVIEW`; no lane is approved. A thin boundary interrupts the copies. The card opens to show the reason, evidence snapshot, review queue, owner, review window, release condition, and `RELEASED OR RETAINED` disposition. The ending keeps the suspect card retained with evidence attached.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence-board background, one restrained amber hold marker, a thin boundary line, and red only for unresolved risk. No alarms, countdowns, urgency, fear, real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, or unsupported product label. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers the held proof card, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; medium suspect-card push-in; close readable proof holds for reason/evidence and release fields; lateral copy propagation; boundary-line insert; retained-state hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static three-column copy comparison if motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, and unreadable proof rather than hiding defects with effects.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material. Arrangement is original abstract cards, neutral lanes, a single boundary line, cleared type, a cleared narrator, and original or cleared sound. No final asset is approved. Sound motif: soft card movement for each copy, silence at `CAN THIS TRAVEL?`, then one quiet boundary placement when the hold fields appear. No alarm bed, urgency cue, or unlicensed music.
