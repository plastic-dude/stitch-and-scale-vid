## Concept, distinctness, and research boundary

A number can travel while its meaning stays behind. The nearest prior angle is `identity-state`, which asks which record or entity a reopened, copied, revised, or exported item became. `context-state` asks what a value belongs to when reused: body location, garment piece, size, fit/ease purpose, method, date, and source. Hook: “Attached is not contextualized.” Fictional proof surface: `VALUE / SUBJECT / LOCATION / PURPOSE / METHOD / SIZE / EASE / DATE / SOURCE / REVIEWED`. Emotional turn: “I have the number” becomes “I know what this number belongs to.” Final line: “Keep the context attached.” Distinct from `calibration-state`, `schema-state`, `applicability-scope`, `comparison-basis`, `unit-state`, `provenance-chain`, and `selection-state`.

W3C recommends provenance and metadata that expose origins, changes, temporal/spatial context, and agent responsibility. Craft Yarn Council distinguishes actual body measurements from completed-garment measurements, ties them to sizing charts and schematics, explains ease, and specifies measurement location and method. These sources support workflow education only; they do not prove that Stitch & Scale currently exposes contextual attachment, sizing/ease semantics, or reviewed measurement context.
