## Concept and claim boundary

> “Recorded is not custody.”

One fictional evidence card has a stable evidence ID. A source/provenance strip shows how it was produced, but the card has no handling history. It moves through collection, safeguarding, transfer, and analysis lanes; each custody event adds handler, date/time, purpose, from/to, safeguard, condition, transfer acceptance, next handler, and disposition. The ending is an inspectable fictional handoff, not a legal claim, immutable-record claim, or product promise. The closing line is **“Name who held it next.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes a custody ledger, immutable history, chain-of-custody controls, transfer acceptance, handler tracking, legal sufficiency, or audit functionality. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Treatment and beat sheet

The treatment is an abstract evidence-handoff lesson. A neutral board holds one evidence card labeled `EVIDENCE ID: E-001`. A small source strip shows `PRODUCED FROM`, but no handler history. The card moves through four lanes: `COLLECTED`, `SAFEGUARDED`, `TRANSFERRED`, and `ANALYZED`. Each lane adds a dated fictional custody strip. The final card shows a pending disposition and the next handler field.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence-board background, one stable evidence ID, dated transfer strips, a restrained blue custody line, amber for an unaccepted handoff, and red only for an unresolved condition. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, or product claim. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers evidence ID, handler, date/time, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; medium evidence-card push-in; close readable proof holds for handler/date-time/purpose and from/to/condition fields; lateral lane movement; transfer-acceptance insert; final custody-trail hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static four-step lane comparison if motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, and unreadable proof rather than hiding defects with effects.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material. Arrangement is original abstract cards, four neutral lanes, cleared type, a cleared narrator, and original or cleared sound. No final asset is approved. Sound motif: one soft placement sound per custody event, a quiet pause at `TRANSFER ACCEPTED`, and no alarm, urgency cue, or unlicensed music.
