## Research decision

The ICO’s current data-protection-principles guidance lists purpose limitation and data minimisation among the UK GDPR principles and quotes the requirement that personal data be adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the purpose. NIST describes its Privacy Framework as a voluntary tool for identifying and managing privacy risk, not a certification or legal-compliance result. These are general guidance sources, not legal advice, a universal jurisdictional rule, or proof of Stitch & Scale privacy controls.

The research changed the concept from generic privacy reassurance into **purpose-limited record design**. The film must not claim privacy compliance, encryption, deletion, anonymity, consent management, retention enforcement, or data safety.

## Campaign assumption

No active human brief was present in the checked repository state. This run assumes founding-tester recruitment remains the working objective through the free demo and early-access cohort. The film invites designers to inspect one verified privacy, record, redaction, consent, or context surface; it does not promise a privacy system or safe-data outcome.

## Dramatic engine

| Element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | A useful workflow record grows beyond what the task needs. |
| Desire | To keep the record useful without making the person the record. |
| Obstacle | Names, contact details, body measurements, addresses, and identity fields can appear by habit rather than necessity. |
| Stakes | Unnecessary person-level data can add exposure and distract from the pattern or sample task. |
| Change | The viewer moves from “capture everything” to “ask purpose, necessity, and retention before adding a field.” |
| Contradiction | A local record can preserve context without becoming a complete profile; this film does not claim the product does that. |
| Escalation | Fictional record fills with person-level fields → fields fade → `PURPOSE / NEEDED / KEEP?` remains → current proof or blocker. |
| Final image | A small task card beside `PURPOSE / NEEDED / KEEP?`, with no personal values visible. |

## Difference from prior concepts

The nearest prior angle is `measurement-moment`, which concerns preserving the meaning of a measurement through unit, size, body/garment context, and project identity. `data-minimum` changes the pain to **a useful workflow record expanding into unnecessary person-level data**; the hook to **“The task may need a note. It may not need your whole identity.”**; the visual metaphor from measurement context to a fictional record shedding fields until `PURPOSE / NEEDED / KEEP?`; the proof surface from measurement/grading entry to a privacy, record, redaction, consent, or context surface; the emotional turn from maker control through context to restraint before collection; and the final line to **“Record the task. Not the person.”**

It is distinct from `permission-line` because it is necessity and scope before collection, not a sharing permission; from `recovery-proof` because it is not backup or restore; from `decision-ledger` because it is not recorded-versus-estimated values; from `measurement-moment` because it does not interpret a measurement; from `support-boundary`, `brief-before-knit`, and `feedback-loop` because it is not question routing or tester participation; and from `care-context`, `material-notes`, `sample-chain`, `technique-profile`, `equivalent-meaning`, `readability-under-load`, `false-completion`, `release-gate-roster`, `event-window`, `gauge-reality`, `reader-handoff`, `chart-parity`, `evidence-over-output`, `inherited-thread`, `offline-continuity`, `correction-trace`, and `locale-drift` because the conflict is data scope versus task necessity. It does not reopen, overwrite, rename, or consume the imported reservations.

## Treatment, visual grammar, and production bible

Open on an original record card labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. It contains only fictional placeholders such as `PROJECT NOTE`, `SAMPLE ID`, `CONTACT?`, `BODY DATA?`, and `ADDRESS?`; no real personal data. The card grows visually until a calm boundary removes unnecessary fields. Three cards remain: `PURPOSE`, `NEEDED`, and `KEEP?`. The final card is a question, not a privacy badge, deletion control, or compliance result.

The product insert is optional and blocked until a current, versioned, non-private proof capture verifies exact privacy, record, redaction, consent, data-purpose, retention, or context labels. If absent, retain `DATA-SCOPE PROOF REQUIRED — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Any real body measurement, contact detail, tester identity, address, consent record, or private project requires explicit permission and appropriate review. David may appear only with authorization and must be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Use one narrator family. Captions must not cover record fields, proof, hands, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free cuts.
