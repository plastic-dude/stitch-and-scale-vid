## Research-informed distinction

Pass 209 recorded the reasoning that connects applicable evidence to a bounded disposition. Pass 210 makes the conditions that bound that disposition explicit: identify the entity, purpose, environment, version, sample, human gate, prerequisites, limitations, and review trigger that must remain true for the decision to be reused. NIST authorization terminology distinguishes specific permission and documented risk decisions; its authorization-package glossary describes assessment results and supporting documentation used in risk-based decisions. W3C WCAG-EM 2 bounds scope, baseline, sample, version, and re-evaluation. Craft Yarn Council keeps specified yarn, needles, stitch, instructions, and gauge tied to the result. [1] [2] [3] [4]

> **A reasoned decision can still stop applying when its conditions change.**

These sources inform general workflow education only. They do not establish that Stitch & Scale currently records conditional decisions, enforces prerequisites, expires dispositions, or automatically triggers reassessment.

## Treatment and beats

The film opens on a neutral tabletop. A reasoned decision enters a lane bounded by purpose, material, version, scope, sample, and human-gate conditions. One condition card changes, and the lane turns amber. The decision is not declared universally wrong; it is held for recheck.

| Time | Beat | Image | Spoken idea | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | Decision lane appears | Reasoned decision inside a narrow condition frame | “A reasoned decision can still stop applying when its conditions change.” | Original props; general framing |
| 4–10s | Entity and purpose | `AUTHORIZED_ENTITY` / `AUTHORIZED_PURPOSE` cards enter | “Name who it is for and what it is for.” | Fictional prop |
| 10–17s | Prerequisites and conditions | `PREREQUISITES` / `CONDITIONS` cards attach to lane | “Keep the prerequisites and conditions attached.” | Fictional prop; no product-control claim |
| 17–25s | Limitations and scope | `LIMITATIONS` / `SCOPE` / `SAMPLE_OR_COVERAGE` cards narrow lane | “Show where it was checked—and where it was not.” | General educational framing |
| 25–33s | Version and human gate | `VERSION_OR_DATE` / `HUMAN_GATE_CONDITION` cards enter | “Name the version and the human check it still needs.” | Human evidence boundary |
| 33–40s | Condition changes | Material or version card swaps; lane turns amber | “When a condition changes, do not silently carry the decision forward.” | No product or outcome claim |
| 40–45s | Safe turn | `RECHECK` and hold disposition fill frame | “Name the conditions, show what changed, and recheck before the decision travels.” | Blocked pending product proof |
