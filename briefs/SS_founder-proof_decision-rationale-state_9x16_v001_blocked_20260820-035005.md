## Research-informed distinction

Pass 208 tested whether qualified evidence applies to the exact claim, user, environment, scope, sample, version, and assumptions. Pass 209 adds a rationale record: identify the applicable evidence, explain the inference or reasoning that links it to the decision, state the alternatives or uncertainty considered, name the decision owner, and keep the disposition bounded. UK Civil Service guidance describes how exposing evidence and data lets others check reasoning and consider alternative interpretations. NIST RMF provides a repeatable, documented risk process, but does not establish Stitch & Scale behavior. W3C reporting separates scope, reviewers, methods, dates, results, recommendations, and follow-up. Craft Yarn Council keeps instructions, gauge, and human activities distinct. [1] [2] [3] [4]

> **Evidence can apply to a decision and the decision can still need its reasoning recorded.**

These sources inform general workflow education only. They do not establish that Stitch & Scale currently records rationale, supports audit, makes decisions explainable, or provides current decision controls.

## Treatment and beats

The film opens on a neutral tabletop. An applicable evidence card reaches a bridge marked `REASONING`. One segment is missing, so the decision card stays on the far side. The film does not show a jump from evidence directly to approval.

Inference, alternatives, uncertainty, owner, scope, conditions, review trigger, and follow-up cards enter one at a time. The bridge remains incomplete where the basis is not recorded; the disposition stays `HOLD` until accountable human review exists.
