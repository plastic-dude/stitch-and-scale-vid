## Concept and distinctness

A changed decision can look clean while losing the prior state, affected scope, rationale, or way back. The nearest prior angle is `supersession-state`, with `correction-trace`, `artifact-status`, `change-notice`, and `decision-ledger` as adjacent occupied concepts. `decision-reversal` changes the pain to a change that cannot be safely understood or reversed because its prior state, scope, rationale, reversibility, or rollback method is missing. The hook becomes “A reversal needs a record.” The visual metaphor changes from old-versus-new preservation to a new decision linked back to an earlier state with a visible path back. The proof surface is `PRIOR STATE / NEW STATE / AFFECTED SCOPE / RATIONALE / REVERSIBLE? / ROLLBACK METHOD / OWNER / DATE-VERSION / STATUS / NOTES`. The emotional turn is from preserving history to restoring agency without erasure. The final line is “Record the way back before you change the way forward.” It does not recreate a correction archive, release roster, or acceptance-status card.
