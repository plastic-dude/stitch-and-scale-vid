## Dramatic engine before camera

**Subject:** A fictional `DEPENDENCY CHECK` card naming one pattern support resource.  
**Desire:** Let a designer or reviewer know what a linked or attached resource is for before relying on it.  
**Obstacle:** A file, chart, video, schematic, or alternate version can exist without clear purpose, scope, version, source, access, or relationship to the pattern.  
**Stakes:** A maker may follow the wrong resource or miss a required support file; the video must not claim this happened to a Stitch & Scale user.  
**Change:** Move from an attachment-looking-complete to an explicit dependency record with a human review boundary.  
**Contradiction:** The resource can be present and still be unusable if its relationship is unnamed.  
**Escalation:** Resource appears; purpose appears; source and scope appear; version and last check appear; access and alternate-version relation appear; human dependency review remains unresolved.  
**Final image:** `PATTERN VERSION / RESOURCE / PURPOSE / SOURCE / SCOPE / LAST CHECK / LINK LABEL / ACCESS / ALTERNATE VERSION / HUMAN DEPENDENCY REVIEW / STATUS` with `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

## Treatment

Open on a clean desk with a paper card titled `DEPENDENCY CHECK`. A single fictional resource card slides beside it: `RESOURCE: CHART KEY`, `PURPOSE: READ SYMBOLS`, `SOURCE: PATTERN SUPPORT`, `SCOPE: CABLE SECTION`, `VERSION: V2`, `LAST CHECK: UNRESOLVED`, `ACCESS: UNCONFIRMED`, and `STATUS: HUMAN REVIEW`. The narrator says, “A file can be attached and still not tell you what it is for.” The card adds `ATTACHED ≠ EXPLAINED`, then `LINK LABEL: CHART KEY`, `ALTERNATE VERSION: NONE SHOWN`. The narrator explains that a support resource needs purpose, scope, version, and access context before a maker can rely on it. The founder turns the paper away and says he will not pretend it is the app. The final card invites founding testers to identify what the current product can actually preserve about pattern dependencies.
