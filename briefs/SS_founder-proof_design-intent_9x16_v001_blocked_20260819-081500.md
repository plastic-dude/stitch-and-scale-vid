## Research decision

Natalie in Stitches describes knitwear development as the stage where ideas move into reality, with testing and swatching as opportunities for innovation, and connects pattern development with writing pattern “code” and debugging. A second GoSadi source was identified but returned no extractable text or screenshot in this run, so no detailed claims from its framework were used. Local craft-business records guidance keeps rationale distinct from technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting.

The research changed the concept from a generic design diary into **rationale alongside evidence**. The film must not claim intent capture, design correctness, fit, or automated reasoning.

## Campaign assumption

No active human brief was present in the checked repository state. This run assumes founding-tester recruitment remains the working objective through the free demo and early-access cohort. The film invites designers to inspect one verified design-note, rationale, or decision-context surface; it does not promise that the product stores intent or proves the decision.

## Dramatic engine

| Element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | A technical change remains visible while the reason for it disappears. |
| Desire | To preserve why a construction or proportion choice was made. |
| Obstacle | Numbers, revisions, and tests can record what changed without preserving the design question or intention. |
| Stakes | A later collaborator, editor, or maker may interpret a choice without its original rationale. |
| Change | The viewer moves from “record the result” to “record the rationale beside the test and human gate.” |
| Contradiction | A reason can guide a later reader while still not proving the technical decision is correct. |
| Escalation | Technical card changes → `WHY?` appears → `INTENT / ASSUMPTION / TEST / GATE` split → no reasoning badge → current proof or blocker. |
| Final image | A fictional design note beside `INTENT / ASSUMPTION / TEST / GATE`. |

## Difference from prior concepts

The nearest prior angle is `decision-ledger`, which distinguishes recorded costs, derived rollups, estimates, and planned values. `design-intent` changes the pain to **a technical record preserving what changed while losing why the designer chose the construction**; the hook to **“The number tells you what changed. The note can tell you why.”**; the visual metaphor from value-status cards to `INTENT / ASSUMPTION / TEST / GATE`; the proof surface from design-ledger classifications to a design-note, rationale, or decision-context surface; the emotional turn from numeric honesty to preserving creative reasoning beside evidence; and the final line to **“Keep the why beside the proof.”**

It is distinct from `source-note` because it is rationale, not source class; from `as-of` because it is why, not when; from `correction-trace` because it is intent, not change history; from `feedback-loop` because it is design reasoning, not response state; from `material-notes` because it is the reason for a choice, not the material assumption; and from `false-completion`, `release-gate-roster`, `notation-key`, `readability-under-load`, `chart-parity`, `equivalent-meaning`, `size-boundary`, `measurement-moment`, `gauge-reality`, `technique-profile`, `care-context`, `sample-chain`, `support-boundary`, `brief-before-knit`, `permission-line`, `recovery-proof`, `data-minimum`, `event-window`, `evidence-over-output`, `inherited-thread`, `offline-continuity`, `locale-drift`, and `reader-handoff` because the conflict is rationale versus technical proof. It does not reopen, overwrite, rename, or consume the imported reservations.

## Treatment, visual grammar, and production bible

Open on an original fictional design card labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`: `SLEEVE EASE CHANGED`. A second line reads `WHY?`. Four cards appear: `INTENT`, `ASSUMPTION`, `TEST`, and `GATE`. The note is placed beside a swatch and a human-gate card, never above them as a correctness claim. A glowing “reason verified” badge is rejected.

The product insert is optional and blocked until a current, versioned, non-private proof capture verifies exact design-note, rationale, intent, assumption, decision-context, or linked-evidence labels. If absent, retain `INTENT PROOF REQUIRED — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Any real pattern page, design notebook, swatch, tester data, or founder voice/image requires source and rights review. David may appear only with authorization and must be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Use one narrator family. Captions must not cover design notes, swatches, proof, hands, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free cuts.
