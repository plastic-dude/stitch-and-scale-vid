## Research decision

Craft Yarn Council explains row/round concepts and notes that symbol variants require consulting the pattern key. GOV.UK’s current step-by-step pattern supports logical order, essential context before action, and explicit step relationships. These sources support visible direction and side cues, but they do not prove Stitch & Scale provides row arrows, highlighted rows, right-side/wrong-side detection, round-direction controls, automatic traversal, or direction-aware chart navigation.

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `sequence-map`, which focuses on where a maker moves through pattern sections and related documents. `direction-cue` changes the pain to **which way this row or round should be read and what side of the work the symbol represents**. The hook changes to **“The chart did not change. Your reading direction did.”** The visual metaphor changes from a route/sequence map to **`ROW / SIDE / READ / TURN / KEY`**. The proof surface changes from navigation/sequence mapping to a row arrow, right-side/wrong-side indicator, round-direction cue, highlighted current row, chart-key direction note, or direction-aware chart control. The emotional turn changes from knowing where to go next to orientation before action rather than automatic row tracking. The final line changes to **“Show the direction before the symbol.”**

It is distinct from `chart-parity` because it is traversal orientation rather than chart/text agreement; from `notation-key` because it is which way and from which side to read rather than what a symbol means; from `contrast-proof` because it is direction rather than perceivability; from `sequence-map` because it is row/round reading rather than document route; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.

## Treatment and production bible

New cards enter: `ROW`, `SIDE`, `READ`, `TURN`, and `KEY`. The final card reads `DIRECTION NAMED — SYMBOL NEXT`.

A real Stitch & Scale chart, arrow, highlighted-row state, or direction control may replace the fictional card only if a current, approved, non-private capture or proof-registry entry verifies the exact surface, labels, product version/date, and rights to show it. If proof is absent, retain the blocker card and disclaimer. No real chart, pattern page, screenshot, or customer project may appear without scoped permission. Do not imply automatic row tracking, direction detection, or traversal control.

Use one narrator family. David may appear only with authorization and remains described as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor.
