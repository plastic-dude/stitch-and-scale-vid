## Research-informed premise

Craft Yarn Council guidance asks designers to reconfirm yarn and color, check all ball bands for the same dye-lot number, keep notes, and preserve yarn labels as part of a submission package. [1] Its yarn-label guidance explains that labels may contain yarn type, yardage, fiber content, care information, recommended tools, color details, and dye-lot numbers, and that different batches can show subtle color differences. [2] Lion Brand describes a dye lot as the batch in which yarn was dyed and warns that different batches may vary under different light; it also notes that exact lots may not remain available. [3] Hobbii recommends checking labels when yarn arrives and saving brand, color, lot, and gauge in a project journal, while cautioning that same-color skeins may come from different lots. [4]

These sources support a bounded record-design distinction: **a generic yarn name or same-color label is not proof of the lot actually used for a particular design or sample**. They do not prove Stitch & Scale records lot identity, predicts color continuity, matches inventory, validates fiber behavior, or guarantees a visual outcome.

Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 submission guidance provides seasonal publication context for Winter 2026 and asks for a complete, separately organized submission package, but it must not be used as urgency, affiliation, or a customer outcome claim. [5]

## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester. If he appears, he speaks as a developer explaining why a material record should not rely on memory; he does not claim craft-practitioner authority.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies that Stitch & Scale exposes a lot-level material surface. Every proposed card is a fictional prop and must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` in the production bible and on any visible frame.

Do not claim that Stitch & Scale records dye lots, attaches labels to designs, reconciles skeins, predicts color continuity, matches inventory, validates fiber behavior, confirms gauge, determines care suitability, or guarantees a sample or finished-object outcome. A recorded lot does not prove color uniformity, fiber performance, gauge, fit, care suitability, or publication acceptance.

## Treatment and beats

Open on a neutral tabletop with an original card that reads `COLOR: DUSTY ROSE`. A second layer slides out: `LOT: UNKNOWN`. The narrator names the label, project link, source, quantity, and observation fields without presenting them as product UI. The end state remains `MATERIAL LOT: UNVERIFIED`; the story is a prompt to preserve identity, not a prediction of color behavior.

## Visual grammar and production bible

Use one quiet neutral tabletop, diffuse side light, restrained camera movement, and high-contrast original cards. The color name must be generic and fictional. Do not use a real yarn label, manufacturer logo, purchase receipt, customer material, barcode, pattern screenshot, supplier image, or current app chrome. Every card must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

Keep `COLOR NAME`, `LOT`, `LABEL`, `PROJECT LINK`, `QUANTITY`, `SOURCE`, and `OBSERVATION` as separate field families. Do not show a dense full-screen table. Do not use a green “matched” badge, color-correctness stamp, supply guarantee, inventory-complete chime, or visual effect that implies the lot was automatically verified. Generated text is not UI truth.

The production bible locks the same card orientation, typography, tabletop, hand direction, narrator family, room tone, and light temperature across takes. David may appear in a neutral developer frame or remain voice-only; he must not perform knitting or be framed as a craft practitioner. Technical editing, gauge review, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

## Sound motif and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family with a close, dry voice and consistent room tone. The optional sound motif is a paper-label peel on `LABEL_REFERENCE`, a soft card tap on `LOT: UNKNOWN`, and held silence before `MATERIAL LOT: UNVERIFIED`. No color-match chime, inventory-complete stamp, supply guarantee, or outcome-confirmation bed.

Record clean room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, accent shifts, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, stale UI sounds, and effects that conceal **color**, **lot**, **source**, **project**, **quantity**, or **status**. Deliver caption-on and caption-free review versions. Automatic captions are draft-only; a human must verify every material name, color, lot, quantity, unit, and status word.

## Acceptance gates and blocker

The package cannot move beyond **blocked** until the product owner supplies a current, versioned, non-private, rights-cleared proof-registry entry or approved capture for the exact material-lot identity surface. It must identify `MATERIAL M-001`, design ID, sample ID, material name, brand/source, fiber/content, color name/code, lot/dye lot, label reference, quantity/unit, acquisition time, used-from relationship, substitution flag, observed difference, checked-at time, source/basis, reviewer, review time, and status. A recorded lot remains separate from gauge, care, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting.

Do not use a real label, supplier image, purchase record, pattern, customer material, barcode, screenshot, or private record without scoped rights/privacy clearance. Review caption-on and caption-free renders; reject captions that cover faces, hands, material cards, labels, numbers, proof, controls, logos, or primary actions. Reject stale UI, unsupported claims, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, duplicate filenames, and any prop text that could be mistaken for current product UI.

**Exact Generator action:** Do not generate or upload. Await a current proof-registry entry or approved capture, rights/privacy clearance, confirmed destination platform, and Director re-authorization after all gates pass.
