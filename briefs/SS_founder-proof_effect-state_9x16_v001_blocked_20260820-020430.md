## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or tester. He may explain why a follow-through action needs an observed before/after difference and a verification method before anyone treats the downstream effect as established. Calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies a current effect surface with request ID, target artifact or gate, before state, after state, observation, verification method, residual limitation, and disposition. ## Treatment

The film opens with an original request card labeled `R-001 / REVIEW CHART 3` carrying an `ACTION COMPLETED` stamp. Two artifact cards sit beside it: `BEFORE: V001` and `AFTER: ?`. A hand reaches to mark `EFFECT VERIFIED`, then stops because no observed difference or verification method is named.

Cards are added for target artifact or gate, before state, after state, observation, verification method, residual limitation, and disposition. The effect record remains deliberately incomplete: `TARGET_ARTIFACT_OR_GATE: CHART 3`, `BEFORE_STATE: V001`, `AFTER_STATE: V001?`, `OBSERVATION: NO VERIFIED DIFFERENCE`, `VERIFICATION_METHOD: PENDING`, `RESIDUAL_LIMITATION: UNKNOWN`, and `DISPOSITION: HOLD`. The film does not claim that any real artifact changed or that any gate passed.
