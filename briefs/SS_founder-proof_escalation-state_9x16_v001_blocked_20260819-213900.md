## Concept and claim boundary

> “Noticed is not escalated.”

One fictional finding card says only `FOUND` and `OPEN`. It drifts through three unlabeled queues until the route, accountable owner, next action, due point, acknowledgment, response, and disposition are made explicit. The ending is a responsible handoff, not a product promise, completion stamp, or automated escalation claim. The closing line is **“Name who carries it next.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes issue escalation, severity, routing, ownership, due dates, acknowledgment, response tracking, or disposition controls. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Treatment and beat sheet

The treatment is an abstract evidence-handoff lesson. A neutral review board holds a fictional finding card. The card moves through three empty lanes labeled only `QUEUE 1`, `QUEUE 2`, and `QUEUE 3`; nothing changes because no route or owner is named. The card then opens to show evidence and severity, an explicit route, owner, next action, due point, acknowledgment, response, and `DISPOSITION: OPEN`. The visual ends on a bounded human handoff.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral review-board background, one restrained amber severity marker, thin labeled route arrows, and red only for unresolved risk. No alarms, countdowns, urgency, fear, real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, or unsupported product label. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers the finding card, numbers, tables, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; medium finding-card push-in; close readable proof holds for evidence and route fields; lateral queue drift; route-arrow insert; acknowledgment hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static three-column queue comparison if motion reduces field legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, and unreadable proof rather than hiding defects with effects.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material. Arrangement is original abstract cards, neutral lanes, a single route arrow, cleared type, a cleared narrator, and original or cleared sound. No final asset is approved. Sound motif: one soft card movement for each queue drift, followed by silence at `WHO CARRIES THIS?`, then one clean placement sound when the route and owner appear. No alarm bed, urgency cue, or unlicensed music.
