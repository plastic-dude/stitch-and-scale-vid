## Concept and claim boundary

> “Evidence present is not evidence linked.”

A fictional claim card sits beside several evidence cards. The claim initially appears supported because evidence is nearby. The chain then requires exact field, evidence ID, source/version, activity, responsible agent, reviewer, limitation, link status, and disposition. One link remains broken and the claim is held. The story is about a **disciplined support graph**, not a product provenance graph, measurement traceability system, calibration service, AI assurance process, or correctness guarantee.

NIST describes the AI RMF as a voluntary framework for managing AI-associated risks and incorporating trustworthiness into design, development, use, and evaluation [903]. W3C PROV-O distinguishes entities, activities, and agents and provides relationships for use, generation, derivation, attribution, and responsibility [904]. NIST's metrological-traceability guidance describes an unbroken documented chain to a reference and warns that traceability alone does not guarantee fitness for purpose [905]. These sources motivate the fictional support graph and production review only; none proves that Stitch & Scale implements these frameworks or related capabilities.

All claims, fields, evidence IDs, sources, versions, activities, agents, reviewers, limitations, link statuses, and dispositions are fictional. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, numbers, warnings, evidence references, and controls require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry. David is a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use original abstract claim and evidence cards, a small support graph, and a neutral evidence board. Amber marks undefined or unverified support context. Red marks only `SUPPORT INCOMPLETE`. There is no green verified, traceable, fit-for-purpose, accurate, or approved state. Captions occupy a fixed lane away from `CLAIM`, `FIELD`, `EVIDENCE_ID`, `SOURCE`, `VERSION`, `ACTIVITY`, `AGENT`, `REVIEWED`, `LINK_STATUS`, `LIMITATION`, `DISPOSITION`, tables, numbers, labels, buttons, menus, hands, and primary actions.

Coverage: locked overhead master; claim/evidence insert; field/evidence-ID hold; source/version hold; activity/agent hold; reviewer/limitation hold; broken-link/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; mobile crop review; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static claim-to-evidence graph with the broken link centered if motion weakens legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collision, fake provenance, invented source or agent, unsupported traceability, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, and unreadable fields.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no private material.
