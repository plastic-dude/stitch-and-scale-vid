## Research-informed premise

Current public guidance shows that “complete” is destination-specific and package-shaped. Knitty’s June 17, 2026 guide requires a completed pattern plus separate written pattern, image, schematic, chart, and signed rights/originality materials, and distinguishes technical editing from optional test knitting. [1] Craft Yarn Council guidance separates the actual project, label, yarn bands, gauge swatch, electronic instructions, measurements, schematic, charts, invoice or agreement, and return shipping, while also requiring notes, material confirmation, and project-specific checks. [2] [3] Vogue Knitting uses a different one-page-PDF submission shape with swatch, sketch, technique explanation, and contact fields. [4]

Adjacent tools document broad export or PDF-import behavior, but an export bundle does not prove destination acceptance, technical correctness, chart readability, schematic scale, physical print review, test knitting, rights clearance, or publication approval. [5] [6] [7] The decision is therefore to frame a fictional bundle opening into individual evidence surfaces, with the destination and required components explicitly named before any handoff. The current product-proof blocker prevents showing this as Stitch & Scale UI.

## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or publication authority. If he appears, he speaks as a developer explaining why a package should be inspectable; he does not claim craft-practitioner, legal, accounting, tax, or publishing authority.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies that Stitch & Scale exposes an evidence-package or attachment-context surface. Every proposed card must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Do not claim that Stitch & Scale assembles packages, checks missing attachments, validates destination rules, preserves rights records, tracks package completeness, blocks a handoff, or predicts acceptance.

Preserve calculation, recorded fact, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, test knitting, image/model context, rights/permission, and destination-specific submission rules as distinct human evidence gates. A package card cannot prove the math, technical edit, test-knit result, chart readability, schematic scale, fit, rights clearance, or publication outcome.

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | A fictional sealed submission envelope marked `DESTINATION: UNCONFIRMED`. |
| Desire | Know whether the intended recipient can inspect every required artifact and its evidence boundary. |
| Obstacle | A single PDF, ZIP, or green “complete” label can conceal missing charts, schematics, image rights, material records, invoice/agreement, or return/custody notes. |
| Stakes | A handoff may be misunderstood if a bundle’s scope and missing components are invisible; no actual rejection, loss, or outcome is claimed. |
| Change | The bundle opens into a named artifact map and ends with `PACKAGE: UNVERIFIED` plus a clear next action. |
| Contradiction | More files do not automatically mean a complete package, and one export does not define every destination’s requirements. |
| Escalation | Sealed bundle → destination question → component cards → human-gate distinctions → one missing/uncertain item → explicit pause. |
| Final image | `PACKAGE: UNVERIFIED` beside `DESTINATION: NAME REQUIRED`, `REQUIRED ARTIFACTS: NOT VERIFIED`, and separate human-gate cards. |

## Treatment and beats

The film opens on an original envelope or neutral export-bundle prop. It is not a product screen and must carry `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. The envelope is labeled `DESTINATION: UNCONFIRMED`, then opens into individual cards: written pattern, image set, schematic, chart, gauge/material record, rights statement, invoice/agreement, and return/custody note. The cards do not become a fake checklist or a real publication submission. The camera pauses when `DESTINATION` and `REQUIRED ARTIFACTS` remain unresolved. The final state is `PACKAGE: UNVERIFIED`.

| Time | Beat | Image and action | Spoken idea | Evidence status |
|---:|---|---|---|---|

| 5–11s | Name the destination | Destination card opens: `WHO RECEIVES THIS? / WHICH RULES APPLY?`. | “Complete for whom—and under which submission rules?” | Destination question, not a current product feature. |
| 11–20s | Open the bundle | Individual cards appear one at a time: pattern text, image set, schematic, chart, gauge/material record. | “The pattern is one piece. The photos, chart, schematic, gauge, and materials are separate pieces.” | Public workflow framing from [1]–[3]. |
| 20–27s | Rights and custody | Rights/originality statement, invoice/agreement, and return/custody note appear, each separate. | “Then there are rights, the agreement, and what happens to the sample or remaining materials.” | Destination-specific and rights-sensitive, not legal advice. |
| 27–34s | Human gates stay human | Cards remain separate: `TECH EDIT`, `PRINT REVIEW`, `CHART READABILITY`, `SCHEMATIC SCALE`, `TEST KNIT`. | “A bundle can carry the evidence. It cannot make those human checks disappear.” | Process distinction; no technical correctness claim. |

## Visual grammar and production bible

Use one neutral tabletop, diffuse side light, restrained overhead and 45-degree inserts, and high-contrast original cards. The prop envelope must not use Stitch & Scale branding, publication logos, real pattern names, real contact details, real invoices, real customer/tester data, or actual destination forms. Every illustrative card must show `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` in a readable corner without covering the field being discussed.

Keep artifact families visually separate: `PATTERN TEXT`, `IMAGES`, `SCHEMATIC`, `CHART`, `GAUGE / MATERIALS`, `RIGHTS`, `AGREEMENT / INVOICE`, and `RETURN / CUSTODY`. Keep human gates on a separate row: `CALCULATION`, `TECHNICAL EDITING`, `PHYSICAL PRINT REVIEW`, `CHART READABILITY`, `SCHEMATIC SCALE`, `TEST KNITTING`, and `PUBLICATION DECISION`. Do not collapse them into a green approval badge or imply that one card proves another.

Lock envelope orientation, card order, typography, hand direction, narrator family, room tone, and light temperature across takes. David may appear in a neutral developer frame or remain voice-only. He must not perform knitting or be framed as a craft practitioner. Do not use generated imagery for exact charts, schematics, numbers, or technical instructions. Use only original abstract props until a current product capture is supplied.

### Locations and arrangements

The default location is a quiet desk or studio tabletop with no visible private papers, branded packaging, or real pattern pages. Arrangement A is overhead envelope-to-cards. Arrangement B is a shallow 45-degree handoff with cards laid in a destination order. Arrangement C is voice-only over a static, clearly illustrative bundle. If an approved current product capture later arrives, it must be inserted only after a new Director review and must not be composited with fictional UI fields.

No approval chime, completion sting, deadline cue, or music that implies acceptance. Record room tone before and after each setup.

Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, accent shifts, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, stale UI sounds, and effects that conceal destination, artifact, rights, or missing-item language. ## Continuity, risk, and acceptance gates

Keep the same envelope, card order, tabletop, light, narrator family, wardrobe, hand direction, and room tone across all takes. The final card must stay `PACKAGE: UNVERIFIED`; no take may switch to `APPROVED`, `PUBLISHED`, `READY`, or `SENT`. No caption may cover proof, hands, faces, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, or primary actions. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported claims, duplicate filenames, and props that could be mistaken for current UI.

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| No current product package proof | Critical | Supply a versioned approved capture or proof-registry entry for the exact surface, then rerun claim and asset validation. | Blocks generation. |
| Destination unspecified | Critical | Confirm the intended destination and its current requirements; do not generalize from Knitty, Vogue Knitting, or CYC. | Blocks generation. |
| Fictional cards mistaken for UI or real submission forms | Critical | Mark every card illustrative; use no logos, real forms, real numbers, or private records. | Hard fail. |
| Human gates collapsed into package completeness | High | Keep calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting separate. | Claim and visual review. |
| Rights/privacy ambiguity | High | Record ownership, permission, destination scope, and private-data exclusion for every asset. | Blocks release. |
| Caption obstruction or unreadable dense cards | High | Caption-on and caption-free review; one artifact family per close shot; no shrinking critical type. | Hard fail. |
| Mixed voice or audio defects | High | One narrator family, room tone, full-length listening review. | Hard fail. |
| Platform drift | Medium-high | Recheck official platform requirements and actual preview at approval. | Platform gate. |
| Unsupported urgency or outcome language | High | No deadline pressure, acceptance, sales, adoption, or customer-result claims. | Claim ledger gate. |
