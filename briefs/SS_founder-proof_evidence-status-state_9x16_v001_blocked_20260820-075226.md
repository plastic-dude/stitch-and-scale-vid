## Research-informed premise

Atlassian’s current status-report guidance describes a status report as a concise, timely update that summarizes progress, risks, and next steps, and recommends a report date, gathered data, blockers, and assigned actions. [1] Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 guidance demonstrates that publication workflow has several states: a submission may be accepted, held for future consideration with permission, awaiting a decision, technically edited, test knit or not, and eventually published; these are not interchangeable. [2]

Craft Yarn Council’s idea guidance distinguishes an idea, swatch/sketch/sample, completed project, acceptance, contract, and review, while noting that organizations have their own requirements and review timelines. [3] Its project checklist keeps labels, swatches, instructions, schematics, invoices, and return shipping as separate evidence surfaces. [4]

These sources support a bounded record-design distinction: **a status is meaningful only when its subject, scope, evidence window, setter, supporting records, next check, and limitations are visible**. The film must not claim that a status is correct, fresh, approved, technically edited, test-knit, rights-cleared, or publication-ready merely because a label says so.

## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester. If he appears, he speaks as a developer explaining why status labels need scope and evidence; he does not claim accounting, legal, publishing, or craft-practitioner authority.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies that Stitch & Scale exposes an evidence-status surface. Every proposed card is a fictional prop and must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

Do not claim that Stitch & Scale automates status, verifies freshness, checks evidence windows, tracks reviewers, predicts readiness, validates technical editing, replaces test knitting, establishes rights clearance, or approves publication. Preserve technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, test knitting, and publication approval as separate human evidence gates.

## Visual grammar and production bible

Use one neutral tabletop, diffuse side light, restrained camera movement, and high-contrast original cards. The status card must be abstract and contain no real pattern title, test-knit result, customer record, publication decision, invoice, private message, logo, or current UI. Every card must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

Keep `STATUS_VALUE`, `STATUS_SCOPE`, `EVIDENCE_WINDOW_START`, `EVIDENCE_WINDOW_END`, `STATUS_SET_AT`, `STATUS_SET_BY`, `SUPPORTING_RECORDS`, `NEXT_CHECK`, `REVIEWER`, `REVIEWED_AT`, `LIMITATIONS`, and `STATUS` as separate field families. Do not show a green `CORRECT`, `APPROVED`, `PUBLISHED`, `TAX READY`, or `DONE` badge. Do not imply that a timestamp proves freshness or that a reviewer field proves approval. Generated text is not UI truth.

Lock card orientation, typography, tabletop, hand direction, narrator family, room tone, and light temperature across takes. David may appear in a neutral developer frame or remain voice-only; he must not perform knitting or be framed as a craft practitioner. Technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, test knitting, rights clearance, and publication approval remain distinct human evidence gates.

If a status card is unavailable, use original cards labeled `SUBJECT / SCOPE / WINDOW / SETTER / RECORDS / NEXT CHECK / LIMITS`. If a hand actor is unavailable, use stop-motion cards or a text-only original sequence. If a founder frame is unavailable, use one approved founder voice. If a current approved product capture is later supplied, replace only fictional cards after a new Director review; do not infer automation, freshness, or approval from this brief.

## Sound motif and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family with consistent room tone. The optional sound motif is a soft status-badge peel, a restrained paper slide on the evidence window, and silence on `STATUS: UNVERIFIED`. No correctness chime, approval bed, urgency sting, or completion sound.

Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, accent shifts, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, stale UI sounds, and effects that conceal **scope**, **evidence window**, **setter**, **supporting records**, **next check**, or **limits**. Deliver caption-on and caption-free review versions. Automatic captions are draft-only; a human must verify every status, date, role, and limitation word.

## Acceptance gates and blocker

The package cannot move beyond **blocked** until the product owner supplies a current, versioned, non-private, rights-cleared proof-registry entry or approved capture for the exact evidence-status surface. It must identify `STATUS S-001`, design ID, subject ID, status value, scope, evidence-window start and end, status-set time, status-set by, supporting records, next check, reviewer, review time, limitations, and status. A status record does not prove underlying evidence, technical correctness, test-knit success, publication approval, rights clearance, or user outcome.

Do not use a real pattern, test-knit report, customer record, publication decision, invoice, logo, or private data without scoped rights/privacy clearance. Review caption-on and caption-free renders; reject captions that cover cards, dates, roles, records, status, logos, controls, or primary actions. Reject stale UI, unsupported claims, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, duplicate filenames, and any prop text that could be mistaken for current product UI.

**Exact Generator action:** Do not generate or upload. Await a current proof-registry entry or approved capture, rights/privacy clearance, confirmed destination platform, and Director re-authorization after all gates pass.
