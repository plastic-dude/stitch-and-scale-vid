## Concept and distinctness

A record missing from an output may be intentionally excluded or accidentally omitted. The nearest prior angle is `absence-state`, which concerns what a non-value means. `exclusion-state` changes the pain to the explicit decision to leave a source record out of a particular output and the evidence that the omission was not accidental. The hook becomes “Omitted is not excluded.” The visual metaphor changes from a number branching into value states to a source record approaching an output gate with an intentional-exclusion path and an omission-review path. The proof surface is `SOURCE / EXPECTED IN OUTPUT / INCLUDED? / EXCLUSION REASON / SCOPE / FILTER / REVIEWED / OUTPUT`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from false-precision anxiety to bounded export scope. The final line is “Name what left the output.” It does not recreate `absence-state`, `dependency-state`, `selection-state`, `preview-state`, `reconciliation-state`, `provenance-chain`, or `actionability-state`.
