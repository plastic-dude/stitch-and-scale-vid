## Research decision

W3C’s EPUB 3.3 Recommendation defines authoring requirements and a distribution/interchange format with package resources, metadata, and reading-system conformance.[188] The Library of Congress describes PDF/A-1 as a constrained PDF form intended for long-term preservation, seeking device independence, self-containment, and self-documentation, while noting that PDF/A-1b addresses visual appearance rather than structural or semantic properties.[189] Stitch & Scale’s publishing/export contract requires separate locale, metadata, chart, schematic, device, print, and warning review.[190] These sources support an export-integrity checklist, but do not prove Stitch & Scale’s export behavior.

| Engine element | Director decision |

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `paper-proof`, which focuses on whether a printed page remains usable. `export-portability` changes the pain to **whether a file’s identity, resources, metadata, page/chart meaning, and reader behavior survive before and beyond print**. The hook changes to **“Opening the file is not the same as carrying the pattern.”** The visual metaphor changes from paper inspection to **`FILE / READER / PAGE / CHART / METADATA`**. The proof surface changes from a printed-page close-up to one current exported file with manifest/preflight record, version, locale/unit, metadata, embedded resources, page/chart checks, and device/reader review. The emotional turn changes from physical confidence to transport confidence without standards claims. The final line changes to **“Check the file after it leaves the app.”**

It is distinct from `artifact-status` because it is transport and reader integrity rather than which copy exists; from `repro-note` because it is export survival rather than reproducibility; from `chart-description` because it is file/package/page behavior rather than chart meaning; from `locale-number-format` because it is export integrity rather than locale punctuation; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or evidence-roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.

## Treatment and production bible

Open on a neutral tabletop with an original abstract card marked `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. A fictional file card reads `PATTERN_v001.pdf`; it moves past cards labeled `FILE`, `READER`, `PAGE`, `CHART`, and `METADATA`. Add `LOCALE / UNIT / VERSION` and `HUMAN PRINT REVIEW`. The final card reads `FILE CHECKED — HUMAN REVIEW OPEN`.

A real Stitch & Scale exported file, manifest, preflight record, or reader capture may replace the fictional card only if a current, approved, non-private artifact verifies the exact filename, version, locale/unit, metadata, output condition, and rights to show it. If proof is absent, retain the blocker card and disclaimer. No PDF/A, EPUB, portability, accessibility, print-perfect, chart-perfect, or reader-independent claim is permitted.

Use one narrator family. David may appear only with authorization and remains described as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Captions must not cover file names, metadata, chart proof, tables, buttons, menus, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions. Generated text remains illustrative and cannot substitute for current product proof.

## Caption map and audio plan

Use one narrator family with close, dry narration. Sound motif: a download click, a soft page turn, and a pencil tick on `HUMAN PRINT REVIEW`. No “portable” chime, accessibility-pass stamp, or export-perfect bed. Check for voice drift, doubled words, overlaps, accent changes, inconsistent mic distance, and room-tone jumps.

Captions are narration-verbatim, sentence by sentence, inside a declared safe zone that never covers file names, metadata, chart proof, tables, or CTA. Keep one integrity family on screen at a time for mobile reading. Retain caption-on and caption-free review masters. Automatic captions are not accepted as final.
