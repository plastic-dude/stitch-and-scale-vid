## Research decision

The Government Analysis Function’s current dashboard guidance emphasizes audience knowledge, user testing, accessibility experts, and clear metadata such as source data, last update, relevant dates, period, definitions, methodology, quality information, and commentary. GOV.UK content guidance says tables should use `no data` or `not applicable` instead of empty cells and avoid ambiguous multi-item cells for assistive-technology users. These sources do not prove Stitch & Scale onboarding, sync, loading, recovery, persistence, or any empty-state component.

The research changed the concept from generic onboarding into **honest state labeling at the first record**. The film must not claim ease, onboarding completion, sync, data recovery, persistence, or record creation.

## Campaign assumption

No active human brief was present in the checked repository state. This run assumes founding-tester recruitment remains the working objective through the free demo and early-access cohort. The film invites designers to inspect one verified empty-state, first-record, record-status, or unavailable-data surface; it does not promise that the product creates a record or restores missing information.

## Dramatic engine

| Element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | A blank workspace does not say whether nothing has been created, a record is incomplete, or information is unavailable. |
| Desire | To know what the blank means and what the safest next action is. |
| Obstacle | A blank cell or empty screen can be mistaken for no data, a load failure, a missing field, or completed absence. |
| Stakes | A designer may enter duplicate information, overwrite context, or make a decision from an ambiguous absence; no product failure or outcome is claimed. |
| Change | The viewer moves from `EMPTY` to explicit states: `NO RECORD YET / INCOMPLETE / NOT APPLICABLE / UNAVAILABLE / NEXT SAFE ACTION`. |
| Contradiction | A calm first step can be offered without promising onboarding success or data availability. |
| Escalation | Blank screen → meaning questions → explicit status labels → one safe next action → current proof or blocker. |
| Final image | A fictional empty workspace with one labeled state and one safe next-action card; no recovered-data or success badge. |

## Difference from prior concepts

The nearest prior angle is `decision-ledger`, which distinguishes recorded, derived, estimated, and planned values. `first-record` changes the pain to **a blank or partial workspace not explaining what absence means**; the hook to **“A blank page needs a truthful label.”**; the visual metaphor from typed record categories to an empty workspace changing from `EMPTY` to `NO RECORD YET / INCOMPLETE / NOT APPLICABLE / UNAVAILABLE / NEXT SAFE ACTION`; the proof surface from ledger record types to empty-state, first-record, record-status, or unavailable-data behavior; the emotional turn from classification of values to calm orientation before creating or editing a record; and the final line to **“Label the blank before you fill it.”**

It is distinct from `decision-ledger` because it is not recorded-versus-derived classification; from `data-minimum` because it is not collection restraint; from `offline-continuity` and `recovery-proof` because it is not network loss or restore evidence; from `as-of` because it is not temporal currentness; from `error-language` because it is not warning phrasing after a validation issue; from `hands-busy` because it is not interrupted return state; and from `false-completion`, `release-gate-roster`, `support-boundary`, `feedback-loop`, `brief-before-knit`, `permission-line`, `sample-chain`, `material-notes`, `care-context`, `measurement-moment`, `gauge-reality`, `technique-profile`, `size-boundary`, `equivalent-meaning`, `readability-under-load`, `paper-proof`, `chart-parity`, `notation-key`, `sequence-map`, `count-check`, `caption-clearance`, `design-intent`, `source-note`, `event-window`, `evidence-over-output`, `inherited-thread`, `correction-trace`, `locale-drift`, and `reader-handoff` because the conflict is meaning assignment to absence at the first record. It does not reopen, overwrite, rename, or consume the imported reservations.

## Treatment, visual grammar, and production bible

Open on an original fictional workspace labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`: a blank project card reads `EMPTY`. The card splits into four explicit statuses: `NO RECORD YET`, `INCOMPLETE`, `NOT APPLICABLE`, and `UNAVAILABLE`. Only one is selected in any given frame. A fifth card reads `NEXT SAFE ACTION: REVIEW BEFORE CREATE`. A green `ONBOARDED`, `SYNCED`, `RECOVERED`, or `SAVED` badge is rejected.

The product insert is optional and blocked until a current, versioned, non-private proof capture verifies exact empty-state, first-record, record-status, or unavailable-data labels. If absent, retain `EMPTY-STATE PROOF REQUIRED — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Any real product UI, pattern page, record, personal data, person, or founder voice/image requires source and rights review. David may appear only with authorization and must be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Use one narrator family. Captions must not cover state labels, proof, buttons, menus, tables, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free cuts.
