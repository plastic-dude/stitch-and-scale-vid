## Treatment

The film opens with an original request card labeled `R-001 / REVIEW CHART 3` pointing to a recipient card marked `ACKNOWLEDGED`. A hand reaches for a `COMPLETED` stamp, then stops because no actor, completion time, affected artifact or gate, or unresolved issue is identified.

Cards are added for request ID, action requested, owner/actor, status, completed-at evidence, evidence artifact or gate, unresolved issue, and disposition. The track remains deliberately incomplete: `STATUS: ACKNOWLEDGED`, `OWNER_OR_ACTOR: UNCONFIRMED`, `COMPLETED_AT: UNCONFIRMED`, `EVIDENCE_ARTIFACT_OR_GATE: PENDING`, `UNRESOLVED_ISSUE: UNKNOWN`, and `DISPOSITION: HOLD`. The film does not claim that any recipient completed the action or that any craft gate passed.
