## Concept and claim boundary

> “Reviewed is not fresh.”

One fictional craft evidence card has a prior review stamp. Its current context changes, but the card has no explicit validity period, change-since-review state, or revalidation due point. The card stops at `REVALIDATION REQUIRED` rather than silently passing. The ending is a bounded temporal-validity lesson, not a product promise, default expiry rule, recommendation, or accuracy claim. The closing line is **“Name when this must be checked again.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes freshness timestamps, validity periods, expiry rules, revalidation schedules, continuous monitoring, current-context matching, or automatic stale detection. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, dates, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence board, one record card with a past review stamp, a current-context strip that changes, a restrained calendar/clock cue without fabricated product UI, amber for currentness unknown, red only for revalidation required, and no green current state. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, product claim, or default expiry policy. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers record ID, dates, validity, current context, change, revalidation, owner, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; medium record-card push-in; close readable proof holds for recorded-at/valid-for and current-context change; change-since-review insert; revalidation-due/owner hold; disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static before/after context comparison if temporal motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unreadable proof, and unsupported claims rather than hiding defects with effects.
