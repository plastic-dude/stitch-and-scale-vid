## Concept and claim boundary

> “Exported is not handed off.”

A fictional file leaves a sender's workspace. The receiving card is initially empty. Context, version, required action, open items, rights boundary, access needs, receipt, and disposition are attached one at a time. The story is about a **usable responsibility transfer**, not a product export workflow, recipient tracking, read receipt, task assignment, ownership transfer, accessibility implementation, or handoff-completion claim.

NICE describes structured handovers with complete and current information, defined roles, ongoing tasks, communication needs, and action by the receiving provider [894]. The NCBI/NICE guideline defines handover as transferring responsibility and information and warns that incomplete or delayed information can compromise safety and quality [895]. GOV.UK service guidance covers accessibility, delivery, technology, team roles, user research, and assessment [896]. These sources inform the fictional treatment only; none proves that Stitch & Scale implements a structured handoff.

All names, versions, contexts, tasks, open items, rights statements, access needs, receipts, and dispositions are fictional. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, recipient fields, warnings, and controls require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry. David is a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use original abstract sender/recipient cards, one file icon, and a neutral evidence board. The visual metaphor is a transfer line that cannot complete until context and open work travel with the file. Amber marks missing context or unverified fields. Red marks only `HANDOFF INCOMPLETE`. There is no green delivered, received, approved, or complete state. Captions occupy a fixed lane away from `HANDOFF`, `SENDER`, `RECIPIENT`, `CONTEXT`, `VERSION`, `REQUIRED_ACTION`, `OPEN_ITEMS`, `RIGHTS`, `ACCESS_NEEDS`, `RECEIPT`, `REVIEWED`, `DISPOSITION`, tables, numbers, labels, buttons, menus, hands, and primary actions.

Coverage: locked overhead master; sender card; file-transfer insert; recipient card; missing-context hold; version/action hold; open-items hold; rights/access hold; receipt/review/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; mobile crop review; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static sender-to-recipient diptych with required fields arranged as a transfer checklist if motion weakens legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collision, fake delivery, invented receipt, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, and unreadable fields.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no private material. Arrangement is original abstract sender/recipient cards, cleared type, one cleared narrator, and original or cleared audio. Sound motif: one soft file movement, silence at `CONTEXT: MISSING`, a restrained field-placement tone, and a neutral review tone. No delivered chime, notification ping, urgency cue, access-denied cue, or approval sound. No final asset is approved.
