## Research decision

W3C’s current target-size guidance frames easier activation around sufficient target size or spacing and reducing accidental activation for people with dexterity limitations; it describes a 24×24 CSS-pixel minimum criterion with exceptions. Apple’s current Human Interface Guidelines describe controls as a symbol, title, and optional value, with the title describing what the control relates to and the value representing state. These sources do not prove Stitch & Scale dimensions, accessibility conformance, hands-free support, one-handed support, or Android behavior.

The research changed the concept from generic mobile ergonomics into **state clarity during an interrupted hands-busy workflow**. The film must not claim one-handed success, voice control, hands-free operation, accessibility conformance, or automatic resume.

## Campaign assumption

No active human brief was present in the checked repository state. This run assumes founding-tester recruitment remains the working objective through the free demo and early-access cohort. The film invites designers to inspect one verified pause/resume, state, quick-return, or interruption-recovery surface; it does not promise that the product remembers or restores a workflow.

## Difference from prior concepts

The nearest prior angle is `measurement-moment`, which concerns preserving body/sample/unit/project context while entering a measurement. `hands-busy` changes the pain to **a maker needing to return to a pattern while the phone is not the only thing in their hands**; the hook to **“When the hands are busy, the next state should still be clear.”**; the visual metaphor from tape/body/unit labels to a phone beside yarn/tool moving through `PAUSE / RETURN / STATE`; the proof surface from measurement/grading entry to pause/resume, current-state, quick-return, or interruption-recovery surface; the emotional turn from preserved measurement meaning to calm return orientation; and the final line to **“Make the return visible.”**

It is distinct from `measurement-moment` because it is not measurement context; from `offline-continuity` because it is not network availability or sync; from `readability-under-load` because it is not dense reading; from `caption-clearance` because it is not video caption placement; from `sequence-map` because it is not pattern dependency; from `support-boundary` because it is not question routing; from `paper-proof` because it is not medium change; and from `false-completion`, `release-gate-roster`, `recovery-proof`, `decision-ledger`, `data-minimum`, `source-note`, `as-of`, `notation-key`, `count-check`, `equivalent-meaning`, `chart-parity`, `gauge-reality`, `material-notes`, `care-context`, `sample-chain`, `technique-profile`, `size-boundary`, `event-window`, `permission-line`, `feedback-loop`, `brief-before-knit`, `design-intent`, `evidence-over-output`, `inherited-thread`, `correction-trace`, `locale-drift`, and `reader-handoff` because the conflict is interruption state in a hands-busy mobile workflow. It does not reopen, overwrite, rename, or consume the imported reservations.

## Treatment, visual grammar, and production bible

The phone shifts slightly out of reach. Four cards appear: `PAUSE`, `RETURN`, `STATE`, and `CHECK`. The frame never shows a voice waveform, hands-free icon, one-handed success badge, or automatic-resume confirmation.

The product insert is optional and blocked until a current, versioned, non-private proof capture verifies exact pause, state, return, quick-return, or interruption-recovery labels. ## Exact script

**0–7s — Narrator:** “When the hands are busy, the next state should still be clear.”

**7–15s — Narrator:** “What did you pause? Where do you return? What should you see first?”

**15–25s — Narrator:** “A clear state can support a return. It cannot promise a hands-free workflow.”

**25–31s — Narrator:** “Pause the work. Check the state. Then continue.”

**31–40s — Narrator:** “If Stitch & Scale shows a current return surface, show exactly what it shows. If not, keep the claim open.”

**40–45s — Narrator:** “Make the return visible.”
