## Research-informed premise

The Craft Yarn Council’s current industry checklist asks a designer to reconfirm yarn, dye lot, sample size, and destination instructions, keep notes, and separately submit the actual project, label, yarn labels, gauge swatch, electronic and printed instructions, measurements for each size, charts, schematic, invoice, and return-shipping information. [1]

CYC’s current schematic guidance defines a schematic as a visual rendering with relevant measurements that gives a size/shape overview, fit reference, and modification template. [2] That makes the review subject specific: a schematic for one piece and set of measurements is not a review of every written instruction, chart, size, or test-knit result.

Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 submission guidance requests a complete design as individual files and distinguishes the designer’s submission from later technical editing, optional test knitting, rights, editorial selection, and publication. [3] W3C’s current WCAG Evaluation Methodology 2.0 requires evaluation scope definition, exploration, representative sample selection, evaluation, and reporting. It warns that a selected subset generally does not support conformance claims for an entire website. [4]

Do not claim automatic review coverage, sample selection, reviewer tracking, extrapolation, technical correctness, accessibility conformance, publication readiness, or acceptance.

Calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, test knitting, rights, privacy, accessibility review, and publication decision remain distinct human evidence gates.

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | Fictional chart inspection card for one design. |
| Desire | Make a review statement people can trust without overgeneralizing it. |
| Obstacle | A broad `PATTERN REVIEWED` badge hides the exact sample and what remains uninspected. |
| Stakes | A finding about one chart, one size, one version, or one screen condition may be mistaken for a finding about every representation and size. |
| Change | The broad badge is removed and replaced with exact subject, version, sample, representation, conditions, question, reviewer, finding, limit, and outside-scope fields. |
| Contradiction | A narrow review can be useful and honest; it becomes misleading only when its limits disappear. |
| Escalation | Broad badge → subject/version → sample/representation → conditions/question → finding/limit → outside-scope boundary. |
| Final image | `FINDING: LIMITED / OUTSIDE SCOPE VISIBLE / HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`. |

## Treatment, beats, and visual grammar

Use one unbranded tabletop, one fictional inspection card, one broad status sticker, one sample/condition rail, one finding card, one outside-scope card, and one reviewer card. All cards are original illustrative props. Open on the tidy but misleading status and make the inspection boundary visible one field at a time.
