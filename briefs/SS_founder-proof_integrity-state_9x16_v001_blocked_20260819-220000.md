## Concept and claim boundary

> “Present is not integrity.”

One fictional craft artifact has a stable artifact ID. Its source/provenance strip and custody history are present, but the content has not been checked. The card splits into `EXPECTED STATE` and `OBSERVED STATE`; a fictional integrity check reveals one changed field, scopes the change, explains it, records review, and sets `DISPOSITION: HOLD FOR CORRECTION`. The ending is a bounded comparison, not a product promise, cryptographic claim, legal conclusion, or approval. The closing line is **“Name what changed before you trust what stayed.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes an integrity checker, hash, checksum, immutable record, change detector, audit log, expected/observed comparison, or automatic correction flow. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence-board background, one stable artifact ID, a split expected/observed card, an amber check line, red only for detected change, and a restrained blue provenance/custody strip in the background. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, product claim, cryptographic symbol, hash visual, or automatic green approval. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers artifact ID, expected/observed values, change scope, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; medium artifact-card push-in; close readable proof holds for expected/observed fields; mismatch insert; change-scope and explanation insert; review/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static comparison table if motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unreadable proof, and unsupported claims rather than hiding defects with effects.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material.
