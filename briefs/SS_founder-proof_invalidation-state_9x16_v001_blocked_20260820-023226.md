## Research-informed distinction

Pass 197 separated suitability evidence from authority to accept for a defined scope. Pass 198 asks what happens next: if an upstream entity, source, material, version, or human-gate result changes, the earlier decision may no longer be safe to reuse. W3C PROV-DM and PROV-O support representing entities, activities, agents, derivation, revision, responsibility, and invalidation; Craft Yarn Council guidance supports recording notes, reconfirming materials, checking dye lots, matching gauge, checking math, and keeping human submission evidence distinct. [1] [2] [3]

> **A decision can be authorized and still require review when what it depends on changes.**

These sources inform general workflow education only. They do not establish that Stitch & Scale currently exposes dependency graphs, provenance links, stale-input warnings, invalidation flags, or review propagation.

## Nearest prior and concept difference

| Element | Pass 197 `authorization-state` | Pass 198 `invalidation-state` |
|---|---|---|
| Primary pain | A suitability decision is treated as authorized for every scope or recipient | An authorized decision is reused after an upstream dependency changes |
| Hook | “Fit for use is not the same as authorized for this scope.” | “An authorized decision can still need review when what it depends on changes.” |
| Visual metaphor | Complete evidence packet beside an unused authorization stamp | A linked source-input-decision chain whose changed source breaks the decision’s usable state |
| Proof surface | `AUTH A-001 / EVIDENCE_PACKET / DECISION_SCOPE / AUTHORIZED_ROLE / LIMITATIONS / REVIEW_TRIGGER / DECISION_RECORD / DISPOSITION` | `INV A-001 / SOURCE_OR_INPUT / VERSION_OR_MATERIAL / DEPENDENCY_LINK / CHANGE_EVENT / IMPACT_CHECK / REVIEW_STATUS / DISPOSITION` |
| Emotional turn | Confidence becomes bounded authority | Confidence becomes honest rechecking rather than silent reuse |
| Final line | “Name who can accept it, what they accepted, and when the decision must be checked again.” | “Show what changed, what depended on it, and what must be checked again.” |

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Design |
|---|---|
| Subject | A linked chain of illustrative source, input, human-gate, and authorized-decision cards. |
| Desire | Reuse a decision safely without repeating every step blindly. |
| Obstacle | One upstream source, material, version, or human-gate condition changes, but the downstream decision still looks approved. |
| Stakes | A stale decision can travel into later work; the film must not claim a real incident or product outcome. |
| Change | The changed dependency is named, impact is marked unknown, and disposition changes to `REVIEW REQUIRED`. |
| Contradiction | A decision can remain historically authorized while no longer being safe to reuse unchanged. |

| Final image | `INV A-001 / SOURCE_OR_INPUT: YARN + DYE LOT + GAUGE NOTE / VERSION_OR_MATERIAL: CHANGED / DEPENDENCY_LINK: PRESENT / CHANGE_EVENT: NEW INPUT / IMPACT_CHECK: PENDING / REVIEW_STATUS: REQUIRED / DISPOSITION: HOLD`. |

## Treatment and beats

The film opens with an original neutral chain of cards: `SOURCE / INPUT`, `HUMAN GATE`, and `AUTHORIZED DECISION`. A thin paper thread visibly connects the cards. The decision card reads `AUTHORIZED FOR SCOPE — ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. A hand begins to move the decision downstream.

The upstream card changes from `YARN + DYE LOT + GAUGE NOTE / VERSION 1` to `YARN + DYE LOT + GAUGE NOTE / VERSION 2`. The thread is cut and a small `CHANGE EVENT` card appears. The downstream decision remains visibly marked from history but receives a new overlay: `REVIEW REQUIRED`. The human-gate cards remain separate and unsupplied. The film does not show an actual customer project, product control, alert, or outcome.

## Visual grammar and coverage

Use overhead and hands-only side-angle coverage, neutral paper, high contrast, sentence-case labels, and dedicated readable inserts for the source/input, version/material, dependency link, change event, impact check, review status, and disposition cards. Capture a wide chain master, a macro of the linked cards, a clean version-change insert, a close shot of the broken-link state, a human-gate boundary insert, a caption-free proof pass, and a final disclaimer frame. Captions may not cover hands, cards, labels, numbers, logos, controls, menus, or the primary action.

Primary location is a quiet tabletop with neutral light and no private or branded material. Alternatives are a wall-mounted dependency board, a no-hand version, or text-only original cards. Do not substitute a screenshot, copyrighted pattern, customer record, private message, logo, or current UI capture without approved proof and rights.

## Sound motif and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only, restrained card movement, a soft paper-thread break cue when the upstream version changes, and a neutral hold tone when `IMPACT_CHECK: PENDING` and `REVIEW_STATUS: REQUIRED` appear. No unlicensed music. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, clipped consonants, stale UI, unsupported claims, and effects that conceal a defect. Deliver caption-on and caption-free review versions. Human-review captions; automatic captions are only a draft.
