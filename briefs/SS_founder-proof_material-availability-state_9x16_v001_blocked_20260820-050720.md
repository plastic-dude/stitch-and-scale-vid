## Research-informed premise

Craft Yarn Council guidance says to reconfirm yarn and color selection with an editor or design director, check that ball bands share a dye-lot number, reconfirm with the yarn manufacturer that the selected yarn will be available for at least the next year, reconfirm sample size, keep notes, and discuss yarn or color problems with the manufacturer or editor. It also calls for yarn type, yardage and weight, fiber content, quantities by size, accessories, measurements, and the size made to be identified in instructions. [1]

Craft Yarn Council’s designer pitfalls list names missing yarn colors and precise amounts, discontinued yarn colors, missing accessory quantities, gauge mismatch, missing records, incomplete instructions, and schematics not drawn to scale as recurring problems. [2] Its standards page describes shared guidelines intended to make yarn, needle, hook, and pattern information more uniform so consumers can select materials and complete projects. [3]

> **A yarn name is not a material handoff.**
