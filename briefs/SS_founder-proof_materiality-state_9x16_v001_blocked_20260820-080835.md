## Research-informed premise

The Craft Yarn Council’s current body-sizing guidance distinguishes actual body measurements, finished garment measurements, ease, and schematics. It says sizing charts are guidelines and that changes to body and sleeve lengths may be appropriate for individual differences, but certain sizing changes may alter a garment’s appearance. [1]

CYC’s current pattern-writing overview lists written patterns, video tutorials, charts, graphs, schematics, and combinations. It recommends notes during making, transcription, checking abbreviations and sizing, technical editing, and testing. Testers are asked whether directions, abbreviations, symbols, charts, materials, and finished appearance line up. [2]

Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 guidance distinguishes the designer’s complete submission from later technical editing, fit work, optional test knitting, rights ownership, and publication decision. [3] Digital.gov’s current plain-language guidance says public content should be written for a specific audience and designed and tested for understanding. [4]

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | Fictional change card moving through consequence context. |
| Desire | Make a truthful edit without hiding which review gate it may affect. |
