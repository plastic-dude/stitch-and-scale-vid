## Dramatic engine before camera

**Subject:** A fictional `MEANING PRESERVATION` card, blank chart-like grid, and blank schematic outline.
**Desire:** Know that a maker can still interpret and use the pattern after it changes medium.
**Obstacle:** Rendering can preserve appearance while losing reading order, chart/key relationships, schematic scale, labels, locale, structure, or table usability.
**Stakes:** A designer may mistake a visually complete export for a reader-ready pattern. The film must not claim this happened to a Stitch & Scale user.
**Change:** Move from `RENDERED: YES` to named significant property, reader test, and human status.
**Contradiction:** A prettier or more stable-looking render can still be less usable if the relationship that carries meaning is gone.
**Escalation:** Source property; rendered property; medium; significant property; reader test; phone/print; chart/schematic; locale; status.
**Final image:** `RENDERED → READER TEST → MEANING PRESERVED?`, with `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.
