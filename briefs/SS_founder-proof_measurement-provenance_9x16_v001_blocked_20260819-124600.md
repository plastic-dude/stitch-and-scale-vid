## Research decision

CYC distinguishes actual body measurements from completed-garment sizing and schematics, explains ease relative to body measurements, and calls fit/length charts guidelines.[231] The Truly Myrtle route presented a robot challenge, so no substantive listing or schematic claims were taken from it.[232] Stitch & Scale’s contracts keep measurement point, body/garment distinction, basis, source, date, units, technical editing, schematic/print review, and test knitting separate.[233] [234] These sources support a provenance checklist, but do not prove Stitch & Scale captures or explains one.

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `measure-point`, which focuses on where a measurement starts and ends. `measurement-provenance` changes the pain to **whether the maker can trace a number’s origin and transformation**. The hook changes to **“A measurement needs a before.”** The visual metaphor changes from a measurement line to **`BODY / EASE / FINISHED / SAMPLE / DERIVED / METHOD / DATE / SOURCE / STATUS`**. The proof surface changes from a measurement point to a current measurement-provenance surface. The emotional turn changes from locating the line to understanding the number’s lineage without a fit promise. The final line changes to **“Show where the number came from.”**

It is distinct from `basis-label` because it traces lineage across multiple value types; from `fit-intent` because it does not state intended fit; from `rounding-rule` or `locale-number-format` because it concerns origin rather than presentation; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or evidence-roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.

The end card reads `MEASUREMENT LINEAGE VISIBLE — REVIEW OPEN`.

A real Stitch & Scale measurement-provenance surface may replace the fictional card only if a current, approved, non-private capture or proof-registry entry verifies the exact surface, labels, product version/date, and rights to show it. If proof is absent, retain the blocker card and disclaimer. No provenance capture, automatic ease calculation, derived-value explanation, or fit-validation claim is permitted.

Use one narrator family. David may appear only with authorization and remains described as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Captions must not cover measurement fields, numbers, proof, tables, buttons, menus, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions. Generated text remains illustrative and cannot substitute for current product proof.
