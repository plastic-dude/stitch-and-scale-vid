## Research decision

Craft Yarn Council guidance says the Standard Yarn Weight System is not an interchangeability chart, that gauge and yarn characteristics still require checking, and that fit and length charts are guidelines whose changes may alter a garment’s appearance. The QA and publishing contracts preserve human review gates and warn against treating changed inputs as silently certified outputs. These sources support a visible boundary between supported guidance, personal modification, and recheck; they do not prove Stitch & Scale validates edits or preserves grading.

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `fit-intent`, which focuses on the meaning of body, finished garment, ease, and fit measurements. `mod-boundary` changes the pain to **what remains reviewed after a maker changes an input, construction choice, or dimension**. The hook changes to **“A modification is a new question, not a free pass.”** The visual metaphor changes from body/garment/ease cards to **`SUPPORTED CHANGE / PERSONAL MOD / RECHECK / NOT REVIEWED`**. The proof surface changes from sizing/fit guidance to a current customization note, supported alteration point, post-edit warning, modified-pattern state, recheck prompt, or human-review boundary. The emotional turn changes from choosing with context to creative agency with honest review boundaries. The final line changes to **“Name what changed before you trust what remains.”**

It is distinct from `fit-intent` because it is post-review change scope rather than measurement meaning; from `change-notice` because it is a maker-originated modification rather than a designer-issued version change; from `rounding-rule` because it is not a hidden numeric transformation; from `gauge-reality` because it is not gauge capture; from `technique-profile` because it is not prerequisite technique; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.

The first card reads `CHANGE SLEEVE LENGTH?` A second card divides the request into `SUPPORTED CHANGE` and `PERSONAL MOD`. A third card adds `RECHECK` and `NOT REVIEWED`. The final card reads `NAME WHAT CHANGED — RECHECK WHAT FOLLOWS`.

A real Stitch & Scale screen may replace the fictional card only if a current, approved, non-private capture or proof-registry entry verifies the exact surface, labels, product version/date, and rights to show it. If proof is absent, retain the blocker card and disclaimer. No real pattern files, customer modifications, photos, or private messages may appear without scoped permission. Do not imply automatic recalculation, preserved grading, safe modifications, or validated fit.

Use one narrator family. David may appear only with authorization and remains described as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor.
