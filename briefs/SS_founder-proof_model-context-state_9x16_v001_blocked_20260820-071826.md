## Research-informed premise

Craft Yarn Council guidance asks designers to reconfirm the sample garment size required to fit the photography model and not make the project to fit the designer unless that is the requested size. It also separates the actual project, gauge swatch, instructions, schematic, labels, invoice, and return shipping. [1] CYC sizing guidance distinguishes actual body measurements from completed-garment measurements and schematics, describes ease as a relationship between them, and calls the charts guidelines rather than guarantees. [2] Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 submission guidance says designers photograph their own submissions or arrange photography, keeps the sample in the designer’s possession in that workflow, and asks wearable garments to be shown on a correctly sized model in the intended way and setting; it distinguishes an oversized presentation from a minimal-ease presentation. [3]

These sources support a bounded record-design distinction: **a photograph can show a garment while leaving the garment size, model context, fit intention, pose, and image purpose unstated**. They do not prove that a photo demonstrates fit, grading, size inclusion, comfort, accessibility, or publication acceptance, and they do not prove a Stitch & Scale model-context feature.

Knitty’s Winter 2026 page is seasonal publication context only. It must not be used as urgency, affiliation, guarantee, or a customer outcome claim. The film remains year-round and does not mention Knitty or its deadline.

## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester. If he appears, he speaks as a developer explaining why a photo needs context; he does not claim craft-practitioner authority.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved, non-private, rights-cleared capture verifies that Stitch & Scale exposes a model-context, image-context, or sample-photo record. Every proposed card is a fictional prop and must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` in the production bible and on any visible frame.

Do not claim that Stitch & Scale identifies a garment size from a photograph, matches a model to a sample, evaluates fit, verifies ease, validates grading, measures a body, checks size inclusion, approves photography, or guarantees a visual or physical outcome. Keep technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, and test knitting as distinct human evidence gates.

## Treatment and beats

Open on a neutral tabletop with an original card reading `PHOTO CONTEXT`. A fictional garment silhouette sits beside it. The image card looks complete until it opens to reveal `GARMENT_SIZE_MADE: UNKNOWN`, `MODEL_CONTEXT: NOT RECORDED`, `EASE_OR_FIT_INTENT: NOT RECORDED`, `POSE_OR_VIEW: NOT RECORDED`, and `IMAGE_PURPOSE: UNKNOWN`. A boundary card keeps `FIT`, `GRADING`, `SIZE INCLUSION`, `TECH EDIT`, `PRINT`, `SCHEMATIC`, and `TEST KNIT` separate. The end state remains `PHOTO CONTEXT: UNVERIFIED`.

## Visual grammar and production bible

Use one quiet neutral tabletop, diffuse side light, restrained camera movement, and high-contrast original cards. The garment silhouette must be abstract and non-identifiable. Do not use a real garment, model, body measurement, publication photo, customer record, pattern screenshot, supplier image, logo, or current app chrome. Every card must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

Keep `GARMENT_SIZE_MADE`, `BODY_CONTEXT`, `MODEL_CONTEXT`, `FINISHED_MEASUREMENTS`, `EASE_OR_FIT_INTENT`, `POSE_OR_VIEW`, `IMAGE_PURPOSE`, `CAPTURED_AT`, and `RIGHTS_SCOPE` as separate field families. Do not show a dense full-screen table or a face/body image. Do not use a green `FITS` badge, size-recommendation stamp, inclusive-size badge, publication-ready stamp, or visual effect that implies the photo validated fit or grading. Generated text is not UI truth.

The production bible locks the same card orientation, typography, tabletop, hand direction, narrator family, room tone, and light temperature across takes. David may appear in a neutral developer frame or remain voice-only; he must not perform knitting or be framed as a craft practitioner. Technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

### Alternatives

If a garment silhouette is unavailable, use a neutral rectangle labeled `SAMPLE IMAGE: EXAMPLE ONLY`. If a hand actor is unavailable, use stop-motion cards or a text-only original sequence. If a founder frame is unavailable, use one approved founder voice over the tabletop. If a current approved product capture is later supplied, replace only the fictional cards after a new Director review; do not infer a model-context feature from this brief.

## Sound motif and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family with a close, dry voice and consistent room tone. The optional sound motif is a soft camera-shutter imitation only if original and non-assertive, a paper-card slide on `MODEL_CONTEXT`, and held silence on `PHOTO CONTEXT: UNVERIFIED`. No fit-confirmation chime, size-recommendation stamp, inclusion badge, or publication-ready bed.

Record clean room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, accent shifts, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, stale UI sounds, and effects that conceal **size**, **model**, **fit intent**, **purpose**, **rights**, or **status**. Deliver caption-on and caption-free review versions. Automatic captions are draft-only; a human must verify every size, context, fit-intent, purpose, rights, and status word.

## Acceptance gates and blocker

The package cannot move beyond **blocked** until the product owner supplies a current, versioned, non-private, rights-cleared proof-registry entry or approved capture for the exact image-context surface. It must identify `IMAGE I-001`, design ID, sample ID, garment size made, body context, model context, finished measurements, ease or fit intention, image purpose, pose/view, captured-at time, source/basis, rights scope, reviewer, review time, and status. A context record does not prove fit, grading, comfort, size inclusion, accessibility, or publication acceptance.

Do not use a real model, body measurement, garment, publication photo, customer record, pattern, logo, or private data without scoped rights/privacy clearance. Review caption-on and caption-free renders; reject captions that cover hands, cards, numbers, rights fields, faces, or primary actions. Reject stale UI, unsupported claims, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, duplicate filenames, and any prop text that could be mistaken for current product UI.

**Exact Generator action:** Do not generate or upload. Await a current proof-registry entry or approved capture, rights/privacy clearance, confirmed destination platform, and Director re-authorization after all gates pass.
