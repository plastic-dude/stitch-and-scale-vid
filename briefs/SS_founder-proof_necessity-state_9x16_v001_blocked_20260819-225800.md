## Concept and claim boundary

> “Permitted is not necessary.”

Two fictional evidence cards describe one craft decision. Several fields are permitted or visible, but no purpose, minimum value, consequence of omission, extra-exposure review, retention, or disposition is attached. The card becomes bounded only when every retained field has a stated reason to stay. The ending is a data-use and decision-quality lesson, not a legal-minimization test, privacy-compliance claim, redaction promise, secure-processing claim, or product capability claim. The closing line is **“Name why this field stays.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes field-level necessity, data minimization, purpose limitation, minimum values, withholding, retention, privacy controls, or automated minimization. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence board, two fictional evidence cards for `DECISION D-001`, a permitted-field tray separated from a necessity frame, and arrows from decision purpose to field to why-needed to minimum value to consequence/exposure to retention to review. Amber marks undefined necessity; red marks only `NECESSITY INCOMPLETE`; there is no green minimized state. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, product claim, or field identity. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers decision, purpose, field, why-needed, minimum, withheld, consequence, exposure, retention, review, disposition, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; two-card side-by-side; permitted-tray insert; decision/purpose close hold; field/why-needed hold; minimum/withheld/consequence hold; exposure/retention hold; review/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static permitted-versus-necessary comparison with one explicit unjustified-field callout if motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unreadable proof, and unsupported claims rather than hiding defects with effects.
