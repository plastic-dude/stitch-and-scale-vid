## Concept and claim boundary

> “No error is not observable.”

A fictional process card looks quiet. It does not tell the reviewer whether the process is healthy, waiting, progressing, stale, or silently failing. A second card names expected state, observed signal, last update, gap, review, and disposition without inventing a monitoring system. The story is about **state evidence**, not observability infrastructure, uptime, reliability, status-message implementation, live regions, telemetry, logs, metrics, traces, continuous monitoring, or product correctness.

OpenTelemetry defines observability as understanding internal state from outputs such as traces, metrics, and logs [885]. W3C's Status Messages guidance concerns programmatically determined messages for assistive technologies without taking focus [886]. NIST SP 800-137 discusses continuous monitoring for visibility and timely response [887]. These sources inform the fictional proof surface and production review only; none proves that Stitch & Scale implements any related capability.

All process labels, timestamps, signals, states, gaps, and dispositions are fictional. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, warnings, progress, waiting states, errors, and monitoring behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry. David is a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester.

## Treatment and beat sheet

Use a neutral evidence board with fictional `PROCESS P-001`. A progress line remains still while the screen appears quiet. The card divides into `EXPECTED`, `OBSERVED`, `LAST_UPDATED`, `SIGNAL`, `STATE`, and `GAP`. The final state makes uncertainty explicit and does not display a fabricated error or health badge.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use original abstract process cards, a neutral evidence board, and a silent progress line. The visual metaphor is a quiet surface beside an expected-versus-observed signal bridge. Amber marks missing or unverified signal. Red marks only `OBSERVABILITY INCOMPLETE`. There is no green health, completion, uptime, reliability, or approval state. Captions occupy a fixed lane away from `PROCESS`, `EXPECTED`, `OBSERVED`, `LAST_UPDATED`, `SIGNAL`, `STATE`, `GAP`, `REVIEWED`, `DISPOSITION`, tables, numbers, labels, buttons, menus, hands, and primary actions.

Coverage: locked overhead master; quiet-process establishing shot; expected/observed split; last-update close hold; state/signal close hold; gap bridge insert; review/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; mobile crop review; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static quiet-screen versus state-card diptych if motion weakens the observable-state contrast. Reject stale UI, caption collision, fake error, invented health state, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, and unreadable fields.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no private material. Arrangement is original abstract process cards, cleared type, one cleared narrator, and original or cleared audio. Sound motif: quiet room tone, a restrained signal tick only when an observed signal appears, silence at `STATE: UNKNOWN`, and a neutral review tone. No error alarm, success chime, urgency cue, uptime cue, or monitoring-system sound. No final asset is approved.
