## Dramatic engine before camera

**Subject:** A fictional observation card for one test-knit attempt.  
**Desire:** Preserve what happened, under which conditions, before making a broader decision.  
**Obstacle:** A green check can survive after pattern version, size, gauge, representation, steps, evidence, or unresolved follow-up disappear.  
**Stakes:** A bounded observation may be mistaken for proof about every size, every knitter, or release readiness; the video must not claim this happened to a Stitch & Scale user.  
**Change:** Move from “TESTED: YES” to “OBSERVED: bounded, evidenced, and limited.”  
**Contradiction:** An honest unresolved decision can be safer than an unscoped pass.  
**Escalation:** The card starts with a green check; fields appear; the scope narrows; evidence and limit remain visible; `DECISION: UNRESOLVED` is preserved.  
**Final image:** `PATTERN VERSION / SIZE / GAUGE / OBSERVATION / EVIDENCE / LIMIT / FOLLOW-UP` with `STATUS: OBSERVED / DECISION UNRESOLVED` and `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

## Treatment

Open on a clean desk with a paper card reading `TEST KNIT OBSERVATION`. The narrator says, “A test knit is an observation, not a universal verdict.” A green check appears, then the card unfolds into `PATTERN VERSION`, `SIZE`, `GAUGE`, and `REPRESENTATION`. A second strip reveals `STEPS`, `OBSERVATION`, and `EVIDENCE`. The final fields read `LIMIT`, `FOLLOW-UP`, and `STATUS: OBSERVED / DECISION UNRESOLVED`. The narrator explains that this is not a release roster and does not replace technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, or additional test knitting. The founder turns the paper away and says he will not pretend it is the app. The final card asks founding testers what the current product can actually preserve about an observed knit.

## Visual grammar and production bible

Use one narrator family, one tabletop, one paper system, one charcoal/cream/ink-blue palette, and a strict type hierarchy. The only readable words are the fictional labels listed in this brief, with the fixed footer `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Do not render real tester identities, pattern names, size outcomes, gauge numbers, feedback screenshots, chart symbols, schematic measurements, customer records, or UI controls. Generated text is not UI truth. Any future product number, label, warning, or control must come from a current approved capture.

The primary location is a quiet desk with no private documents. Use one locked overhead camera and one 45-degree macro angle. Keep the observation card within a high-contrast central area. Use one green check, a field-by-field reveal, an unresolved decision, and a pencil underline beneath `LIMIT` as the visual rhythm. No digital glitch, fake loading, fake save, fake database, or effect may imply product behavior. The arrangement is observation card centered with version/condition fields at top, evidence in the middle, limit and decision fields below.
