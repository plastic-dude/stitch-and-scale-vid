## Concept and distinctness

An alarming label can collapse different questions: how much harm is possible, how soon it matters, when it is due, who owns it, and what happens if it waits. The nearest prior angle is `acknowledgement-state`, which concerns the message-to-action lifecycle. `priority-state` changes the pain to unexplainable ranking between competing review items. The hook becomes “Urgent is not important.” The visual metaphor changes from a warning travelling through handoff gates to multiple review cards separating into impact and urgency axes before a bounded queue. The proof surface is `IMPACT / URGENCY / DUE / OWNER / PRIORITY / REASON / ESCALATION / REVIEWED`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from accountable handoff to defensible triage. The final line is “Name the reason before you raise the priority.” It does not recreate `risk-trigger`, `acknowledgement-state`, `consent-state`, `response-state`, `review-expiry`, `intent-state`, `outcome-proof`, imported `false-completion`, or imported `release-gate-roster`.
