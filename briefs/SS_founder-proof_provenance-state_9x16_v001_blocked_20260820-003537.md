## Truth boundary

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester. His role is to explain why trustworthy records matter to the people who perform the work. Human evidence remains separate: calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting are not replaced by a product, a card, or a film.

No current product-proof registry entry or approved capture verifies provenance records, version lineage, revision threads, retained source notes, automatic agent attribution, immutable history, archival retention, or legal compliance. Every on-camera card is an original tabletop prop labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Do not show or imply current Stitch & Scale labels, fields, controls, numbers, warnings, or workflows.

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Design |
|---|---|
| Subject | One pattern instruction page that has changed after a note, sample observation, technical edit, or test observation. |
| Desire | To trust the current page without losing the reason it became current. |
| Obstacle | A polished present-tense page hides the prior entity, change activity, source note, responsible agent, affected gate, and disposition. |
| Stakes | A reader, editor, or tester may act on a change without knowing its basis or scope; the story must not claim a specific defect or outcome. |
| Change | The page is re-seen as a lineage question rather than a finished artifact. |
| Contradiction | “Current” can look complete while being historically opaque. |
| Escalation | Current page → missing change mark → disconnected note/sample/edit/test evidence → explicit safe next action. |
| Final image | A paper thread links the current page back to a note, sample detail, edit mark, and test observation; the final card reads `SHOW WHAT THIS VERSION CAME FROM`. |

## Treatment and visual grammar

The film opens on a clean current page under calm room tone. A hand places a small card reading `CURRENT VERSION` beside it. The card looks complete. A second card asks `WHAT CHANGED?`; the answer is absent. The camera pulls back to reveal four disconnected evidence objects: a dated working note, a sample detail, a technical-edit mark, and a test observation. A neutral tabletop line is drawn between the objects, but it stops at the current page. The narrator names the gap without blaming an individual.

The visual language is tactile and documentary: overhead 9:16 compositions, locked-off tabletop shots, restrained macro inserts, natural paper texture, neutral daylight, no simulated product screens, no glossy launch effects, no fake notifications, and no recognizable private records. Use one narrator family only. Keep captions in an unobstructed lower safe area; when proof objects occupy that area, move captions to the upper safe area or use a proof-free reaction shot. Every dense card receives a dedicated readable insert and a caption-free review.

## Coverage and alternatives

Primary coverage is an overhead master, a side-angle hands-only master, macro inserts of each evidence object, a locked readable insert of the fictional provenance card, and a clean final frame. Alternatives are a no-hand version using only paper movement, a caption-free proof version, and a text-only version for rights or privacy constraints. Do not substitute a screenshot or fabricated UI for the paper prop.

## Sound motif and audio plan

Use one cleared narrator family, one restrained pencil-on-paper texture, one neutral paper placement sound, and a single soft boundary tick when the thread stops. No music unless original and cleared. Record room tone before and after each take; reject mixed voice families, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, or effects used to conceal defects.
