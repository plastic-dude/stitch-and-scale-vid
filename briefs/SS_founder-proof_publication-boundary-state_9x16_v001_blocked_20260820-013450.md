## Treatment

The film opens with a `RESOLVED` issue card beside an output envelope labeled `SAMPLE-PACK`. A hand reaches to seal the envelope. The motion stops as the narrator asks what exactly is being released. Cards are added for output ID, version, included components, open exclusions, approvals, rights, platform profile, disposition, and transfer scope.

The envelope is not sealed. It reads `VERSION: V001`, `OPEN: PRINT + TEST KNIT`, `APPROVALS: PENDING`, `RIGHTS: PENDING`, `DESTINATION: UNSET`, and `DISPOSITION: HOLD`. The final image narrows the release claim to an explicit hold rather than showing approval. All cards are original props and must not resemble current Stitch & Scale UI.
