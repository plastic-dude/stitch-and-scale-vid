## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Design |
|---|---|
| Subject | A full evidence envelope containing a swatch note, review mark, and test observation, but no qualification boundary. |
| Desire | Know what exact decision the evidence is allowed to support before passing it to the next role. |
| Obstacle | Evidence is present, but item, condition, method/source, reviewer, limitation, and permitted decision are blank or ambiguous. |
| Stakes | The next person may generalize evidence beyond its conditions; the film must not claim a specific defect or outcome. |
| Change | The envelope receives an explicit qualification card naming item, condition, method, reviewer, limitation, supported decision, review, and disposition. |
| Contradiction | More evidence in a folder does not automatically make the evidence suitable for every decision. |
| Escalation | Full folder → blank qualification fields → overbroad decision request → bounded evidence card. |
| Final image | A neutral card reading `SUPPORTS: CHART READABILITY REVIEW FOR THIS SAMPLE / NOT: ALL SIZES OR ALL PRINT OUTPUT`. |

## Treatment

The film opens on a sealed neutral evidence envelope labeled `EVIDENCE`. A hand places it beside a card reading `DECISION: READY?`. The envelope is full: a swatch note, a sample detail, a review mark, and a test observation are visible. The narrator asks whether presence is the same as qualification.

The envelope is opened and its contents are separated. Each piece receives a small label, but the proposed decision is too broad: `SUPPORTS ALL SIZES`. A red line is not used as a decorative warning; instead, the label is crossed out and replaced with a bounded card naming the item, condition, method/source, reviewer, limitation, supported decision, review, and disposition. The final decision is narrower and honest: `SUPPORTS CHART READABILITY REVIEW FOR THIS SAMPLE`.
