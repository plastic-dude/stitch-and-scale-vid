## Research-informed premise

The Craft Yarn Council’s current project guidance requires precise yarn type, put-up, yardage and weight, fiber content, quantities by size, units, measurements, sample size, direction, stitch totals, charts, and schematics. It also asks designers to reconfirm yarn, color, dye lot, and sample context. [1] Its Designer Pitfalls page specifically calls vague phrases such as “a small amount of yarn” a failure and separately flags missing sizes, units, counts, abbreviations, charts, schematics, and math. [2] Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 submission guidance separates pattern notes, sizes, charts, schematics, rights, technical editing, test knitting, and publication. [3]

The Department of the Interior and Digital.gov describe plain language as communication the public can read, understand, and use, supported by writing, design, and testing for a specific audience. [4] [5] W3C WCAG 2.1 treats labels, instructions, information relationships, text alternatives, captions, and human evaluation as parts of accessible content, while noting that high conformance does not address every user need. [6] These sources support qualifier visibility, not a comprehension or product capability claim.

## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | Fictional value card: `YARN: 400g`. |
| Desire | Let a designer or reader use the value without overreading its scope. |
| Obstacle | A bare value hides whether it is required, optional, estimated, conditional, unknown, or not applicable. |
| Stakes | A viewer may treat a scoped or estimated value as universal; no actual loss or outcome is claimed. |
| Change | The value gains qualifier, scope, source, date, and limitation cards. |
| Contradiction | A filled field can still be semantically incomplete. |
| Escalation | Bare value → qualifier missing → scope/stage cards → five qualifier states → human check and limitation. |
| Final image | `YARN: 400g / ESTIMATE / SIZE 4 / PROJECT-SPECIFIC / SOURCE UNCONFIRMED` beside `HUMAN CHECK REQUIRED`. |

## Treatment, beats, and visual grammar

Use one unbranded tabletop, one value card, and a restrained line of qualifier cards. Open on `YARN: 400g` with `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. The number looks authoritative until the qualifier cards enter: `ESTIMATE`, `FOR SIZE 4`, `PROJECT-SPECIFIC`, `SOURCE UNCONFIRMED`, `CHECK GAUGE`. The card then splits into `REQUIRED`, `OPTIONAL`, `UNKNOWN`, and `NOT APPLICABLE`; none becomes green or approved.

| Time | Beat | Image and action | Spoken idea | Evidence status |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5s | Bare number | `YARN: 400g` card. | “A number without its qualifier is an unfinished instruction.” | General principle; illustrative prop. |
| 5–12s | Missing meaning | Qualifier slot blinks/appears empty; no fake UI. | “Four hundred grams for what size, which stage, and under which conditions?” | Scope question; no product claim. |
| 12–20s | Scope attaches | `SIZE 4`, `PROJECT-SPECIFIC`, `SOURCE`, `AS OF`. | “Keep the scope, source, and date beside the value.” | Craft workflow synthesis. |
| 20–29s | Qualifier split | `REQUIRED`, `OPTIONAL`, `ESTIMATE`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT APPLICABLE`. | “Required is not optional. Estimate is not a measured fact. Unknown is not zero.” | Bounded semantic distinction. |
| 29–37s | Human gates | `GAUGE`, `MATH`, `TECHNICAL EDITING`, `PRINT REVIEW`, `TEST KNITTING`. | “The qualifier helps you ask the right question; it does not do the human check.” | Keep evidence gates distinct. |
