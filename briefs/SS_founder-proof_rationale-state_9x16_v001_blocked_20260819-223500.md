## Concept and claim boundary

> “Explained is not justified.”

Two fictional decision cards describe one craft record. A provenance strip shows where the record came from, but the decision path lacks criteria, assumptions, alternatives, tradeoff, and an explicit reason. The card becomes bounded only when question, evidence, criteria, assumptions, alternatives, tradeoff, reason, limit, reviewer, and disposition are linked. The ending is a decision-logic lesson, not a product promise, recommendation, correctness claim, risk-management certification, authorization, or approval claim. The closing line is **“Name why this decision follows.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes rationale templates, evidence-to-decision links, criteria, alternative comparison, tradeoff notes, or decision explanations. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

Arrows connect question, evidence, criteria, assumptions, alternatives, tradeoff, reason, limit, reviewer, and disposition. The final state remains `HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence board, two decision cards, a separated provenance strip, a rationale bridge with arrows from evidence to criteria to reason to decision, amber for missing rationale links, red only for `RATIONALE INCOMPLETE`, and no green approval state. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, product claim, or reviewer identity. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers decision, question, evidence, criteria, assumptions, alternatives, tradeoff, reason, limit, reviewer, disposition, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; two-card side-by-side; provenance strip insert; question/evidence/criteria close hold; assumptions/alternatives/tradeoff hold; reason-arrow close hold; limit/reviewer/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static rationale bridge with an explicit missing-link callout if motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unreadable proof, and unsupported claims rather than hiding defects with effects.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material. Arrangement is original abstract cards, provenance strip, cleared type, a cleared narrator, and original or cleared sound. No final asset is approved. Sound motif: one card placement per decision, a restrained link tone when evidence connects to criteria, silence at `RATIONALE INCOMPLETE`, and no alarm, urgency cue, or unlicensed music.
