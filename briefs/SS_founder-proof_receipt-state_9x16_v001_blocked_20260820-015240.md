## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or tester. He may explain why a sender should distinguish a notice created or delivery attempted from a recipient-confirmed receipt, acknowledgement, comprehension check, action, and residual limitation. ## Treatment

The film opens with an original notice card labeled `N-001 / V002 CHANGE` moving from `CREATED` to `DELIVERY ATTEMPTED`. A hand reaches to stamp `RECEIVED`, then stops before contact. The narrator asks what evidence supports the next state.

Cards are added for recipient scope, received time, acknowledgement, comprehension check, action, unconfirmed limitation, and disposition. The receipt track remains deliberately incomplete: `DELIVERY_STATE: ATTEMPTED`, `RECEIVED_AT: UNCONFIRMED`, `ACKNOWLEDGED_BY: UNCONFIRMED`, `COMPREHENSION_CHECK: PENDING`, `ACTION: REVIEW`, `UNCONFIRMED_LIMITATION: DELIVERY/READ STATE UNKNOWN`, and `DISPOSITION: HOLD`. The film does not claim that a notice was delivered, read, understood, or acted on by Stitch & Scale.
