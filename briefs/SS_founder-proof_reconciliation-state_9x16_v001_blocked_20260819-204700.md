## Concept and distinctness

Two populated records can appear aligned without a documented comparison or discrepancy decision. The nearest prior angle is `comparison-basis`, which concerns whether a comparison has a valid basis. `reconciliation-state` changes the pain to the actual comparison, discrepancy decision, responsibility, and follow-up between two populated records or outputs. The hook becomes “Aligned is not reconciled.” The visual metaphor changes from choosing a comparable basis to two records entering a comparison lane where differences surface and receive a decision. The proof surface is `LEFT / RIGHT / COMPARED AT / DIFFERENCE / DECISION / OWNER / FOLLOW-UP / STATUS`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from invalid-comparison risk to explicit agreement or bounded discrepancy. The final line is “Name how the records agree.” It does not recreate `comparison-basis`, `authority-state`, `absence-state`, `dependency-state`, `provenance-chain`, `override-state`, or `actionability-state`.
