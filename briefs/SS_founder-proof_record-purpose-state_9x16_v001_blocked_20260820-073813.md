## Research-informed premise

Craft Yarn Council’s submission checklist separates before-project notes, actual project, gauge swatch, instructions, schematic, invoice, return shipping, labels, and yarn amounts. It asks designers to keep notes and not rely on memory, and it treats the sample size, finished measurements, charts, and schematic as distinct submission information. [1] CYC’s idea guidance separates a design idea, a swatch/sketch/sample, a completed project, acceptance, contract, and review process. [2] Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 guidance similarly requires individual files and distinguishes pattern text, images, charts, schematics, statements, and rights concerns. [3]

Atlassian’s current single-source-of-truth guidance warns that multiple unclear locations produce duplication, conflicting information, and outdated files; it recommends purpose mapping, update responsibility, and consistent templates. [4] This is category context only, not evidence of superiority or a Stitch & Scale capability.

These sources support a bounded record-design distinction: **a record may be stored in one place and still be misread if its purpose, stage, source, owner, and limits are unclear**. The film must not claim that one ledger automatically becomes authoritative or that a purpose field verifies the underlying facts.

## Treatment and beats

It first reads `PURPOSE: UNKNOWN`, then shows `DESIGN NOTE`, `SUBMISSION`, `TEST KNIT`, and `PUBLICATION REVIEW` as mutually exclusive example purposes. Once `PURPOSE: SUBMISSION` is selected, `WORKFLOW_STAGE`, `SOURCE_RECORD_ID`, `RECORDED_FACTS`, `DERIVED_VALUES`, `NEXT_DECISION`, `DECISION_OWNER`, and `LIMITATIONS` appear. Separate cards keep calculation, technical editing, print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, test knitting, and publication approval visible. The final state remains `RECORD PURPOSE: UNVERIFIED` because no product proof is supplied.

## Visual grammar and production bible

Use one neutral tabletop, diffuse side light, restrained camera movement, and high-contrast original cards. The record card must be abstract and contain no real project title, invoice, customer, sample, private message, pattern, publication, logo, or current UI. Every card must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

Keep `RECORD_PURPOSE`, `WORKFLOW_STAGE`, `SOURCE_RECORD_ID`, `RECORDED_FACTS`, `DERIVED_VALUES`, `ATTACHMENTS`, `DECISION_OWNER`, `NEXT_DECISION`, `LIMITATIONS`, `REVIEWER`, and `STATUS` as separate field families. Do not show a green `AUTHORITATIVE`, `APPROVED`, `TAX READY`, or `DONE` badge. Do not imply that a central record proves the underlying fact or that a purpose label replaces review. Generated text is not UI truth.

Lock card orientation, typography, tabletop, hand direction, narrator family, room tone, and light temperature across takes. David may appear in a neutral developer frame or remain voice-only; he must not perform knitting or be framed as a craft practitioner. Technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, test knitting, and publication approval remain distinct human evidence gates.
