## Concept and claim boundary

> “Related is not relevant.”

One fictional decision card asks whether to modify a craft piece. A related measurement or warning appears with a dotted connector that fails to land because the present subject, context, intended use, impact, criteria, and limits are missing. The connector becomes solid only after a relevance basis is named. The ending is a bounded decision-context lesson, not a fit promise, recommendation, risk score, or product claim. The closing line is **“Name why this evidence belongs to this decision.”**

This package does not claim that Stitch & Scale exposes a relevance score, risk engine, decision authorization, context matching, impact analysis, criteria engine, or automated recommendation. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, controls, warnings, numbers, and behavior require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry.

David may be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted. He must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, tester, or as using an unverified feature.

## Treatment and beat sheet

The treatment is an abstract decision-context lesson. A neutral board holds a decision card labeled `MODIFY THIS PIECE?`. A related evidence card labeled `MEASUREMENT` approaches on a dotted connector, but the connector stops at `CONTEXT MISSING`. The decision card expands with subject, context, intended use, impact, criteria, relevance basis, limit, owner, and disposition. The final state remains `REQUIRES CONTEXT` rather than approved.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use a neutral evidence board, one decision card, one related-evidence card, a dotted connector that fails to land, and a solid connector only after context fields are named. Amber marks missing basis; red is reserved for the mismatch; no green approval state. No real UI, private records, recognizable people, copyrighted patterns, customer data, branded property, legal language, product claim, or fit promise. Captions occupy a fixed lane that never covers decision, subject, context, criteria, evidence, limits, tables, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, hands, or primary action.

Coverage: locked overhead master; medium decision-card push-in; close readable proof holds for mismatch and context fields; connector insert; criteria/relevance-basis hold; owner/disposition hold; caption-free clean master; caption-on review master; accessibility review frame. Alternative coverage is a static decision/evidence comparison if connector motion reduces legibility. Reject stale UI, caption collisions, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unreadable proof, and unsupported claims rather than hiding defects with effects.

## Locations, arrangements, sound motif, and approved-assets plan

Location is a controlled desk or studio surface with no recognizable private material. Arrangement is original abstract cards, dotted and solid connectors, cleared type, a cleared narrator, and original or cleared sound. No final asset is approved. Sound motif: a soft approach sound for related evidence, silence at `CONTEXT MISSING`, and one quiet connector tone when relevance basis is named. No alarm, urgency cue, recommendation cue, or unlicensed music.
