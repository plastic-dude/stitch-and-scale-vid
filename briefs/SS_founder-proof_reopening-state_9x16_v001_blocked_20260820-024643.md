## Research-informed distinction

Pass 199 isolated an uncertain or nonconforming record from unintended reuse while preserving reason, evidence, correction, scope, owner, and review state. Pass 200 moves to the transition out of hold: a correction action is not itself clearance. NIST’s Corrective Action Plan definition links corrective actions to removing or reducing deficiencies and obtaining or sustaining authorization. GOV.UK guidance preserves failing inputs and explains what went wrong and how to fix it. Craft Yarn Council guidance keeps project, gauge, instructions, measurements, charts, schematic, notes, mathematics, and recipient-specific checks distinct. [1] [2] [3]

> **Fixed is not the same as reviewed, and reviewed is not the same as cleared for this scope.**

These sources inform general workflow education only. They do not establish that Stitch & Scale currently exposes corrective-action plans, re-review workflows, clearance controls, reopening permissions, or release automation.

## Nearest prior and concept difference

| Element | Pass 199 `quarantine-state` | Pass 200 `reopening-state` |
|---|---|---|
| Primary pain | An uncertain or nonconforming record remains available for unintended reuse | A corrected held record is treated as cleared without reviewed evidence or defined scope |
| Hook | “Do not erase the uncertain record. Isolate it before it travels.” | “Fixed is not the same as reviewed, and reviewed is not the same as cleared for this scope.” |
| Visual metaphor | A record moves into a marked hold tray with its reason and correction path attached | A held record receives a correction tag, pauses at a review desk, and leaves only with a scoped disposition |
| Proof surface | `QUAR A-001 / RECORD_ID / REASON / EVIDENCE / CORRECTION / REUSE_SCOPE / REVIEW_OWNER / REVIEW_STATUS / DISPOSITION` | `REOPEN A-001 / RECORD_ID / ORIGINAL_REASON / CORRECTION_EVIDENCE / REVIEW_RESULT / REVIEW_OWNER / REUSE_SCOPE / HUMAN_GATE_STATUS / DISPOSITION` |
| Emotional turn | Relief through controlled incompleteness | Disciplined relief through evidence-based clearance rather than a premature reopening |
| Final line | “Keep the reason with the record, keep it out of reuse, and name what reopens it.” | “Show the correction, the reviewer, the scope, and the evidence that lets it reopen.” |

## Treatment and beats

The film opens on a neutral tabletop. A record sits in a tray marked `HOLD — DO NOT REUSE — ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. A correction card is placed beside it: `CORRECTION PERFORMED`. A hand reaches toward a large neutral `CLEARED FOR REUSE` stamp, but the stamp remains unused.

The record moves to a separate review desk. Cards appear for `ORIGINAL_REASON`, `CORRECTION_EVIDENCE`, `REVIEW_RESULT`, `REVIEW_OWNER`, `REUSE_SCOPE`, and the distinct human gates. The review result, owner, and scope remain incomplete. The final frame keeps the corrected record visible, but its disposition remains `HOLD`.
