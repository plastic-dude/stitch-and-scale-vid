## Concept and claim boundary

> “Worked once is not independently replicable.”

A fictional author result card hands an exercise kit to an independent replicator. The kit exposes inputs, steps, environment, expected output, actual output, delta, replicator, changed conditions, reviewer, and disposition. The independent run cannot be called a match until its differences and conditions are visible. The story is about a **declared replication package**, not product reproducibility, replicability, complete instructions, artifact badges, external validation, gauge, fit, or correctness.

NIST TN 1297 distinguishes repeatability under the same conditions from reproducibility under changed conditions and says a valid reproducibility statement specifies what conditions changed [909]. ACM's artifact-review guidance distinguishes repeatability, reproducibility, and replicability and describes artifact qualities such as documented, consistent, complete, and exercisable alongside independent review [910]. Craft Yarn Council recommends reading the full relevant instructions, performing them, and using specified materials and gauge swatches before beginning [911]. These sources motivate the fictional exercise kit and production review only; none proves that Stitch & Scale implements reproducibility controls, has artifact badges, provides complete instructions, checks gauge, or guarantees fit.

All inputs, steps, environments, expected outputs, actual outputs, deltas, replicator names, changed conditions, reviewers, and dispositions are fictional. Generated text is not UI truth. Exact product labels, values, warnings, and controls require a current approved product capture or proof-registry entry. David is a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester.

## Visual grammar and coverage plan

Use original abstract author and replicator cards, a sealed exercise kit, expected/actual output cards, a visible delta connector, and a neutral evidence board. Amber marks undefined or unverified exercise fields. Red marks only `REPLICATION INCOMPLETE`. There is no green reproduced, replicated, complete, validated, fit, accurate, or approved state. Captions occupy a fixed lane away from `REPLICATION`, `INPUTS`, `STEPS`, `ENVIRONMENT`, `EXPECTED_OUTPUT`, `ACTUAL_OUTPUT`, `DELTA`, `REPLICATOR`, `CONDITIONS_CHANGED`, `REVIEWED`, and `DISPOSITION`, tables, numbers, labels, buttons, menus, hands, and primary actions.
