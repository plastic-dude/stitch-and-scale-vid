## Dramatic engine

| Engine element | Design |
|---|---|
| Subject | A reply card that says `ACKNOWLEDGED` beside an unresolved craft-production issue. |
| Desire | Know whether the response actually closes the issue or safely transfers the next action. |
| Obstacle | A response arrived, but the issue, decision, reason/correction, affected artifact, next action, and closure state are absent or ambiguous. |
| Stakes | A team may mistake receipt for resolution and continue with an unclear gate; the film must not claim a specific defect or outcome. |
| Change | The response is converted into a bounded resolution card that names what changed, why, what it affects, who owns next, and whether the issue is closed. |
| Contradiction | Communication can increase activity without reducing uncertainty. |
| Escalation | `ACKNOWLEDGED` → blank issue → vague “looks good” → explicit decision/reason/correction → next action and closure state. |
| Final image | A neutral card reading `DECISION: HOLD / REASON: CHART REVIEW PENDING / AFFECTED: SAMPLE A / NEXT: TECH EDIT / CLOSED: NO`. |

## Treatment

The film opens with an original reply card reading `ACKNOWLEDGED — THANKS`. It is placed beside a request card for `CHART + SWATCH REVIEW`. The narrator asks whether the reply resolved anything. A second hand moves the card toward a `DONE` tray, but the move stops because no issue, decision, reason, affected artifact, or next action is named.

The reply is rebuilt as a resolution record. Cards identify `REQUEST R-001`, `ISSUE: CHART SYMBOLS`, `DECISION: HOLD`, `REASON: REVIEW PENDING`, `AFFECTED: SAMPLE A`, `OWNER: TECHNICAL REVIEW`, `NEXT: CHECK SYMBOLS`, and `CLOSED: NO`. The final frame refuses to invent approval and keeps the issue open with a clear next action. These original paper cards must not resemble current Stitch & Scale UI.
