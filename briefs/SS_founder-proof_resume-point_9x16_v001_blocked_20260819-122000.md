## Campaign assumption

No active human brief was present in the refreshed repository. This run assumes founding-tester recruitment through the free demo and early-access cohort remains the campaign objective. The film is a year-round story about reconstructing context after a pause, not an automatic save or recovery claim. It invites designers to inspect one current approved resume-state surface if proof is supplied; it does not promise that Stitch & Scale provides that behavior.

## Research decision

Android says users expect UI state to remain when they temporarily switch apps or return after system-initiated interruption, and distinguishes saved state from local persistence.[218] CYC’s Knit 911 hub covers row starts, yarn changes, circular needles, dropped/twisted stitches, seams, and finishing but does not define a complete handoff record.[219] Human-factors research frames resumption as reconstruction of task context after interruption.[220] Stitch & Scale’s contracts keep records, tools, counts, images, source/date, technical review, print/chart review, and test knitting separate.[221] [222] These sources support a resume-state checklist, but do not prove Stitch & Scale captures or restores one.

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `hands-busy`, which focuses on working while hands are occupied. `resume-point` changes the pain to **reconstructing the active work state after a pause**. The hook changes to **“The hard part is finding your place again.”** The visual metaphor changes from hands-busy capture cues to **`ROW / ROUND / SIDE / REPEAT / MARKER / COUNT / TOOL / YARN / LAST CHECKED / PHOTO`**. The proof surface changes from a hands-occupied note to a current resume-state surface with source/date and status. The emotional turn changes from reducing friction during work to leaving a usable handoff for the future self. The final line changes to **“Leave yourself a way back.”**

It is distinct from `first-record` because it is resumption after interruption rather than first entry; from `count-check` because it preserves context around a count rather than checking a count; from `context-lock` because it is a future-self handoff rather than an active-context lock; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or evidence-roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.

## Treatment and production bible

Open on a neutral tabletop with an original abstract card marked `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. A fictional handoff card assembles `ROW / ROUND`, `SIDE / REPEAT`, `MARKER / COUNT`, `TOOL / YARN`, and `LAST CHECKED / PHOTO`. The hand pauses, then places the card beside the project. The end card reads `RESUME POINT VISIBLE — REVIEW OPEN`.

A real Stitch & Scale resume-state surface may replace the fictional card only if a current, approved, non-private capture or proof-registry entry verifies the exact surface, labels, product version/date, and rights to show it. If proof is absent, retain the blocker card and disclaimer. No automatic save, restore, active-position detection, or mistake-prevention claim is permitted.

Use one narrator family. David may appear only with authorization and remains described as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Captions must not cover resume fields, numbers, proof, tables, buttons, menus, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions. Generated text remains illustrative and cannot substitute for current product proof.

## Caption map and audio plan

Use one narrator family with close, dry narration. Sound motif: a soft pencil tick on `ROW`, a marker tap on `MARKER`, a quiet camera shutter on `PHOTO`, and a chair shift at the pause. No resume-guarantee chime, saved stamp, or mistake-free cue. Check for voice drift, doubled words, overlaps, accent changes, inconsistent mic distance, and room-tone jumps.

Captions are narration-verbatim, sentence by sentence, inside a declared safe zone that never covers resume fields, numbers, proof, tables, or CTA. Keep one context family on screen at a time for mobile reading. Retain caption-on and caption-free review masters. Automatic captions are not accepted as final. TikTok and Instagram/Reels controls and overlays remain approval-time validation gates.
