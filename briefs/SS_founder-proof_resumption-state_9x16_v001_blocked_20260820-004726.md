## Treatment

The film opens on a table with a row of original cards: `ROW 9`, `ROW 10`, `ROW 11`, `ROW 12`, `ROW 13`. A neutral bookmark sits between `ROW 11` and `ROW 12`, but the saved card says only `SAVED`. The narrator asks whether saved is the same as ready to resume. A second card marked `UNRESOLVED` is partly hidden beneath the bookmark.

The table is reset into a return contract. One card states `LAST KNOWN STEP: ROW 11`, another states `SAVED AT: 18:42`, a third states `UNSAVED WORK: NONE CONFIRMED`, and a fourth states `SEQUENCE: CHECK COUNT BEFORE ROW 12`. The final action card reads `RETURN: ROW 12 / CHECK COUNT / REVIEW PENDING`. These are illustrative paper props and must not resemble current Stitch & Scale UI.
