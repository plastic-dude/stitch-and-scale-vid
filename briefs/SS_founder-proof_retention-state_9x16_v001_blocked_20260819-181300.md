## Concept and distinctness

Stored material is not automatically retained correctly, indefinitely, privately, recoverably, deletably, or securely sanitized. The nearest prior angle is `withdrawal-state`, which focuses on the downstream impact of a permission withdrawal. `retention-state` changes the pain to stored material being treated as either permanent by default or safely deleted without a purpose, basis, review, access, disposal, or exception record. The hook becomes “Stored is not retained correctly.” The visual metaphor changes from a downstream-impact map to a retention card with a purpose-to-review timeline. The proof surface is `RECORD / PURPOSE / RETENTION BASIS / KEEP UNTIL / REVIEW AT / ACCESS SCOPE / DISPOSAL METHOD / EXCEPTION / OWNER / STATUS`. The emotional turn is from confronting changed permission to making continued storage answerable. The final line is “Name why it stays before you call it retained.” It does not recreate withdrawal-state, consent-state, rights-scope, review-expiry, capture-freshness, deletion-state, erasure-state, drift-state, provenance-chain, false completion, release roster, or a human release checklist.
