## Concept and distinctness

A record may be dated yet lack a policy or evidence basis for when it should be reviewed again. The nearest prior angle is `drift-state`, which asks whether a stable-looking state has a baseline, current observation, comparison context, and review period. `review-expiry` changes the pain to a review obligation being hidden or collapsed into a universal current/expired label, even though the review window needs a stated basis. The hook becomes “Not reviewed is not current.” The visual metaphor changes from a stale status card to a review-window card with `IN WINDOW / DUE / OVERDUE / EXTENDED` states. The proof surface is `REVIEW WINDOW / BASIS FOR WINDOW / LAST REVIEW / DUE BY / OVERDUE? / EXTENSION RATIONALE / NEXT ACTION / STATUS`. The emotional turn is from detecting drift to making review obligations explicit. The final line is “Name the review window before you call it current.” It does not recreate drift-state, capture-freshness, outcome-proof, comparison-basis, response-state, risk-trigger, failure-mode, false completion, release roster, or a human release checklist.
