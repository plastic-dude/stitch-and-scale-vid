## Treatment

The film opens on a small paper card marked `CHANGE B-001` beside a neutral source card. The change is visually tiny: one symbol or one number on an original illustrative instruction card. A hand begins sliding it down a paper thread toward `CHART`, `PRINT`, `SCHEMATIC`, and `TEST KNIT` cards. The narrator stops the movement before the change reaches the thread’s end.

The thread is rebuilt with a visible correction boundary. Cards name `PROPOSED CHANGE`, `AFFECTED OUTPUTS`, `AFFECTED GATES`, `REVIEW REQUIRED`, `REVERSIBLE OR AMENDABLE`, `OWNER`, and `NEXT CHECK`. The change remains proposed, not completed. The final frame shows a human review stop and a bounded next check. These are original paper props and must not resemble current Stitch & Scale UI.
