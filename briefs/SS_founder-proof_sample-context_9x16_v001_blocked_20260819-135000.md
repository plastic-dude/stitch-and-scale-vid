## Research decision

CYC advises confirming the sample garment size required to fit the photography model, reconfirming yarn/color, checking dye lots, keeping project notes, and sizing the project against standards.[256] CYC also distinguishes actual body measurements from completed-garment sizing and schematics and explains ease as a relationship between body and garment measurements.[257] Stitch & Scale’s contracts keep sample, gauge, yarn, model/size context, measurements, source/date, technical editing, print/chart review, and test knitting separate.[258] [259] These sources support a sample-context checklist, but do not prove Stitch & Scale manages or interprets samples.

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `fit-intent`, which focuses on what kind of fit a designer intends. `sample-context` changes the pain to **whether a maker knows what a photographed or described sample can—and cannot—prove**. The hook changes to **“The sample is one size, not every size.”** The visual metaphor changes from a fit-intent label to **`SAMPLE SIZE / MODEL CONTEXT / BODY MEASUREMENTS / FINISHED MEASUREMENTS / YARN / GAUGE / PHOTO REFERENCE / SOURCE / DATE / STATUS`**. The proof surface changes from intended-fit wording to a current sample-context surface. The emotional turn changes from naming an intended silhouette to turning a beautiful sample into an honest reference rather than an unmarked fit promise. The final line changes to **“Label the sample before you trust the picture.”**

It is distinct from `measurement-provenance` because it governs the interpretive context of a sample rather than the lineage of a number; from `before-download` because it does not present marketplace metadata; from `gauge-state` because it does not record swatch state; from `material-trace` because it does not trace the actual yarn lot; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or evidence-roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.

## Treatment and production bible

Open on a neutral tabletop with an original abstract card marked `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. A fictional sample begins as `SAMPLE SIZE`, gains `MODEL CONTEXT`, then `BODY MEASUREMENTS / FINISHED MEASUREMENTS`, then `YARN / GAUGE / PHOTO REFERENCE / SOURCE / DATE / STATUS`. The end card reads `SAMPLE CONTEXT VISIBLE — REVIEW OPEN`.

A real Stitch & Scale sample-context surface may replace the fictional card only if a current, approved, non-private capture or proof-registry entry verifies the exact surface, labels, product version/date, and rights to show it. If proof is absent, retain the blocker card and disclaimer. No sample management, model matching, photo-based fit inference, size-inclusion, or sample-to-pattern validation claim is permitted.

Use one narrator family. David may appear only with authorization and remains described as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Captions must not cover sample fields, numbers, proof, tables, faces, model images, buttons, menus, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions. Generated text remains illustrative and cannot substitute for current product proof.

## Caption map and audio plan

Use one narrator family with close, dry narration. Sound motif: a camera shutter substitute on `SAMPLE SIZE`, a paper measure on `BODY MEASUREMENTS`, a yarn pull on `YARN / GAUGE`, and a held silence before `STATUS`. No fit-result chime, model-match stamp, or outcome-guarantee bed. Check for voice drift, doubled words, overlaps, accent changes, inconsistent mic distance, and room-tone jumps.

Captions are narration-verbatim, sentence by sentence, inside a declared safe zone that never covers sample fields, numbers, proof, faces, model silhouettes, tables, or CTA. Keep one sample-context family on screen at a time for mobile reading. Retain caption-on and caption-free review masters. Automatic captions are not accepted as final. TikTok and Instagram/Reels controls and overlays remain approval-time validation gates.
