## Concept, distinctness, and research boundary

The same result can carry a different meaning in a different situation. The nearest prior angles are `reproducibility-state` and `fitness-state`, which concern rerun inspectability and intended-use suitability. `scenario-state` asks whether cross-scenario reuse is supported by named scenario, environment, conditions, inputs, workflow, reference, transfer limits, review, and decision. Hook: “Same result is not same situation.” Fictional proof surface: `RESULT / SCENARIO / ENVIRONMENT / CONDITIONS / INPUTS / WORKFLOW / REFERENCE / TRANSFER LIMIT / REVIEWED / DECISION`. Emotional turn: “The number is the same” becomes “The situation is not the same.” Final line: “Name the situation before you reuse the result.” Distinct from `calibration-state`, `context-state`, `portability-state`, `sampling-state`, and `sensitivity-state`.

W3C PROV-DM frames provenance through entities, activities, agents, generation, use, derivation, attribution, and time. NIST explains that traceability requires a documented unbroken reference chain and explicitly says traceability alone does not guarantee fitness for purpose. Craft Yarn Council gauge guidance supplies practical pattern-specified and swatch context. These sources support workflow education only; they do not prove that Stitch & Scale currently tracks scenarios, environments, transfer limits, or cross-scenario validity.
