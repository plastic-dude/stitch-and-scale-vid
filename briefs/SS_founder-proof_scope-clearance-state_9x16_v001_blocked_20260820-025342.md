## Research-informed distinction

Pass 200 separated correction, review, and clearance. Pass 201 narrows the next boundary: even a reviewed and cleared record may be authorized only for a specific entity, purpose, or scope. NIST’s authorization glossary includes permission for a specific entity or resource and verification that a requested action is approved for a specific entity. GOV.UK’s check-answers pattern places scoped review before confirmation, shows relevant sections, and lets users change specific answers. Craft Yarn Council guidance distinguishes company-specific requirements, project components, and recipient-specific checks. [1] [2] [3] [4]

> **Cleared for one use is not cleared for every use.**

These sources inform general workflow education only. They do not establish that Stitch & Scale currently exposes scoped permissions, access control, authorization records, role-based clearance, or release profiles.

## Founder and product truth

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester. He may explain why a reviewed record should state exactly which entity, purpose, and human gates its clearance covers, while leaving other uses outside the decision. Calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

Every card is an original prop labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Do not invent current labels, controls, warnings, permissions, automation, validation, suitability, usability, project-level classification, accuracy, outcomes, adoption, or capabilities.

## Treatment and beats

The film opens on a neutral tabletop. A reviewed record sits beside a large neutral `CLEARED` stamp. Around it are four blank lanes labeled `PRINT`, `CHART READABILITY`, `TEST KNIT`, and `OTHER SIZES`. The stamp does not move until the scope card appears.

The record is narrowed to an illustrative entity and purpose: `AUTHORIZED_ENTITY: INTERNAL REVIEW TEAM` and `AUTHORIZED_PURPOSE: FOLLOW CHART 3 — SIZE M`. The included gate is `CALCULATION CHECK ONLY`; other human gates remain `NOT INCLUDED`. The clearance lane is bright only within its narrow boundary. The final disposition keeps human confirmation visible and refuses universal release.
