## Concept and distinctness

A size, gauge, scenario, or variant can look selected without proving what downstream result it controls. The nearest prior angle is `dependency-state`, which concerns stale downstream outputs after an upstream change. `selection-state` changes the pain to an unverified selected context. The hook becomes “Selected is not applied.” The visual metaphor changes from an upstream ripple to a selected chip connecting to an applied-context path. The proof surface is `SELECTED / APPLIED TO / CURRENT CONTEXT / DOWNSTREAM VIEWS / LAST UPDATED / CONFIRMED? / EXPORT USE`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from hidden ripple to accountable context. The final line is “Name what the selection changes.” It does not recreate `dependency-state`, `input-state`, `authority-state`, `unit-state`, `tolerance-state`, `applicability-scope`, `commit-state`, `context-state`, imported `false-completion`, or imported `release-gate-roster`.
