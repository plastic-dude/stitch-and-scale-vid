## Concept, distinctness, and research boundary

A list can be ordered visually without proving that the order controls meaning or permission. The nearest prior angle is `dependency-state`, which concerns downstream staleness after upstream change. `sequence-state` asks whether prerequisites and review gates establish what had to happen first and what may happen next. Hook: “Ordered is not sequenced.” Fictional proof surface: `STEP / POSITION / PREREQUISITE / INPUT / OUTPUT / REVIEW GATE / NEXT ALLOWED / BLOCKED BY / RECORDED`. Emotional turn: “Everything is checked” becomes “The order still matters.” Final line: “Name what had to happen first.” Distinct from `selection-state`, `priority-state`, `reproducibility-state`, `audit-state`, and `actionability-state`.

W3C PROV-O represents provenance through usage, generation, activities, entities, agents, and context, which can describe relationships across systems. Craft Yarn Council guidance separates pattern outline/design, standards and best practices, and technical editing before public release. These sources support workflow education only; they do not prove that Stitch & Scale currently records or enforces prerequisite-aware sequences.
