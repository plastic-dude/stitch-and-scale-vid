## Research decision

Craft Yarn Council’s current yarn-label guidance describes standardized yarn-weight and skill-level symbols, while its designer guidance asks designers to reconfirm yarn and color selection, check ball bands, keep work available for reference, and record project details in its submission context. These materials describe standards and designer practice; they do not prove that Stitch & Scale verifies sources, material content, standards compliance, or evidence status.

The research changed the concept from generic citation or fact-checking into **source-type transparency plus reconfirmation**. The film must not claim source verification, material truth, standards compliance, or fact-checking.

## Campaign assumption

No active human brief was present in the checked repository state. This run assumes founding-tester recruitment remains the working objective through the free demo and early-access cohort. The film invites designers to inspect one verified source-note, provenance, or evidence-status surface; it does not promise verification, traceability, or factual accuracy.

## Dramatic engine

| Element | Director decision |
|---|---|
| Subject | A note appears factual while its source type is hidden. |
| Desire | To know whether a statement was observed, reported, labeled, tested, or externally guided before repeating it. |
| Obstacle | Notes from different sources can collapse into one confident sentence. |
| Stakes | A designer may repeat an un-rechecked material or workflow statement as if it were verified fact. |
| Change | The viewer moves from “write the note” to “name the source and mark the recheck boundary.” |
| Contradiction | A source label improves honesty but does not prove the underlying statement. |
| Escalation | Confident note appears → source tabs split it → `RECONFIRM` remains → no verified seal → current proof or blocker. |
| Final image | A note card beside `OBSERVED / LABEL / TEST KNIT / EXTERNAL` and `RECONFIRM`. |

## Difference from prior concepts

The nearest prior angle is `material-notes`, which concerns preserving material assumptions and substitution rechecks. `source-note` changes the pain to **a record presenting a statement without showing where it came from or whether it was rechecked**; the hook to **“Before you call it a fact, name where it came from.”**; the visual metaphor from material cards to a note splitting into `OBSERVED / LABEL / TEST KNIT / EXTERNAL`; the proof surface from materials/gauge/substitution to a source-note, provenance, or evidence-status surface; the emotional turn from material rechecking to epistemic humility; and the final line to **“Name the source. Mark the recheck.”**

It is distinct from `material-notes` because it applies across note types and source classes rather than yarn substitution; from `evidence-over-output` because it does not compare generated language with physical proof; from `decision-ledger` because it does not classify recorded/derived/estimated values; from `feedback-loop` because it does not track response state; from `data-minimum` because it is not data collection scope; and from `care-context`, `sample-chain`, `technique-profile`, `support-boundary`, `brief-before-knit`, `measurement-moment`, `permission-line`, `recovery-proof`, `gauge-reality`, `readability-under-load`, `chart-parity`, `equivalent-meaning`, `event-window`, `inherited-thread`, `offline-continuity`, `correction-trace`, `locale-drift`, `reader-handoff`, `false-completion`, and `release-gate-roster` because the conflict is source attribution versus unmarked certainty. It does not reopen, overwrite, rename, or consume the imported reservations.

## Treatment, visual grammar, and production bible

Open on an original note card labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`. It contains a fictional statement such as `SAMPLE: 20 CM AFTER WASH?`, with no real measurement, material, person, or product detail. Four source tabs appear: `OBSERVED`, `LABEL`, `TEST KNIT`, and `EXTERNAL`. A final card reads `RECONFIRM`, not `VERIFIED`, `CERTIFIED`, or `FACT CHECKED`.

The product insert is optional and blocked until a current, versioned, non-private proof capture verifies exact source-note, provenance, evidence-status, or reconfirmation labels. If absent, retain `SOURCE PROOF REQUIRED — NOT PRODUCT UI`. Any real supplier label, test-knitter report, external standard, pattern page, personal note, or evidence record requires source and rights review. David may appear only with authorization and must be described only as a developer whose late mother knitted; he is not a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. Use one narrator family. Captions must not cover note text, source tabs, proof, hands, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free cuts.
