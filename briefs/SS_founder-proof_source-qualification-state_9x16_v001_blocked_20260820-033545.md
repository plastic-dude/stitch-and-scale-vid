## Research-informed distinction

Pass 206 exposed a source-to-activity-to-result lineage and a visible gap when the chain is incomplete. Pass 207 adds a qualification step before a source is allowed to support a decision: name the intended use, assess relevance, timeliness, validity, completeness, and accuracy as appropriate, record the method or basis for qualification, and keep the result bounded when evidence is not sufficient. GOV.UK says good quality data is fit for purpose and that different uses require different combinations of quality dimensions. NIST’s evidence-management guidance separates an item existing from its integrity and chain-of-custody condition in its forensic context. W3C WCAG-EM 2 bounds scope, sample, expertise, and reporting; Craft Yarn Council keeps instructions, gauge, and human activities distinct. [1] [2] [3] [4]

> **A source can be real and still not be enough for this decision.**

These sources inform general workflow education only. They do not establish that Stitch & Scale currently qualifies sources, scores evidence, validates source fitness, or automatically blocks unsupported decisions.

## Treatment and beats

The film opens on a neutral tabletop. A source card is identifiable and linked to a question card but stops at a marked qualification gate. The source does not enter the decision lane merely because it has a title or link.

Use, relevance, timeliness, validity, completeness, accuracy basis, method, sample coverage, source state, and integrity cards enter one at a time. A small sample remains labeled `NOT WHOLE PRODUCT`. The final status stays `HOLD` until the use-specific basis is sufficient and accountable review exists.
