## Research-informed premise

Craft Yarn Council guidance tells designers to reconfirm yarn and color selection with an editor or design director, check ball bands for the same dye lot, confirm availability and sample size, keep notes, discuss concerns or alternatives with the manufacturer or editor, and submit a gauge swatch that matches the project gauge. [1] Its yarn-weight page describes category gauges as guidelines and says to follow the gauge stated in the pattern. [2]

Knitty’s substitution guidance warns against substituting by gauge alone. It recommends considering fiber content, yarn structure, pattern behavior, row gauge, and a large substitution swatch. [3] Knitty’s submission guidance also says a yarn-characteristics descriptor can help readers substitute, asks designers to list an alternate if the original is discontinued, and keeps pattern, image, schematic, originality, and permission boundaries distinct. [4]

> **A substitute can fit the gauge and still need a record.**

## Treatment and beats

The film opens on a substitute card stamped `SAME GAUGE?` moving toward an `APPROVED` lane. Four amber questions interrupt it. A swatch card supports one fit observation, but does not merge with the confirmation card. A pattern-note card names where an alternate would be disclosed, while the reviewer card remains a separate human gate. The close refuses a product approval and names the evidence still needed.
