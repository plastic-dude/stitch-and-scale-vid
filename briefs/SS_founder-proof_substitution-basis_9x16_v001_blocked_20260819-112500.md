## Research decision

CYC presents weight categories, commonly used gauge ranges, and needle ranges, but calls them guidelines and says to follow the pattern’s stated gauge.[197] Lion Brand’s current guidance says substitution requires more than matching the word “worsted”: compare weight, gauge, fiber behavior, yardage, needle size, and swatch/blocking evidence.[198] Stitch & Scale’s contracts separate gauge, technical editing, test knitting, recorded values, and estimates.[199] [200] These sources support a comparison checklist, but do not prove Stitch & Scale recommends or validates yarn substitutes.

## Difference from nearest prior angle

The nearest prior angle is `care-path`, which focuses on evidence supporting washing or blocking. `substitution-basis` changes the pain to **whether a yarn swap has been compared across material and technical fields before commitment**. The hook changes to **“Same weight is not the same yarn.”** The visual metaphor changes from care evidence to **`ORIGINAL / SUBSTITUTE / FIBER / WEIGHT / GAUGE / YARDAGE / NEEDLE / CARE / SWATCH`**. The proof surface changes from a care/evidence surface to a substitution comparison with source/date and explicit review state. The emotional turn changes from pausing before care to replacing “similar” with a checklist. The final line changes to **“Compare the evidence before you change the yarn.”**

It is distinct from `fit-intent` because it is material substitution evidence rather than design ease; from `count-check` because it is comparison inputs rather than reconciliation; from `care-path` because care is one field among many before a yarn swap; and from imported `false-completion` and `release-gate-roster` because it is not completion or evidence-roster accountability. Neither imported concept is reopened, renamed, overwritten, or approved.
