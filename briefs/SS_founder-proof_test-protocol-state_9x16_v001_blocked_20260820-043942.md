## Research-informed premise

The National Academies’ reproducibility framework says that checking a result depends on visible inputs, methods, steps, and conditions, and recommends complete descriptions of methods, materials, procedures, measurements, variables, constraints, and uncertainty. It also warns that exact reproduction does not guarantee correctness. [1] [2]

Craft Yarn Council guidance gives the knitwear-specific boundary: reconfirm yarn and color, check dye lots and availability, confirm the requested sample size, keep notes, complete a gauge swatch in the project stitch, record stitch and row counts, and preserve instructions, materials, measurements, schematic, and separate submission responsibilities. [3] Its hook-and-needle guidance says to use metric sizing and compare a completed gauge swatch with the pattern gauge. [4]

> **A test result needs the conditions that make it readable.**
