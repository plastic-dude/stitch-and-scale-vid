## Concept and distinctness

A difference from target can be intentional ease, allowed tolerance, measurement uncertainty, rounding, or failure. The nearest prior angle is `input-state`, which concerns value origin at capture. `tolerance-state` changes the pain to an unexplained decision boundary. The hook becomes “Within range is not understood.” The visual metaphor changes from an origin branch to target/observed bands meeting at a decision boundary. The proof surface is `TARGET / OBSERVED / DIFFERENCE / EASE OR TOLERANCE / UNCERTAINTY / ROUNDING / DECISION / EXPLANATION`, explicitly illustrative until product proof exists. The emotional turn is from pass/fail verdict to explainable measurement decision. The final line is “Name the range before you call it a failure.” It does not recreate `acceptance-criteria`, `rounding-rule`, `uncertainty-context`, `fit-intent`, `comparison-frame`, `value-state`, `gauge-reality`, `absence-state`, imported `false-completion`, or imported `release-gate-roster`.
