## Research-informed premise

W3C distinguishes a short text alternative for a simple informative image from a longer description for a complex image. Its guidance says complex-image descriptions may need structure, relationships, values, trends, or adjacent text rather than a short phrase. [1] [2] W3C’s prerecorded audio-description guidance describes synchronized spoken information about actions, scene changes, on-screen text, and other important visual information during pauses in dialogue. [3] These sources do not prove a Stitch & Scale description feature or conformance.

Knitty’s current June 17, 2026 submission guidance asks for clean, crisp images, a variety of clear detail shots from all sides, and visibility of relevant design features because those images help people follow patterns. It separates atmosphere from detail photography, pattern text, charts, schematics, rights statements, and technical editing. [4] The research supports a bounded distinction: **a caption, short alt text, audio description, pattern, chart, and schematic are different information layers with different purposes**.

Knitty’s Winter 2026 context is seasonal research only. It must not be used as urgency, affiliation, or a customer outcome. The film remains year-round and does not mention Knitty or its deadline.

Do not claim that Stitch & Scale automatically describes images, creates compliant alt text, synchronizes audio description, connects descriptions to patterns, verifies accessibility, replaces technical editing, or guarantees reader understanding. Preserve technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, and test knitting as separate human evidence gates.

## Visual grammar and production bible

Use one quiet neutral tabletop, diffuse side light, restrained camera movement, and high-contrast original cards. The image should be an abstract non-identifiable knitwear silhouette with no real chart, schematic, pattern excerpt, model, garment, customer record, logo, or publication asset. Every card must display `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

Keep `PURPOSE`, `KEY_FEATURES`, `RELATIONSHIPS`, `LONG_DESCRIPTION`, `LIMITATIONS`, `CAPTION_TEXT`, `AUDIO_DESCRIPTION`, `PATTERN_REFERENCE`, `CHART_REFERENCE`, and `SCHEMATIC_REFERENCE` as separate field families. Do not show a dense table. Do not use a green `ACCESSIBLE` badge, WCAG certification stamp, auto-generated text effect, pattern-complete stamp, or visual effect that implies conformance or reader success. Generated text is not UI truth.

Lock card orientation, typography, tabletop, hand direction, narrator family, room tone, and light temperature across takes. David may appear in a neutral developer frame or remain voice-only; he must not perform knitting or be framed as a craft practitioner. Technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates.

## Sound motif and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family with a close, dry voice and consistent room tone. The optional sound motif is a soft card slide on `PURPOSE`, a held pause before `LONG_DESCRIPTION`, and silence on `IMAGE DESCRIPTION: UNVERIFIED`. No accessibility-certification chime, auto-generated-text sound, pattern-complete stamp, or reader-success bed.

Record clean room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, accent shifts, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, stale UI sounds, and effects that conceal **purpose**, **features**, **relationships**, **limitations**, or **status**. Deliver caption-on and caption-free review versions. Automatic captions are draft-only; a human must verify every description-scope, pattern, chart, schematic, and status word.

## Acceptance gates and blocker

The package cannot move beyond **blocked** until the product owner supplies a current, versioned, non-private, rights-cleared proof-registry entry or approved capture for the exact description surface. It must identify `IMAGE D-001`, design ID, image ID, purpose, short description, key features, relationships, long description, audio description, caption text, pattern reference, chart reference, schematic reference, limitations, language, source/basis, rights scope, reviewer, review time, and status. A description record does not prove pattern correctness, chart equivalence, schematic scale, technical editing, test-knit success, accessibility conformance, or reader understanding.

Do not use a real chart, schematic, pattern, garment, publication photo, customer record, logo, or private data without scoped rights/privacy clearance. Review caption-on and caption-free renders; reject captions that cover cards, text, numbers, logos, controls, or primary actions. Reject stale UI, unsupported claims, mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, duplicate filenames, and any prop text that could be mistaken for current product UI.

**Exact Generator action:** Do not generate or upload. Await a current proof-registry entry or approved capture, rights/privacy clearance, confirmed destination platform, and Director re-authorization after all gates pass.
