## Concept and dramatic engine

The central subject is a designer-facing release decision, represented by a checkbox that appears complete while the required human evidence remains unresolved. The desire is to know whether “finished” is honest. The obstacle is false closure: a status, table, or polished document can look complete without proving that the relevant size, chart, schematic, print copy, technical edit, or test knit received the appropriate review. The stakes are a buyer or knitter encountering an unresolved issue after release. The change is from trusting the checkbox to naming the human gate that still needs evidence. The contradiction is that a finished-looking record may be the least trustworthy frame if its evidence is not visible. The escalation moves from a checked box, to one unresolved human gate, to a clear invitation to audit rather than a promise of automation. The final image should be a current, readable product proof surface beside an explicitly open human gate; this image is unavailable in the current repository, so generation is blocked.
