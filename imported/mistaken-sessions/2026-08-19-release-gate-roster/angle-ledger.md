**Distinctness note — 2026-08-19:** The nearest prior angle is `test-knit-coverage`. This angle changes the pain from an untested size appearing in a table to an unowned evidence gate; the hook from a universal-size warning to a checkbox/roster contradiction; the visual metaphor from a size table to an empty release-room roster; the proof surface from a general by-size tester list to one verified gate slot at a time; the emotional turn from discovering omission to choosing accountability; and the final line from “find the size nobody checked” to “show me who checked the size.” It is adjacent but not a noun substitution. It remains blocked until current product captures confirm the proposed roster surface.

A new concept must explain which five elements differ from the nearest prior concept: pain, hook, visual metaphor, proof surface, emotional turn, and final line. Noun substitution is not angle diversity.
