## Continuity decision

`decision-basis-state` is a new angle about the evidence and roles beneath a craft-workflow decision: observation, source/reference, derivation or formula, assumption, decision owner, approver or gate, contributors, informed, open question, decision time, status, and limitations. It does not claim that Stitch & Scale evaluates evidence, calculates correctness, recommends decisions, records approvals, assigns DACI roles, replaces technical editing, proves test-knit success, gives financial or tax treatment, or establishes publication readiness.

| Existing territory | What it already owns | Why this pass is not a duplicate |

| `record-estimate-state` | Estimate versus recorded value | This pass can identify derivation and assumption but does not create a financial or quantitative estimate. |
