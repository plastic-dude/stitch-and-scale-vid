## Research interpretation

Ink & Switch describes local-first as prioritizing local storage and treating a device copy as primary while servers may hold secondary copies. It distinguishes practical agency from legal intellectual-property ownership and says users retain backup/loss-prevention responsibility. MDN documents that browser storage is best-effort by default, subject to quotas and eviction, and that persistent storage is browser-dependent. web.dev explains that persistent storage can reduce eviction risk but must be requested and may be granted or denied. These are frameworks and platform behavior references, not current Stitch & Scale product proof.
