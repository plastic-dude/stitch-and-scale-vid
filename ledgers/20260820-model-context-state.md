## Continuity decision

`model-context-state` is a new angle about the declared context of a pictured knitwear sample: size made, body/model context, finished measurements, fit intention, pose/view, image purpose, capture time, rights scope, and review status. It does not claim that Stitch & Scale identifies a garment size from a photograph, matches a model to a sample, evaluates fit, verifies ease, validates grading, measures a body, checks size inclusion, approves photography, or guarantees a visual or physical outcome.

| Existing territory | What it already owns | Why this pass is not a duplicate |
|---|---|---|
| `dye-lot-state` | Lot-level identity of physical material used or recorded for a design/sample | This pass concerns image/model context, not material lot identity. |
| `fit-intent` | Meaning of body, finished-garment, ease, and fit-intent numbers | This pass does not interpret a sizing number; it asks whether the pictured sample context is declared before a photo speaks for itself. |
| `fit-criteria-state` | Intended use, criteria, test, observed result, evidence, limitations, and fit disposition | This pass does not issue a fit verdict, pass/fail, suitability decision, or criteria result. |
| `measurement-provenance` | Origin, method, date, source, and status of a measurement | This pass does not trace measurement origin or calculate a size. |
| `publication-boundary-state` | Package transfer, destination, approvals, rights, and publication disposition | This pass does not approve a submission or transfer; it records image context only. |
| `sample-return-state` | Physical sample custody, receipt, return, condition, and current holder | This pass does not track shipping, receipt, return, or custody. |

## Inherited non-negotiables

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, technical editor, or tester. Technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, and test knitting remain distinct human evidence gates; an image-context record does not replace any of them.

Captions must not cover faces, hands, cards, numbers, rights fields, controls, logos, menus, or primary actions. Dense records require separate close, readable proof shots. Use one narrator family, caption-on and caption-free review versions, human caption review, full audio listening, and rejection of mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported claims, and duplicate filenames.

Only original abstract garment silhouettes, fictional context cards, approved founder voice or talking head, consented hand performance, and original or cleared sound may be used. No real model, body measurement, garment, publication photo, customer record, pattern, logo, or private data may appear without explicit scoped clearance.
