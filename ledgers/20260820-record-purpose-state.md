## Continuity decision

`record-purpose-state` is a new angle about the declared purpose and workflow stage of a craft-business record: source, recorded facts, derived values, attachments, next decision, owner, limitations, reviewer, and status. It does not claim that Stitch & Scale is an authoritative single source of truth, automatically transitions lifecycle stages, preserves every record relationship, gives accounting or tax treatment, approves submissions, tracks samples, validates technical editing, or proves outcomes.
