## Continuity decision

`translation-state` is a new angle about the identity and authorization trail of a translated or localized pattern artifact: source artifact, source version/locale, target artifact/locale, language metadata, units, abbreviations, linked chart and schematic, permission, rights basis, reviewer, translation status, and release status. It does not claim that Stitch & Scale translates, localizes, converts units, preserves technical meaning, manages permission, validates translations, certifies accessibility, replaces technical editing, or approves publication.
