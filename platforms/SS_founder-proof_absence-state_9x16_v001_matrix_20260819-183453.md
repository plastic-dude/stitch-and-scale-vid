## Master constraints

This package is a planning artifact, not a generated video. No platform is approved for publication. Do not infer organic rules from paid ad specifications. Before any derivative, inspect the current account-specific composer/preview and record date, account, format, caption length, safe area, and disposition. Keep all cards original and labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` unless an approved current capture replaces them.

## Accessibility acceptance

Captions must be synchronized, complete, readable, and human-reviewed. Keep text out of the lower caption zone and never cover semantic proof, values, numbers, formula, hands, logos, buttons, menus, or primary actions. Review at 320×568, 360×800, 390×844, and 430×932-equivalent phone views where possible, plus desktop and caption-free playback. If product proof later arrives, separately test field error identification, keyboard/focus, large text, reduced motion, empty/zero/N-A/unknown/not-checked states, calculation inclusion, export, interruption, and recovery. Do not turn platform or accessibility checks into a claim that the product supports missing-value codes, accurate grading, fit, accessible forms, or data-quality guarantees.
