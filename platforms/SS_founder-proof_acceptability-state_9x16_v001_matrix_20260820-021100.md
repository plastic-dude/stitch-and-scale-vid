## Caption map and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only. Captions must be human-reviewed and synchronized, including relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions may be used only as a draft and must be corrected. Provide caption-on and caption-free versions. Move captions to an empty safe area whenever the intended-use card, criteria, observed result, test/user evidence, limitation, decision owner, or disposition occupy the default lower area. Avoid speaker changes; if one is introduced, label it deliberately.

Use original or cleared foley: card movement, acceptance-stamp stop, card placement, and neutral hold tone when `TEST_OR_USER_EVIDENCE: PENDING` and `DISPOSITION: HOLD` appear. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, and effects hiding a defect. Confirm comprehension with audio muted through captions and with captions hidden through audio.

## Accessibility and visual review

The film may model distinctions among observed effect, intended use, requirements, test/user evidence, limitations, decision owner, and disposition as general design principles. It may not claim WCAG conformance or that the product provides validation, suitability, usability, project-level classification, accuracy measurement, outcome tracking, or human-gate completion. Use high-contrast original cards, sentence-case text, no color-only meaning, and dedicated readable inserts. Captions must not cover faces, hands, proof, tables, numbers, labels, controls, buttons, menus, intended use, criteria, observed result, test/user evidence, limitations, decision owner, disposition, or primary action.
