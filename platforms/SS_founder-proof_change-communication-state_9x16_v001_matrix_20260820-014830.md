## Caption map and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only. Captions must be human-reviewed and synchronized, including relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions may be used only as a draft and must be corrected. Provide caption-on and caption-free versions. Move captions to an empty safe area whenever the revision note, audience card, impact, owner/channel, limitation, human-gate cards, or disposition occupy the default lower area. Avoid speaker changes; if one is introduced, label it deliberately.

Use original or cleared foley: paper movement, stamp stop, card placement, and neutral hold tone when `DELIVERY UNVERIFIED` and `DISPOSITION: HOLD` appear. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, and effects hiding a defect. Confirm comprehension with audio muted through captions and with captions hidden through audio.

## Accessibility and visual review

The film may model change scope, audience notice, impact, timing, owner/channel, required action, limitations, and explicit disposition as general design principles. It may not claim WCAG conformance or that the product provides notification banners, accessible status messages, alerts, change logs, errata, public disclosure, or audience delivery. Use high-contrast original cards, sentence-case text, no color-only meaning, and dedicated readable inserts. Captions must not cover faces, hands, proof, tables, numbers, labels, controls, buttons, menus, audience, version, impact, timing, owner/channel, action, limitation, disposition, or primary action.
