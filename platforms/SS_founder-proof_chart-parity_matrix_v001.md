## Shared caption and accessibility requirements

Captions must be human-reviewed, synchronized, and checked against the final voice recording. Automatic captions may be a starting point only. Captions must not cover chart cells, legend, written line, counts, hands, face, controls, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions from beginning to end. Never present captions as evidence that the underlying chart/text pair is equivalent.

## Shared claim requirements

The film may use chart, written instructions, legends, repeats, partial repeats, special stitches, shaping, no-stitch cells, and reading direction as workflow context. It may not claim that Stitch & Scale currently compares chart and written instructions, validates semantic equivalence, or guarantees correctness until current product proof is registered and approved.

## Shared rights requirements

Chart fragments, written lines, symbols, and sample imagery must be original or licensed. Third-party chart or product captures require permission and current provenance. David’s voice/image requires authorization. Product captures must be current, non-private, versioned, and approved. Audio must be original or licensed. No external archive or upload while blocked.
