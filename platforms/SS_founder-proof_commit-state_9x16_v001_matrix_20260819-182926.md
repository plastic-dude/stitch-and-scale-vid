## Master constraints

This package is a planning artifact, not a generated video. No platform is approved for publication. Do not infer organic rules from paid ad specifications. Before any derivative, inspect the current account-specific composer/preview and record the date, account, format, caption length, safe area, and disposition. Keep all cards original and labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` unless an approved current capture replaces them.

## Accessibility and interruption acceptance

Captions must be synchronized, complete, readable, and human-reviewed. Include meaningful sound effects and identify the single narrator only when needed. Keep text out of the lower caption zone and never cover status proof, hands, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, or primary actions. Review at 320×568, 360×800, 390×844, and 430×932-equivalent phone views where possible, plus desktop and caption-free playback. If product proof later arrives, separately test edit, save request, save success, save failure, interruption/background, return, retry, keyboard/focus, reduced motion, and large text. Do not turn platform or accessibility checks into a claim that the product autosaves, syncs, backs up, restores, survives process death, prevents data loss, or is WCAG conformant.
