## Acceptance and accessibility

This is a planning artifact, not a generated video, and no platform is approved for publication. Before any derivative, inspect the current account-specific composer/preview and record date, account, format, caption behavior, safe area, and disposition. Keep all cards original and labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` unless an approved current capture replaces them.

Captions must be synchronized, complete, readable, and human-reviewed. Keep text out of the lower caption zone and never cover source field, derived field, unit, size, view, expected relation, discrepancy, reviewed, decision, tables, numbers, faces, hands, logos, buttons, menus, or primary actions. Review at 320×568, 360×800, 390×844, and 430×932-equivalent phone views where possible, plus desktop and caption-free playback. Separately test any future product proof for exact consistency labels, source/derived relationships, units, sizes, views, expected relations, discrepancy, review, decision, and human evidence gates. Do not turn accessibility or platform checks into a claim that the product offers data-quality scoring, unit conversion, grading correctness, export parity, or outcome guarantees.
