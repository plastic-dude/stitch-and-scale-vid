## Cross-platform acceptance gates

Use one narrator family, human-verified captions, caption-on and caption-free review, original or cleared audio, stable room tone, and one information row per close shot. Captions must keep source term, target term, mapping type, and unmapped state readable and may not cover cards, arrows, chips, version/date, faces, hands, logos, controls, menus, disclaimers, or primary actions. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported claims, copyrighted excerpts without clearance, and duplicate filenames.

The matrix does not establish that Stitch & Scale translates, maps, converts, validates, certifies, checks accessibility, proves equivalence, or guarantees destination acceptance. Exact platform details must be rechecked at approval time.
