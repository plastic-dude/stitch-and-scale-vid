## Caption map and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only. Captions must be human-reviewed and synchronized, including relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions may be used only as a draft and must be corrected. Provide caption-on and caption-free versions. Caption placement must move to an empty safe area whenever a task card, status, hint, label, or hand occupies the default lower area. Avoid speaker changes; if one is introduced, label it deliberately.

Use original or cleared foley: card movement, paper placement, one neutral search sound, and a restrained stop tone. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, and effects hiding a defect. Confirm comprehension with audio muted through captions and with captions hidden through audio.

## Accessibility and visual review

The film may model descriptive headings, labels, hints, and honest statuses as a general design principle. It may not claim WCAG conformance or that the product improves memory or accessibility. Use high-contrast original cards, short sentence-case labels, no color-only status meanings, and dedicated readable inserts. Ensure the disclaimer does not compete with the proof card. Captions must not cover faces, hands, proof, tables, numbers, labels, controls, buttons, menus, or primary action.
