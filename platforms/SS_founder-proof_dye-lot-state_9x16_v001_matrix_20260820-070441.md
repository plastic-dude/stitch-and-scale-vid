## Cross-platform acceptance gates

The Generator must not act until a current proof-registry entry or approved capture verifies the exact material-lot identity surface, including `MATERIAL M-001`, design ID, sample ID, material name, brand/source, fiber/content, color name/code, lot/dye lot, label reference, quantity/unit, acquisition time, used-from relationship, substitution flag, observed difference, checked-at time, source/basis, reviewer, review time, and status. A fictional card may remain only as a clearly labeled explanatory prop; it may not be presented as product UI, a real label, or a supplier record.

A recorded lot must remain separate from gauge, fiber behavior, care suitability, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, test knitting, and publication acceptance. Confirm destination platform and rights to every visible prop, voice, hand, sound, and capture. Confirm that the script and captions do not promise color continuity, supply availability, lot matching, fiber outcomes, or product capability. Check caption-on and caption-free renders, inspect mobile readability, listen to the full audio for doubled words, mixed voices, overlaps, drift, clipping, and room-tone jumps, and review platform UI overlays before any approval.
