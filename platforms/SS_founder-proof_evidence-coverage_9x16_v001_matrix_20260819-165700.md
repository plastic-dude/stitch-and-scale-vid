## Master constraints

This package is a planning artifact, not a generated video. No platform is approved for publication. Do not infer organic rules from paid ad specifications. Before any derivative, inspect the current account-specific composer/preview and record the date, account, format, caption length, safe area, and disposition. Keep all cards original and labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` unless an approved current capture replaces them.

## Accessibility acceptance

Captions must be synchronized, complete, readable, and human-reviewed. Include meaningful sound effects and identify the single narrator only when needed. Keep text out of the lower caption zone and never cover proof, hands, numbers, logos, buttons, menus, or primary actions. Review at 320×568, 360×800, 390×844, and 430×932-equivalent phone views where possible, plus desktop and caption-free playback. Do not turn platform caption or layout checks into a claim that evidence covers a claim, requirement, or release decision.
