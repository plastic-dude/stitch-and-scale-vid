## Shared caption and accessibility requirements

Captions must be human-reviewed, synchronized, and checked against the final voice recording. Automatic captions may be a starting point only. Captions must not cover the yarn label, swatch, stitch counts, needle size, hands, face, controls, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions from beginning to end.

## Shared claim requirements

The film may use yarn category, gauge, swatch, substitution, fiber, and fabric as workflow context. It may not claim that Stitch & Scale currently tracks swatches, predicts fabric behavior, makes substitutions safe, or guarantees fit until current product proof is registered and approved.

## Shared rights requirements

Visible yarn labels must be generic original props or covered by written permission. Swatches, needles, and samples must be original or licensed. David’s voice/image requires authorization. Product captures must be current, non-private, versioned, and approved. Audio must be original or licensed. No external archive or upload while blocked.
