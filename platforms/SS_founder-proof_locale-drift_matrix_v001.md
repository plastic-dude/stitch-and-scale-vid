## Shared caption and accessibility requirements

Captions must be human-reviewed, synchronized, and checked against the final voice recording. Automatic captions may be a starting point only. Captions must not cover locale identifiers, counts, keys, paper, hands, face, controls, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions. Any translated subtitle file requires qualified native-language review and a source-text comparison.

## Shared claim requirements

The film may use abbreviations, keys, measurements, symbols, and locale terminology as workflow context. It may not claim that Stitch & Scale currently performs translation QA, semantic equivalence checking, or automatic localization until current product proof is registered and approved.

## Shared rights and language requirements

All paper props must be original. Any translated wording must be reviewed and approved by a qualified native-language reviewer before generation. David’s voice/image requires authorization. Product captures must be current, non-private, versioned, and approved. Audio must be original or licensed. No archive or upload while blocked.
