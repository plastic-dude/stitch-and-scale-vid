## Cross-platform acceptance gates

The Generator must not act until a current proof-registry entry or approved capture verifies the exact image-context surface, including `IMAGE I-001`, design ID, sample ID, garment size made, body context, model context, finished measurements, ease or fit intention, image purpose, pose/view, captured-at time, source/basis, rights scope, reviewer, review time, and status. A fictional card may remain only as a clearly labeled explanatory prop; it may not be presented as product UI, a real model record, a publication asset, or customer data.

An image-context record must remain separate from fit, grading, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, test knitting, accessibility, and publication acceptance. Confirm destination platform and rights to every visible prop, voice, hand, sound, and capture. Confirm that the script and captions do not promise fit, size inclusion, comfort, model matching, or product capability. Check caption-on and caption-free renders, inspect mobile readability, listen to the full audio for doubled words, mixed voices, overlaps, drift, clipping, and room-tone jumps, and review platform UI overlays before any approval.
