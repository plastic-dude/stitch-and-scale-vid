## Cross-platform acceptance gates

Use one narrator family, human-verified captions, caption-on and caption-free review, original or cleared audio, stable room tone, and one evidence-state family per close shot. Captions must keep `OBSERVED`, `TRANSCRIBED`, `CALCULATED`, `INTERPRETED`, `UNRESOLVED`, `SOURCE`, `METHOD`, `INPUTS`, `REVIEWER`, and `LIMIT` readable and may not cover cards, tables, numbers, proof, faces, hands, logos, controls, menus, disclaimers, or primary actions. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported claims, copyrighted excerpts without clearance, and duplicate filenames.

The matrix does not establish that Stitch & Scale automatically captures observations, transcribes faithfully, validates formulas, detects inference, tracks reviewers, reports evidence states, validates craft results, or certifies accessibility or publication readiness. Exact platform details must be rechecked at approval time.
