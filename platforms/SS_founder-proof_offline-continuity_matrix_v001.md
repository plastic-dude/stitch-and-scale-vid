## Shared caption and accessibility requirements

Captions must be human-reviewed, synchronized, and checked against the final voice recording. Automatic captions may be a starting point only. Captions must not cover local/pending/stale labels, record content, hands, face, controls, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions from beginning to end. Do not present captions as evidence that a sync state is accurate.

## Shared claim requirements

The film may use local data, offline continuity, caching, stale state, pending writes, synchronization, and network loss as architecture/workflow context. It may not claim that Stitch & Scale currently works completely offline, syncs automatically, resolves conflicts, or keeps local and remote state equivalent until current product proof is registered and approved.

## Shared rights requirements

Device fixtures must be original and contain no personal data. Workspace, notebook, yarn, and sample imagery must be original or licensed. David’s voice/image requires authorization. Product captures must be current, non-private, versioned, and approved. Audio must be original or licensed. No external archive or upload while blocked.
