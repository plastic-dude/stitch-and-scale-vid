## Caption map and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only. Captions must be human-reviewed and synchronized, including relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions may be used only as a draft and must be corrected. Provide caption-on and caption-free versions. Move captions to an empty safe area whenever the envelope, version card, open items, rights, destination, human-gate cards, transfer scope, or disposition occupy the default lower area. Avoid speaker changes; if one is introduced, label it deliberately.

Use original or cleared foley: envelope movement, paper stop at the seal, card placement, and neutral hold tone when `DISPOSITION: HOLD` appears. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, and effects hiding a defect. Confirm comprehension with audio muted through captions and with captions hidden through audio.

## Accessibility and visual review

The film may model pre-handoff checking, exact scope, change opportunity, rights review, and explicit disposition as general design principles. It may not claim WCAG conformance or that the product provides release controls, export assurance, handoff guarantees, or accessible status notifications. Use high-contrast original cards, sentence-case text, no color-only meaning, and dedicated readable inserts. Captions must not cover faces, hands, proof, tables, numbers, labels, controls, buttons, menus, output ID, version, open items, rights, destination, transfer scope, disposition, or primary action.
