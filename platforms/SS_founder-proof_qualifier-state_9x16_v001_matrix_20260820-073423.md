## Cross-platform acceptance gates

Use one narrator family, human-verified captions, caption-on and caption-free review, original or cleared audio, stable room tone, and one information family per close shot. Captions must keep qualifier and value together and may not cover values, qualifiers, scope, source, dates, units, numbers, faces, hands, logos, controls, menus, disclaimers, or primary actions. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported claims, and duplicate filenames.

The matrix does not establish that Stitch & Scale assigns qualifiers, infers scope, calculates estimates, detects required fields, marks completeness, validates craft output, or guarantees comprehension. Exact platform details must be rechecked at approval time.
