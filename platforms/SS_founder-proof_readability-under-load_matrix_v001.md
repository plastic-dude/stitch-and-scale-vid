## Shared caption and accessibility requirements

Captions must be human-reviewed, synchronized, and checked against the final voice recording. Automatic captions may be a starting point only. Captions must not cover chart, key, text, hands, face, counts, controls, logos, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions from beginning to end. Do not present a captioned video alone as evidence of accessible pattern content.

## Shared claim requirements

The film may use contrast, uncluttered layout, defined abbreviations, written alternatives, readable keys, and pattern usability as workflow context. It may not claim that Stitch & Scale currently provides accessible PDF mode, reflow, contrast checking, screen-reader support, chart-to-text equivalence, or accessibility certification until current proof is registered and approved.

## Shared rights requirements

Phone, paper, yarn, needles, and sample imagery must be original or licensed. Never show private reader data. David’s voice/image requires authorization. Product captures must be current, non-private, versioned, and approved. Audio must be original or licensed. No external archive or upload while blocked.
