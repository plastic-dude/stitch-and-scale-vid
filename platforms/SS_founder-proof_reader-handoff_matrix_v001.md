## Shared caption and accessibility requirements

Captions must be human-reviewed, synchronized, and checked against the final voice recording. Automatic captions may be used as a starting point only. The caption-on version must never obscure hands, the paper handoff, chart/schematic proof, sizing/ease values, controls, labels, faces, logos, or the final CTA. A caption-free version must be reviewed from beginning to end for visual clarity and audio comprehension. If more than one speaker is introduced, captions must identify the speaker.

## Shared claim requirements

The film may use reader handoff, sizing, ease, schematics, chart keys, and symbol interpretation as workflow context. It may not claim that Stitch & Scale currently checks those surfaces, guarantees fit, guarantees readability, or guarantees successful completion until current product proof is registered and approved.

## Shared rights requirements

The paper prop must be original. David’s voice/image requires explicit authorization. Any product capture must be current, non-private, versioned, and approved. Any sample imagery must be original or licensed. Audio must be original or licensed for every intended platform. No upload or external archive is authorized while the brief remains blocked.
