## Cross-platform acceptance gates

Use one narrator family, human-verified captions, caption-on and caption-free review, original or cleared audio, stable room tone, and one information family per close shot. Captions must keep relationship type and direction readable and may not cover cards, connectors, stages, owners, dates, numbers, faces, hands, logos, controls, menus, disclaimers, or primary actions. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported claims, and duplicate filenames.

The matrix does not establish that Stitch & Scale links, routes, merges, selects source of truth, tracks delivery/custody, infers relationships, validates craft output, or guarantees acceptance or payment. Exact platform details must be rechecked at approval time.
