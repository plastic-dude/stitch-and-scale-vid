## Caption map and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only. Captions must be human-reviewed and synchronized, including relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions may be used only as a draft and must be corrected. Provide caption-on and caption-free versions. Move captions to an empty safe area whenever the request tray, owner card, response expectation, waiting status, human-gate cards, or closure state occupy the default lower area. Avoid speaker changes; if one is introduced, label it deliberately.

Use original or cleared foley: card placement, tray slide, quiet waiting pause, and neutral closure tone only when the card’s explicit state changes. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, and effects hiding a defect. Confirm comprehension with audio muted through captions and with captions hidden through audio.

## Accessibility and visual review

The film may model response expectations, status communication, and contextual contact information as general design principles. It may not claim WCAG conformance or that the product provides accessible status notifications, contact routing, or private-channel controls. Use high-contrast original cards, sentence-case text, no color-only meaning, and dedicated readable inserts. Captions must not cover faces, hands, proof, tables, numbers, labels, controls, buttons, menus, tray, owner, time, or primary action.
