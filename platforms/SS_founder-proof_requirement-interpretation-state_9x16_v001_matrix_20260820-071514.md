## Cross-platform acceptance gates

Use one narrator family, human-verified captions, caption-on and caption-free review, original or cleared audio, stable room tone, and a readable proof sequence. Captions may not cover faces, hands, requirement cards, examples, numbers, tables, logos, controls, menus, disclaimers, or the primary action. Show one information family per close shot; do not use a dense table or a small card stack as the only proof. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported claims, and duplicate filenames.

The matrix does not establish that Stitch & Scale detects ambiguity, supports accessible interpretations, validates instructions, or guarantees comprehension. Exact platform details must be rechecked at approval time.
