## Caption map and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only. Captions must be human-reviewed and synchronized, including relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions may be used only as a draft and must be corrected. Provide caption-on and caption-free versions. Move captions to an empty safe area whenever the reply card, issue/decision fields, human-gate cards, or closure state occupy the default lower area. Avoid speaker changes; if one is introduced, label it deliberately.

Use original or cleared foley: reply-card placement, paper stop, card placement, and a neutral open-loop tone when `CLOSED: NO` appears. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, and effects hiding a defect. Confirm comprehension with audio muted through captions and with captions hidden through audio.

## Accessibility and visual review

The film may model explicit issue identification, correction suggestions, and visible closure state as general design principles. It may not claim WCAG conformance or that the product automatically detects or resolves errors. Use high-contrast original cards, sentence-case text, no color-only meaning, and dedicated readable inserts. Captions must not cover faces, hands, proof, tables, numbers, labels, controls, buttons, menus, issue, decision, next action, or primary action.

## Claims and rights

No current product-proof registry entry or approved capture verifies a response-to-resolution record with request ID, issue/gate, decision, reason/correction, affected artifact, owner, next action, closure state, and closure time. Do not show current product UI, private messages, customer names, copyrighted pattern pages, logos, or unlicensed audio/artwork. Use original neutral props and cleared voice/foley only.
