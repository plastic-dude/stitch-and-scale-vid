## Caption map and audio plan

Use one approved narrator family only. Captions must be human-reviewed and synchronized, including relevant non-speech audio. Automatic captions may be used only as a draft and must be corrected. Provide caption-on and caption-free versions. Move captions to an empty safe area whenever the change thread, affected-output cards, human-gate cards, or next check occupy the default lower area. Avoid speaker changes; if one is introduced, label it deliberately.

Use original or cleared foley: paper movement, thread stop, card placement, and neutral review-boundary tone. Record room tone before and after each setup. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, clipped consonants, room-tone jumps, and effects hiding a defect. Confirm comprehension with audio muted through captions and with captions hidden through audio.

## Accessibility and visual review

The film may model review-before-commit, descriptive change scope, and explicit correction boundaries as general design principles. It may not claim WCAG conformance or that the product provides undo, impact analysis, propagation control, or automatic downstream updates. Use high-contrast original cards, sentence-case text, no color-only meaning, and dedicated readable inserts. Captions must not cover faces, hands, proof, tables, numbers, labels, controls, buttons, menus, thread, affected outputs, or primary action.
