## Master constraints and accessibility acceptance

This package is a planning artifact, not a generated video. No platform is approved for publication. Before any derivative, inspect the current account-specific composer/preview and record date, account, format, caption length, safe area, and disposition. Keep all cards original and labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` unless an approved current capture replaces them.

Captions must be synchronized, complete, readable, and human-reviewed. Keep text out of the lower caption zone and never cover latest, snapshot, artifact ID, version, capture time, change, supersedes, reference, use, owner, numbers, tables, hands, logos, buttons, menus, or primary actions. Review at 320×568, 360×800, 390×844, and 430×932-equivalent phone views where possible, plus desktop and caption-free playback. If product proof later arrives, separately test current/latest separation, frozen-state retrieval, claim binding, revision, owner, supersedes, keyboard/focus, large text, reduced motion, and error identification. Do not turn platform or accessibility checks into a claim that the product exposes immutable releases, archives, version history, hashes, or content-integrity guarantees.
