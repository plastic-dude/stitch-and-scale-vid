## Shared acceptance conditions

Every deliverable must use one narrator family or a declared speaker map, narration-verbatim captions, caption-on and caption-free review, readable close proof shots, original or rights-cleared assets, a unique filename, and no unsupported product or founder claims. A dense table, generic generated UI, or crop that makes proof unreadable fails review. No deliverable is approved for generation while the linked brief remains blocked.
