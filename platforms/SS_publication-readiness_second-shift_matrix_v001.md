## Platform profile notes

### YouTube

The standard master should be uploaded with a reviewed caption file and correct original-language metadata. YouTube’s current upload guidance exposes title and description limits and supports language/caption certification metadata. The 9:16 cut should be treated as a Short profile only after the current upload rules are rechecked at generation time. The title and description must not imply guaranteed fit, publication readiness, or measured outcomes.

### Instagram Reels

Instagram’s current help page states that Reels accept aspect ratios from 1.91:1 through 9:16, require at least 30 FPS and 720-pixel resolution, and recommend a 420×654 cover image. The cover still must use a clean garment/evidence composition with no important text near crop danger areas. Verify the in-app cover preview before publishing because the cover cannot currently be edited after upload according to the cited page.

### TikTok

TikTok for Business guidance emphasizes voiceover, captions/subtitles, live-action product-in-use footage, and keeping important elements inside the platform safe zone. Its performance figures are directional platform guidance, not a forecast for Stitch & Scale. The creative should not depend on trending audio; the original paper/fabric sound motif and one narrator family protect continuity and rights.

## Caption safety map

- Never place captions over the garment’s stitch detail, hands, printed numbers, warnings, buttons, menus, logos, or primary actions.
- Use a lower safe zone only in clean tabletop or end-card frames.
- Move to an upper safe zone when the proof occupies the lower half.
- Check caption-on and caption-free versions at 320px-equivalent vertical preview width.
- Caption wording must match the final recording exactly; no generated UI labels or unsupported callouts.

## Acceptance matrix

| Check | Required evidence | Failure response |
|---|---|---|
| Native framing | Separate 16:9 and 9:16 shot/capture plans | Re-shoot or recapture; do not crop-only |
| Proof legibility | Close screenshot or screen recording where one QA/evidence state is readable | Replace shot; never explain unreadable proof in captions |
| Caption safety | Caption-on and caption-free review images or timecoded notes | Move captions or change shot |
| Audio continuity | Full listen-through and transcript comparison | Remake voice/audio; do not hide with music |
| Platform metadata | Title, description, language, caption, cover, and altered-content decisions | Complete metadata before upload |
| Rights | Signed/recorded permissions and asset sources | Block generation or remove asset |
| Claim truth | Claim ledger matches current product and evidence | Rewrite or prohibit claim |
| Human gate clarity | Physical print, chart, schematic, and test-knit checks remain visibly human | Rewrite if software appears to certify them |
