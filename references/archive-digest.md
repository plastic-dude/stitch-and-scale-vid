# Stitch & Scale Archive Digest

The competitive wedge is honest multi-size grading and the broader designer business loop: grading, yield, pricing, channels, testing, and launch. The emotional through-line is invisible labor—hours of spreadsheet math that bury care in handmade work.

The strongest audit blocker is trust around local storage and data loss. Designers fear losing months of work and distrust tools that hide business math. The audience also resists developer language, generic promises, and checkbox grading that appears complete only at smaller sizes.

The product currently recruits founding testers. The testers are the designers in the room who can verify the math, identify missing workflow pieces, and shape pricing. Never overpromise roadmap features. Use current product evidence and label uncertainty.

Future campaign research should rotate through sizing failures, local-first ownership, stale derived values, hidden labor and pricing, test-knit coverage, business math, publication readiness, and the honest outsider-founder story.
