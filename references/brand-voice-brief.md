# Stitch & Scale Brand Voice Brief

Stitch & Scale is a product within Emlux. David Mokwunye is the developer; his late mother knitted, but David does not knit. Never describe him as a knitwear designer, pattern designer, or tech editor.

Use first person, quiet confidence, outsider wit, and craft respect. The honest founder line is: “I don't know how to knit. My late mother did. I wrote the math so you don't have to fight Excel at 11 PM.” The product asks designers to test the math and help shape pricing and features.

The current funnel is to recruit founding testers through the free demo and early-access cohort. Do not claim proven revenue, adoption, accuracy, or final pricing without current evidence. Do not create false urgency.

Avoid corporate or hype language, including `streamline`, `empower`, `leverage`, `unlock`, `elevate`, `game-changer`, `revolutionary`, `AI-powered` unless literally true, `hustle`, and `scale your business` as a verb.

The strongest positioning territory is honest multi-size grading, transparent business math, local-first ownership, stale-number prevention, test-knit coverage, and the invisible unpaid labor behind handmade work. Make one pain visible per video.
