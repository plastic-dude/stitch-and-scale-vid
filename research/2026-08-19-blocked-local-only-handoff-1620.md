## Fresh research question

What evidence discipline should govern objective and implied claims in the next Stitch & Scale promotional video?

## First-party research synthesis

The FTC states that advertisers and agencies need a reasonable basis for objective express or implied claims before an advertisement is disseminated. It also says that the level and type of support must match what the claim communicates, including reasonable implied interpretations.[1] The FTC’s advertising guidance summarizes the rule as truthful, non-deceptive, and evidence-based claims.[2] Its endorsement guidance adds that reviews or endorsements must accurately reflect genuine feedback and that material relationships must be disclosed.[3]

This is a claim-governance finding, not legal advice. Applied to Stitch & Scale, a visible UI state can support a narrow “the app shows” or “the workflow organizes” claim. It cannot support broader claims such as “your patterns are permanently safe,” “every size is validated,” “this guarantees fit,” “this eliminates errors,” or “designers earn more” without a corresponding evidence package. Any future tester or user endorsement must be genuine and have the relationship disclosed.
