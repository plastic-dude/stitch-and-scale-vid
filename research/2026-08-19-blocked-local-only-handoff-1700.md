## First-party research synthesis

Etsy’s official seller guidance says payment-processing fees vary by the seller’s bank-account country, are charged in addition to Etsy’s 6.5% transaction fee, and can apply to the total sale price including shipping and applicable tax.[1] Payhip’s official pricing page states that PayPal and Stripe still charge their standard fees on every plan, so a displayed Payhip plan fee alone is not the complete net calculation.[2] Ravelry’s help search confirms that pattern selling is governed by its own seller/pattern-store guidance and that its in-store sales program passes the designer’s owed share through without additional Ravelry fees in that program, but it does not validate a universal net percentage for every Ravelry sale.[3]
