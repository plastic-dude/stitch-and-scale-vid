## Fresh research question

Which inputs are commonly treated as the minimum evidence for a useful apparel tech pack and factory quote, and how does the live Spec Sheet surface compare without implying production approval?

## Research synthesis

Points of Measure describes a tech pack as a production blueprint containing graded specs, POMs, tolerances, construction callouts, BOM details, colourways, and review information; it also identifies contacting a factory for a quote as a downstream use.[1] MakeMine similarly distinguishes measurement specs, full-size grading, tolerances, construction details, colourways, BOM completeness, and cost information, while warning that vague or missing specifications create vague quotes.[2] Lemura Knitwear’s knitwear-specific guide adds yarn fibre, gauge, trims, BOM, POM diagrams, graded measurements, stitch types, and finishing details as key inputs.[3]

These are practitioner sources, not supplier acceptance criteria or a guarantee of any factory’s requirements. They support a careful creative claim: the product can help expose whether a sheet contains important quote inputs and can show a blocked state before outreach. It cannot claim that the generated sheet is a complete technical pack, that a factory will quote from it, or that production quality is assured.
