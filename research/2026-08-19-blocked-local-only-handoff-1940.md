## Fresh research question

What does apparel grading actually establish, and how can a future grading-proof video show a complete size range without claiming universal fit or validated sizing?

## Research synthesis

Points of Measure explains that a brand size chart begins with a defined customer body type and ease, and that grading is the difference in measurements between sizes. It notes that grades can vary by point of measure, style, fabric, and size category; a consistent template supports consistency but does not eliminate the need for fit development.[1] The Fashion Institute of Technology describes grading as taking an initial sample pattern and producing a full range of sizes for production, while pointing to the separate challenges of anthropometry, fit, and sizing standards.[2]

These sources support a narrow creative claim: grading turns base measurements into a structured multi-size measurement range. They do not support “fits everyone,” “perfect fit,” “inclusive by calculation alone,” or “production-ready” without fit testing and review.
