## Fresh research question

What does the live Receipt Lab concretely provide, and what must a future receipts-and-ledger campaign avoid claiming about payment, buyer invoices, or profit?

## Direct product findings

The live Receipt Lab says it supports chat-first receipts for WhatsApp, Signal, and iMessage, a printable PDF path, and a monthly ledger. It explicitly notes that Etsy does not issue buyer invoices and that this receipt is the designer’s own record. The surface supports Receipt, Order Quote, and Refund Note modes; customer/date/channel/sale-type fields; pattern name and items; tax, platform fee, processing fee, shipping charged/cost, materials cost, notes, and branding choices. The sample receipt is `#REC-001`, dated 2026-08-19, on WhatsApp, for Classic Crew Neck Sweater, total `$0.00`, profit `$0.00`, with actions to copy/share, save as image, print/PDF, or save to ledger. It also says the ledger feeds Income Lab totals.

The direct evidence supports a future angle called `receipt-as-proof`: create a clear designer-owned record with visible assumptions and a ledger trail. It does not prove that a buyer paid, that Etsy issued an invoice, that a platform accepted the transaction, or that the displayed profit is a realized financial result. The sample `$0.00` state must not be presented as a sale.
