## Fresh wide-research question

## External evidence

Etsy’s official seller guidance distinguishes order receipts from buyer invoices: order receipts are physical transaction records for sellers, while neither packing slips nor order receipts are considered invoices for buyers. [1] The IRS explains that business records help identify income sources, track expenses, prepare financial statements and tax returns, and support reported items; the page does not turn an arbitrary displayed receipt into proof of payment or tax compliance. [2] UK government guidance likewise says self-employed people must keep accurate records of sales, income, expenses, and proof such as receipts, bank statements, and sales invoices, while distinguishing amounts owed or committed from amounts already received. [3]
