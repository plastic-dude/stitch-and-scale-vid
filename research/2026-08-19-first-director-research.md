## Craft workflow and audience context

The Director’s angle is consistent with current public craft education: knitting-pattern grading depends on size charts, gauge, ease, finished measurements, and stitch/row calculations, and spreadsheet-based workflows can make those relationships harder to inspect. These sources support keeping the claim at the level of workflow burden and inspectability, not claiming that every designer struggles or that Stitch & Scale guarantees correctness.

- Sister Mountain, “How to Grade Knitting Patterns Using a Spreadsheet”: https://www.sistermountain.com/blog/grade-knitting-patterns-spreadsheet
- School of SweetGeorgia, “Grading Knitwear for Beginners”: https://www.schoolofsweetgeorgia.com/courses/grading-knitwear-for-beginners/
- Ysolda, “Ysolda's sizing chart for knitwear designers”: https://ysolda.com/blogs/journal/ysolda-s-sizing-chart-for-knitwear-designers
- Purl Soho, “Understanding Ease + Selecting Your Size”: https://www.purlsoho.com/create/understanding-ease-selecting-your-size/
- The Knitting Guild Association, “Knitwear Designer Certification”: https://tkga.org/knitwear-designer-certification/

Decision impact: retain the approved line about a designer’s math and spreadsheet burden; avoid universal claims, performance guarantees, or statements that spreadsheets are inherently bad.

## Platform and accessibility context

The declared target is a 16:9 YouTube landing-page/product-explainer cut. Google’s YouTube guidance states that 16:9 is the standard computer aspect ratio and that other aspect ratios are adapted by the player. Caption guidance from W3C and public accessibility guidance emphasizes synchronized, equivalent, readable captions with sufficient contrast and safe placement that does not obstruct key visual content. The Director’s two-line, max-38-character caption map and proof-safe matte are therefore retained.

- Google/YouTube Help, “YouTube recommended upload encoding settings”: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171?hl=en
- W3C WAI, “Captions/Subtitles”: https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/
- W3C WAI, “Understanding Success Criterion 1.2.2: Captions (Prerecorded)”: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html
- Section508.gov, “Video and Other Synchronized Media”: https://www.section508.gov/create/synchronized-media/
- Australian Government Style Manual, “Requirements and standards for video and audio”: https://www.stylemanual.gov.au/content-types/video-and-audio/requirements-and-standards-video-and-audio

Decision impact: keep captions in the reserved top matte over product proof and lower-left only on uncluttered human frames; perform captions-off and captions-on review plus a mobile preview.
