## Direct observation

The live Pricing surface is titled `Pattern Pricing Advisor`. It exposes item type, skill level, market position, current price, hours worked, hourly rate, `Tech edited`, and `Test knitted` inputs. For the observed Classic Crew Neck Sweater state, it displays a recommended price of `$10.00`, a cost-plus floor of `$3.33`, and `At or above floor` versus current `$8.00`. It also shows documented market bands, size-range and skill-level adjusters, and per-platform net scenarios for Ravelry Pattern Store, Etsy, Ribblr, and Payhip.

## Guardrails

These are current demo outputs for the observed inputs, not universal prices, guaranteed market outcomes, revenue, or customer results. A future honest-price video must show the input assumptions and label outputs as modeled or recommended. It must not imply that the tool has validated a final price, that a price will sell, or that the displayed volume scenarios are realized performance. The `Tech edited` and `Test knitted` controls are visible inputs and must not be presented as proof that those checks have actually occurred unless the state is explicitly verified.

## Future angle candidate

`honest-price-model` remains a materially distinct future angle: invisible labor becomes visible through hours, rate, engineering scope, size range, fees, and assumptions. It differs from `privacy-by-default`, `receipt-vs-invoice`, and `test-knit-coverage` by using a cost-model contradiction and pricing proof surface rather than ownership, document-state boundaries, or test evidence. No new Generator brief is created while the privacy-by-default contract remains unpublished.
