# Pass 100 Browser Findings — Evidence Coverage

Checked 2026-08-19.

## NIST verification glossary

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/verification

NIST defines verification as confirmation through objective evidence that specified requirements have been fulfilled. It also describes producing objective evidence that sufficiently demonstrates a system satisfies its applicable requirements or characteristics, and evaluating whether outputs from a development phase satisfy conditions imposed at the phase start.

This is general verification terminology, not proof of a Stitch & Scale feature, accuracy, or publication approval.

## NASA Science Traceability Matrix

URL: https://hesto.smce.nasa.gov/focused-topics/

NASA describes a Science Traceability Matrix as a logical flow from science goals and objectives to mission and instrument requirements and data products. It says objectives should be testable, measurement requirements bridge objectives to instrument requirements, and the matrix summarizes what will be achieved and how. This supports a claim-to-evidence or requirement-to-data coverage concept without implying that a knitwear workflow has such a matrix.

## NIST AI Risk Management Framework

URL: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

NIST says the AI RMF is intended for voluntary use to improve incorporation of trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI products, services, and systems. The page notes that the framework is being revised and that the Generative AI Profile was released in 2024. This is governance guidance, not proof of Stitch & Scale AI behavior or a claim that the app uses the framework.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may display a claim/evidence matrix where one source or one evidence item appears to support several claims, but the matrix marks uncovered or mismatched claims rather than granting a blanket pass. The card should separate `CLAIM / REQUIREMENT / EVIDENCE ITEM / COVERAGE TYPE / SCOPE / OWNER / GAP / STATUS`. It must remain blocked until current product proof exists and must not become a human-gate checklist or release roster.
