# Pass 101 Browser Findings — Evidence Type

Checked 2026-08-19.

## NIST verification glossary

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/verification

NIST defines verification as confirmation through objective evidence that specified requirements have been fulfilled. It also describes evidence sufficient to demonstrate applicable requirements or characteristics. The page contains multiple context-specific definitions, so the broad claim must remain bounded by requirement and context.

This is general verification terminology, not proof of a Stitch & Scale feature, product accuracy, or publication approval.

## NIST metrological traceability

URL: https://www.nist.gov/metrology/metrological-traceability

NIST describes metrological traceability as a documented unbroken chain of calibrations connecting a measurement result to a reference, with each calibration contributing to measurement uncertainty. NIST explicitly states that traceability alone does not guarantee fitness for purpose, and that the provider of a result is responsible for supporting a traceability claim while the user assesses its validity. NIST also notes that results from calibrations/tests apply only to the specific instrument or standard at the time of test unless clearly stated otherwise.

This is measurement-science guidance and must not be turned into a claim that Stitch & Scale provides calibration, traceability, or fitness-for-purpose certification.

## Craft Yarn Council body sizing

URL: https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/body-sizing

CYC distinguishes actual body measurements, finished garment measurements, pattern sizing information, schematics, and ease. It says sizing charts and fit/length charts are guidelines, and that pattern-specific information and individual differences matter. It describes measurements such as chest/bust, sleeve length, armhole depth, and other body dimensions.

This is general craft guidance, not a product fit guarantee or proof of an individual garment outcome.

## Craft Yarn Council yarn weight system

URL: https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/yarn-weight-system

CYC lists common gauge and needle/hook ranges by yarn category but explicitly labels the ranges as guidelines and says to follow the gauge stated in the pattern. The source distinguishes a category guideline from a project-specific gauge observation.

This is general yarn/gauge context, not product proof or evidence of a specific pattern’s gauge.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may classify evidence by what question it can answer: observed measurement, calculation, source guidance, technical edit, physical print review, chart readability review, schematic scale review, or test knit. The visual record should show `QUESTION / EVIDENCE TYPE / SUPPORTS / DOES NOT SUPPORT / CONDITIONS / STATUS`. It must not imply that evidence types are interchangeable or that the product chooses the correct human gate.
