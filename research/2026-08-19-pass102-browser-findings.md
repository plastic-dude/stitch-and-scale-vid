# Pass 102 Browser Findings — Failure Mode

Checked 2026-08-19.

## NASA GSFC Failure Modes and Effects Analysis handbook

URL: https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/GSFC/GSFC-HDBK-8004

NASA describes this handbook as a uniform approach for performing Failure Mode, Effects, and Criticality Analysis concurrently with development, as a living risk assessment document. It describes updating FMECA as designs, materials, operational parameters, processes, operations, or knowledge are refined.

This is NASA mission/infrastructure risk guidance, not proof of a Stitch & Scale risk feature or a claim that a knitwear workflow should use FMECA.

## NASA Technical Risk Management

URL: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-4-technical-risk-management/

NASA characterizes risk with scenario(s) leading to degraded performance, likelihood(s), and consequence(s), while incorporating uncertainty. It describes initiating events, scenario evolution, mitigating or exacerbating events, risk profiles, triggers, mitigation/contingency plans, periodic monitoring, and captured rationale/work products. The page emphasizes tailoring risk management to the project.

This is systems-engineering guidance. It does not establish product risk scoring, hazard analysis, or mitigation behavior for Stitch & Scale.

## NIST Measurement Uncertainty

URL: https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed/topic-areas/measurement-uncertainty

NIST describes measurement as an experimental process producing a value attributable to a quantitative property, and measurement uncertainty as a parameter characterizing dispersion of values reasonably attributable to a measurand. NIST also points to measurement models and uncertainty evaluation.

This does not define a failure-mode UI or prove a measurement result is accurate or fit for a particular knitwear use.

## NIST Metrological Traceability

URL: https://www.nist.gov/metrology/metrological-traceability

NIST states that traceability alone does not guarantee fitness for purpose, and that the provider of a result supports a traceability claim while the user assesses validity. Results apply to the specific instrument or standard at the time unless otherwise stated.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may show a fictional record that names `SCENARIO / INITIATING EVENT / FAILURE MODE / EFFECT / DETECTION / LIKELIHOOD / CONSEQUENCE / MITIGATION / TRIGGER / OWNER / STATUS`, but it must not claim the product performs risk analysis. The angle should focus on making a possible failure visible, not on certifying safety, correctness, or release.
