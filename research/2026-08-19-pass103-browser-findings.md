# Pass 103 Browser Findings — Risk Trigger

Checked 2026-08-19.

## NASA Technical Risk Management

URL: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/6-4-technical-risk-management/

NASA describes risk management with scenarios, likelihoods, consequences, uncertainty, risk status measurements, and risk-reporting requirements. It says risk plans should identify what will trigger actions and how activities will be communicated. It describes selecting thresholds that trigger mitigation action plans, naming stakeholders, monitoring risks periodically, and implementing mitigation/contingency plans when applicable thresholds are triggered.

This is NASA systems-engineering guidance, not proof of a Stitch & Scale risk trigger, monitoring, or mitigation feature.

## NIST Risk Management Framework

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/risk-management

NIST describes the RMF as a comprehensive, flexible, repeatable, and measurable seven-step process for managing information-security and privacy risk. The page was updated July 24, 2026 and includes current publication updates. This is governance guidance and does not establish a Stitch & Scale risk-management implementation.

## NASA Agency Risk Management Office

URL: https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/risk-management

NASA describes objectives-driven risk management, including risk identification, analysis, communication, monitoring, and management, with RIDM and Continuous Risk Management as complementary processes. It emphasizes risk leadership, accountability, communication, and updates as risks evolve.

This does not establish a product risk posture, trigger engine, or mitigation authority for Stitch & Scale.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may show a fictional warning card that is not actionable until it names `OBSERVABLE / THRESHOLD OR EVENT / TRIGGER CONDITION / ACTION / OWNER / DEADLINE / STATUS / REVIEWED AT`. It must not claim that a product monitors risks, triggers mitigation, or calculates a safety posture. The angle should focus on an unbound warning, not a full failure-mode scenario or release status.
