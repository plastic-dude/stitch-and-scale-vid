## NIST Incident Response

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/incident-response

NIST says SP 800-61 Revision 3 was finalized in April 2025 and supersedes Revision 2. Its life-cycle model distinguishes broader preparation/risk-management activities from incident response itself, which is shown as Detect, Respond, and Recover, with continuous improvement informed by lessons learned. The scope is intentionally general because implementation details vary across technologies, environments, and organizations.

This is cybersecurity incident-response guidance, not proof of a Stitch & Scale response workflow or an operational capability.
