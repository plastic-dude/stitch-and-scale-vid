## NIST compensating controls

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/compensating_controls

NIST defines compensating controls as security and privacy controls implemented in lieu of baseline controls that provide equivalent or comparable protection, with the exact definition depending on the applicable NIST publication. This is security/privacy context; it does not establish a Stitch & Scale waiver or substitute-control feature.

## NIST Risk Management Framework

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/risk-management

NIST describes the Risk Management Framework as a comprehensive, flexible, repeatable, and measurable seven-step process for managing information-security and privacy risk. The current page was updated July 24, 2026. It is an organizational risk-management resource, not evidence that Stitch & Scale accepts risk, authorizes exceptions, or manages controls.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may show a fictional record with `REQUIREMENT / DEVIATION / SCOPE / RATIONALE / AUTHORITY / CONDITIONS / COMPENSATING ACTION / EXPIRY OR REVIEW / RESIDUAL STATE / STATUS`. It must distinguish a bounded waiver decision from meeting the original requirement, and must not claim Stitch & Scale approves waivers, accepts risk, supplies compensating controls, or verifies residual risk.
