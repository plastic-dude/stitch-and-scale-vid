# Pass 109 Browser Findings — Consent State

Checked 2026-08-19.

## ICO valid consent

URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/consent/what-is-valid-consent/

The ICO page, which says its guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act, defines consent as freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous agreement by statement or clear affirmative action. It explains that consent should provide genuine choice and control, be unbundled where appropriate, cover named purposes and processing activities, and be easy to withdraw. It also says consent may need refreshing if purposes or activities evolve; the page does not establish a universal duration.

## ICO obtaining, recording, and managing consent

URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/consent/how-should-we-obtain-record-and-manage-consent/

The ICO guidance says requests should be prominent, concise, clear, separate from general terms, and avoid vague or blanket language. It says separate opt-ins may be needed for different purposes or processing types; silence, inactivity, pre-ticked boxes, defaults, and blanket acceptance are not valid affirmative consent. Records should demonstrate who consented, when, what they were told, how they consented, the relevant version, and whether consent was withdrawn. Consent should be managed as ongoing choice and control, with refresh when purposes change and withdrawal as easy as giving consent.

## WIPO rights clearance

URL: https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/rights-clearance-a-guide-for-independent-filmmakers/en/3-rights-assessed-during-clearance.html

WIPO’s filmmaker guidance emphasizes identifying all rights and uses in advance, clearing music, clips, images, artwork, personality rights, and derivative/adaptation uses, keeping written licenses/releases and conditions, and maintaining clearance records. A license for one use does not silently authorize different distribution windows, territories, promotional uses, or adaptations.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may show a fictional consent card with `SUBJECT / PURPOSE / USE / STORAGE / DISTRIBUTION / DERIVATIVE USE / VERSION / GIVEN AT / WITHDRAWN AT / STATUS`. It must not claim Stitch & Scale obtains, stores, manages, or enforces consent. It must distinguish a narrow permission from blanket rights and preserve the rights ledger as a production requirement.
