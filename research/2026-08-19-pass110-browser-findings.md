## ICO consent withdrawal

URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/consent/how-should-we-obtain-record-and-manage-consent/

The ICO guidance page, which says its guidance is under review following the Data (Use and Access) Act, states that people have a right to withdraw consent at any time and that withdrawal should be as easy as giving consent. It distinguishes processing that occurred lawfully before withdrawal from processing after withdrawal, says processing based on consent should stop as soon as possible after withdrawal, and says a different lawful basis cannot simply be swapped in after choosing consent without fairness and communication. The page also says withdrawal should be recorded and that suppression records may be needed for some purposes.

## WIPO rights-clearance guide

URL: https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/rights-clearance-a-guide-for-independent-filmmakers/en/index.html

WIPO’s guide describes early rights-clearance planning as a way to avoid later licensing pitfalls and complex issues. Its detailed rights-clearance guidance covers the need to identify exact uses, territories, windows, people, media, music, clips, artwork, promotion, adaptations, and written licenses/releases. A withdrawal record is therefore not treated as automatic erasure of every downstream asset or license; actual clearance terms and applicable law still control.
