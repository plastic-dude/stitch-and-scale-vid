# Pass 111 Browser Findings — Retention State

Checked 2026-08-19.

## ICO retention guidance

URL: https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/online-tracking/guidance-for-consumer-internet-of-things-products-and-services/how-long-should-we-keep-personal-information-for/

The ICO page says personal information should not be kept longer than needed for the relevant situation and purpose, should not be kept indefinitely merely in case it may be useful, and that retention periods should be reviewed regularly. It says information should be erased or anonymised when no longer needed for the purpose, while recognizing that other laws may require a different retention period. It also notes that when data is shared with other organisations, arrangements are needed for what happens when sharing is no longer needed.

## NIST media sanitization

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/88/r2/final

NIST SP 800-88 Rev. 2 was published September 2025, with a July 2026 planning note. It defines media sanitization as a process that renders access to target data on media infeasible for a given level of effort and describes a program with applicable techniques and controls for sanitization and disposal based on information sensitivity. This is not evidence that any Stitch & Scale record is deleted or sanitized.

## NARA records schedules

URL: https://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/grs

NARA says General Records Schedules provide disposition authority for common federal records and that agencies generally use schedules or justified agency-specific schedules. This is a federal records-management context, not a universal private-business retention rule and not a Stitch & Scale feature.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may show a fictional retention card with `RECORD / PURPOSE / RETENTION BASIS / KEEP UNTIL / REVIEW AT / ACCESS SCOPE / DISPOSAL METHOD / EXCEPTION / OWNER / STATUS`, labelled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`, and ending at `STATUS: RETENTION UNVERIFIED`. The concept must not claim Stitch & Scale retains, deletes, anonymizes, sanitizes, schedules, or enforces records. It must distinguish retention from withdrawal, erasure, deletion, backup, and legal disposition.
