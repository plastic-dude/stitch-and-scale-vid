## Research synthesis and decision shift

The fresh question is: **When a review finding is handed off, what proves it contains enough context to reproduce and act, rather than being a vague note?** Research changes the creative from a generic “issue” label to an actionable finding card naming `FINDING / SCOPE / EXPECTED / ACTUAL / EVIDENCE / CONTEXT / OWNER / NEXT ACTION`, explicitly illustrative until product proof verifies exact labels. Candidate angle: `actionability-state` — **“Noted is not actionable.”** Visual metaphor: a vague note expands through scope, expected/actual, evidence, context, owner, and next action before entering a work queue. Emotional turn: handoff ambiguity becomes next-action clarity. Final line: “Name what makes the finding actionable.”

The candidate is distinct from `response-state`, which separates a planned response from an enacted response; `reproducibility-state`, which concerns rerunning with the same inputs/version/settings; `closure-state`, which concerns evidence required to close an item; `acknowledgement-state`, which concerns message delivery/seen/acknowledged/action; `assumption-register`, which concerns assumed versus observed; and `context`-adjacent prior work because this concept concerns the minimum handoff data needed for an individual finding to be located and acted upon, not general applicability or source authority.
