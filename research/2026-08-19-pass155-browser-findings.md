# Pass 155 browser findings — applicability-state

**Date/time:** 2026-08-19 21:32 Africa/Lagos  
**Angle under review:** `applicability-state` — working premise: a rule, measurement, review, or warning is not universal until its scope, subject, size, unit, context, and intended use are named. This is a planning angle only; no current Stitch & Scale capability is asserted.

## Official sources reviewed

### Craft Yarn Council — Schematics
URL: https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/schematics

The Council defines a schematic as a visual rendering of a knit or crochet piece with relevant measurements. It describes a schematic as an overview of the piece's size and shape, a reference for determining garment fit, and a template for modifying size. It advises reviewing the schematic before beginning and comparing an existing well-fitting garment with the schematic when deciding whether modifications are needed. This supports a craft-specific proof surface in which a result, warning, or change is tied to a named piece, size, measurement, unit, and intended fit context. It does **not** prove a Stitch & Scale feature, grading result, fit outcome, or automatic applicability decision.

### W3C WCAG 2.2 Understanding SC 1.2.2 — Captions (Prerecorded)
URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html

W3C states that captions are provided for prerecorded audio in synchronized media and that captions include dialogue, identify who is speaking, and include meaningful non-speech information conveyed through sound. This supports a caption map that labels a deliberate speaker and meaningful sound cues without covering the proof card or primary action. It does **not** prescribe a platform-specific safe area or prove current platform UI behavior.

## Distinctness decision

`applicability-state` is being evaluated as distinct from prior `baseline-state`, `divergence-state`, `consistency-state`, `dependency-state`, `version-lock`, `provenance-state`, and `context-state`. The angle concerns the scope of a rule or result — where and to whom it applies — rather than whether a reference exists, two outputs drift, fields agree, a dependency is stale, a version is pinned, origin is traced, or context is attached. It does not reopen `false-completion` or `release-gate-roster`, and remains blocked pending current approved product proof.

## Proposed proof vocabulary

`SUBJECT / RULE_OR_RESULT / SCOPE / SIZE / UNIT / CONTEXT / INTENDED_USE / EXCEPTION / REVIEWED / DISPOSITION`

### NIST SP 800-128 publication page
URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/128/upd1/final

NIST describes SP 800-128 as guidance for organizations managing and administering security of federal information systems and associated operating environments. Its abstract frames configuration management as managing and monitoring configurations to support desired functionality while minimizing risk. This is used only as a boundary-management analogy: a rule or result should be tied to the configuration item and environment for which it was evaluated. It is not product proof and is not a craft-specific standard.

### Meta Business Help Center — text overlays and safe zones for ads
URL: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/980593475366490

Meta defines the safe zone for 9:16 Stories/Reels/Feed ads as the area where important text, logos, and visual elements are less likely to be cropped or covered by interface elements. It advises keeping edges free of key creative elements, and notes that disclaimers on Reels ads should leave the bottom 40% free of text, logos, and other key elements. This is ad-specific guidance, not a universal organic-post rule; exact platform treatment can vary. For pass 155, proof cards and primary actions remain central and captions must not cover them.
