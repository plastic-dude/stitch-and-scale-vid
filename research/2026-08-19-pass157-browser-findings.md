# Pass 157 browser findings — containment-state

**Date/time:** 2026-08-19 21:46 Africa/Lagos  
**Angle under review:** `containment-state` — working premise: a suspect output or record may need to be held from propagation while evidence and human review remain intact. This is a planning angle only; no current Stitch & Scale capability is asserted.

## Official sources reviewed

### NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3 — Incident Response Recommendations
URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final

NIST published SP 800-61 Rev. 3 in April 2025. Its abstract frames incident response as part of cybersecurity risk management and emphasizes improving detection, response, and recovery activities. This supports a planning distinction between detecting a suspect condition, preserving the evidence, limiting further propagation, and later responding or recovering. The publication is cybersecurity-specific and is used only as a process analogy; it does not prove a craft workflow or a Stitch & Scale hold, quarantine, or containment feature.

### Craft Yarn Council — Standards and Guidelines for Crochet and Knitting
URL: https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards

The Council says publishers, fiber, needle, hook, and yarn members developed guidelines and symbols to bring uniformity to labeling and patterns, with the goal of helping industry prepare consumer-friendly products and consumers select materials and complete projects. This supports a fictional visual in which a suspect variant is held with its evidence and labels intact instead of being silently propagated. It does **not** establish that the product detects variants, freezes records, quarantines exports, or guarantees consumer success.

## Distinctness decision

`containment-state` is being evaluated as distinct from recent `escalation-state`, `applicability-state`, `baseline-state`, `divergence-state`, `backup-state`, `closure-state`, and imported `false-completion` / `release-gate-roster`. The angle concerns preventing a suspect result from propagating while evidence is preserved; it is not routing responsibility, bounding applicability, identifying a baseline, comparing outputs, restoring backups, declaring closure, or verifying a human-gate roster.

## Proposed proof vocabulary

`SUSPECT_OUTPUT / REASON_FOR_HOLD / EVIDENCE_SNAPSHOT / PROPAGATION_BLOCKED / REVIEW_QUEUE / OWNER / REVIEW_WINDOW / RELEASE_CONDITION / RELEASED_OR_RETAINED`

### W3C WCAG 2.2 — Captions (Prerecorded)
URL: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/captions-prerecorded.html

W3C states that prerecorded synchronized media captions include dialogue, identify who is speaking, and include non-speech information conveyed through sound, including meaningful sound effects. The containment treatment therefore reserves a caption lane, uses one narrator family, labels meaningful hold/release sounds only when they convey information, and requires synchronized caption-on and caption-free review. W3C accessibility guidance does not prove any product caption or containment behavior.

### TikTok for Business — Auction In-Feed Ads

TikTok's page was last updated in June 2026. It recommends 9:16 vertical video at least 540×960 pixels for non-Spark ads and says safe-zone size depends on dimension, ad-caption length, and additional formats. It also warns that preview and live versions may differ because preview is not device-specific. The source is ad-specific, not a universal organic-post rule; the package keeps the held proof card central and requires current platform-profile review.

## Updated research decision

The research changed the treatment from “flag and route” to **hold and preserve**: a suspect output is visibly held from propagation with its reason, evidence snapshot, review queue, owner, review window, release condition, and released-or-retained disposition still attached. Containment is the distinct creative question; escalation may follow later, but is not the story's proof surface.
