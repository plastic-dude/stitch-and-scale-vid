## NASA Systems Engineering Handbook, SEH 4.0 System Design Processes

URL: https://www.nasa.gov/reference/4-0-system-design-processes/

NASA describes system design as iterative and recursive, beginning with stakeholder expectations that include mission objectives, constraints, design drivers, operational objectives, and success criteria. It emphasizes that complete requirements traceability is critical for validation, that clear and unambiguous requirements reduce misunderstanding during changes, and that decisions made during the original design concept should be documented in the technical data package so future changes can be assessed against the original design philosophy and negotiation results.

The source is general systems engineering guidance, updated on the page in 2019; it does not prove a Stitch & Scale feature or a knitwear-specific data model.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may focus on hidden assumptions behind a derived result: record the assumption, source/owner, scope, date/version, and whether it is observed, recorded, estimated, or pending. The visual must not claim the app identifies or validates assumptions. It must preserve calculation, technical edit, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting as separate human gates.
