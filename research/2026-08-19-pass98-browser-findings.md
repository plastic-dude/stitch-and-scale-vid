# Pass 98 Browser Findings — Applicability Scope

Checked 2026-08-19.

## NIST validation glossary

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/validation

NIST defines validation as confirmation through objective evidence that requirements for a **specific intended use or application** have been fulfilled. The glossary also describes validation as determining that an object or process is acceptable according to predefined tests and their results. The phrase “specific intended use or application” is the key boundary for this concept: evidence that supports one use does not silently prove every other use.

This is general validation terminology, not proof of a Stitch & Scale feature, product quality, or knitwear outcome.

## Craft Yarn Council — Standard Body Measurements/Sizing

URL: https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/body-sizing

The Craft Yarn Council says knitwear sizing uses body measurements, finished garment measurements, schematics, and ease. It states that fit and length charts are guidelines, that individual changes may alter a project’s appearance, and that finished garment sizing information should be reviewed before beginning. The source distinguishes actual body measurements from finished measurements and ease choices.

This is sizing guidance, not a fit guarantee and not proof of a product’s calculation or recommendation behavior.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may record `INTENDED USE / SUBJECT / SCOPE / INPUTS / EVIDENCE / RESULT / LIMIT / STATUS` so a valid check is not mistaken for an applicable answer. The record must remain illustrative until current product proof exists and must not claim Stitch & Scale validates intended use or replaces technical editing, print review, chart review, schematic review, or test knitting.
