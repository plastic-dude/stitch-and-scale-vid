# Pass 99 Browser Findings — Provenance Chain

Checked 2026-08-19.

## W3C PROV-DM

URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/

W3C defines provenance as information about entities, activities, and agents involved in producing a piece of data or thing, useful for assessing quality, reliability, and trustworthiness. PROV-DM distinguishes entities, activities, derivations, agents, and time. It describes activities as using and producing entities, agents as bearing responsibility, and derivation as a transformation, update, or construction of one entity from another. The specification also cautions that a usage/generation chain is necessary but not by itself sufficient to establish derivation; some influence must be determined.

This is a domain-agnostic provenance model, not proof of a Stitch & Scale data-lineage feature or a knitwear result.

## NIST data provenance glossary

URL: https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/data_provenance

NIST describes data provenance as the method of generation, transmission, and storage of information used to trace the origin of processed information; in the cited computer/law-enforcement context it is equivalent to chain of custody.

The source is context-specific and does not establish a product behavior or legal chain-of-custody claim for Stitch & Scale.

## NASA Software Engineering Handbook — bidirectional traceability

URL: https://swehb.nasa.gov/plugins/viewsource/viewpagesrc.action?pageId=215777306

NASA guidance requires and explains bidirectional traceability across software requirements, design, implementation, verification, non-conformances, and related elements. It describes traceability as a way to find missing or extra links, support verification and validation, enable change impact analysis, improve communication, and preserve source-to-result relationships. The guidance also says traceability provides objective evidence of compliance in its NASA context.

This is NASA software-engineering guidance, not evidence of Stitch & Scale implementation, product assurance, or publication approval.

## Research implication

A fresh concept may show a source citation that is insufficient without a chain: source/entity, extraction or observation, transformation/activity, responsible agent/tool, version/date, output/result, reviewer, and status. It must not claim the app maintains provenance or that a source alone proves a result.
