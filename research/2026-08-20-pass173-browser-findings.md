# Pass 173 Browser / Source Findings — Handoff State

**Research question:** When a file or record leaves one person's workspace, what makes it a usable handoff rather than an export with no recipient, context, required action, or receipt?

**Selected angle:** `handoff-state` — Exported is not handed off.

## NICE structured handover

NICE's quality statement says structured handovers occur during transitions of care and describes complete, up-to-date information, clearly defined roles and responsibilities, monitoring or investigation plans, ongoing tasks, communication needs, and actions for the receiving provider. The pass-173 use is a general information-design analogy for a craft workflow handoff, not clinical advice or proof that Stitch & Scale implements a structured handoff.

Source: [NICE Quality Statement 4: Structured Patient Handovers](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/qs174/chapter/quality-statement-4-structured-patient-handovers).

## NCBI/NICE guideline evidence

The NCBI Bookshelf chapter on structured handovers defines handover as transferring responsibility and information between professionals. It warns that incomplete or delayed information can compromise safety, quality, and experience, and describes structured formats, tasks still to do, and the importance of adapting structure to context. The chapter also notes that a handover is not simply imparting information; it must be useful to the receiving context. The evidence is healthcare-specific and cannot be generalized into a product outcome claim.

Source: [NCBI Bookshelf — Structured Patient Handovers](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK564933/).

## GOV.UK service guidance

The GOV.UK Service Manual organizes service work across accessibility, delivery, technology, team roles, user research, and service assessments. The pass-173 use is to require an explicit recipient context and accessible handoff review; it does not prove that Stitch & Scale has a delivery workflow, recipient tracking, or service-assessment feature.

Source: [GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual).

## Research decision

The research changes the concept from “the file was exported” to “the receiving person has the context, version, required action, accessibility needs, rights boundary, and receipt state needed to continue.” The fictional proof surface is `HANDOFF H-001 / SENDER / RECIPIENT / CONTEXT / VERSION / REQUIRED_ACTION / OPEN_ITEMS / RIGHTS / ACCESS_NEEDS / RECEIPT / DISPOSITION`.

No current product capture or proof-registry record was supplied. No product claim may be made about recipient tracking, handoff status, read receipt, task assignment, or export behavior.
