# Pass 177 Browser / Source Findings — Scope of Proof State

**Research question:** When proof is valid for one version, size, gauge, context, or audience, what prevents it from being generalized beyond the conditions actually tested?

**Selected angle:** `scope-of-proof-state` — Proof in one scope is not proof everywhere.

## NIST AI RMF 1.0

NIST's AI RMF describes trustworthy AI characteristics including validity and reliability and frames risk-management work around intended goals, design, development, use, and evaluation. The pass-177 use is a fictional scope card that names version, size, gauge, context, audience, test conditions, and exclusions. It does not claim that Stitch & Scale implements the AI RMF, performs validity testing, or guarantees generalizability.

Source: [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf).

## NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5

NIST SP 800-53A provides customizable procedures for assessing controls within systems and organizations and says assessment procedures can be tailored to risk tolerance and scope. The pass-177 use is to motivate an explicit assessment scope, not to claim that Stitch & Scale runs NIST assessments, has control evidence, or has a risk authorization process.

Source: [NIST SP 800-53A Rev. 5](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/a/r5/final).

## Craft Yarn Council gauge guidance

Craft Yarn Council guidance explains that gauge depends on stitches and rows per inch, that a pattern's specified gauge should be achieved for the intended size, and that a swatch may need adjustment when the measured result differs. It supports the craft-specific distinction between a proof at one gauge/size and a universal claim. It does not prove that Stitch & Scale calculates, validates, grades, or guarantees gauge or fit.

Source: [Craft Yarn Council — Gauge and Why It Is Important](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/blog/ydkwydk-gauge-and-why-its-important).

## Research decision

The research changes the concept from “proof exists” to “proof is declared for a specific version, size, gauge, context, audience, test condition, exclusion, reviewer, and disposition.” The fictional proof surface is `PROOF P-001 / VALID_FOR / VERSION / SIZE / GAUGE / CONTEXT / AUDIENCE / TEST_CONDITIONS / EXCLUSIONS / REVIEWED / DISPOSITION`.

No current product capture or proof-registry record was supplied. No product claim may be made about generalizability, validity, reliability, assessment scope, grading, fit, gauge, or universal behavior.
