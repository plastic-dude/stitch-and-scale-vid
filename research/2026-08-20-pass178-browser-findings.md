# Pass 178 Browser / Source Findings — Replication State

**Research question:** Can another person independently reproduce or replicate a result from the preserved inputs, steps, environment, expected output, and reviewer evidence, or did it work only once for the original operator?

**Selected angle:** `replication-state` — Worked once is not independently replicable.

## NIST terminology

NIST TN 1297 distinguishes repeatability as agreement under the same measurement conditions from reproducibility as agreement under changed conditions, and notes that a valid reproducibility statement must specify which conditions changed. The pass-178 use is a fictional replication record, not evidence that Stitch & Scale performs metrological studies or guarantees measurement results.

Source: [NIST TN 1297 Appendix D.1 Terminology](https://www.nist.gov/pml/nist-technical-note-1297/nist-tn-1297-appendix-d1-terminology).

## ACM artifact review and badging

ACM's artifact-review guidance distinguishes repeatability, reproducibility, and replicability and describes artifact qualities such as documented, consistent, complete, and exercisable. It also treats independent review and external replication as separate from author claims. The pass-178 use is a fictional evidence package and reviewer boundary, not evidence that Stitch & Scale has ACM badges, public artifacts, external reproduction, or validated results.

Source: [ACM Artifact Review and Badging](https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-badging).

## Craft Yarn Council instructions

Craft Yarn Council guidance recommends reading the full relevant instructions, performing what they say, and using a gauge swatch with the specified yarn, needles, and pattern stitch before beginning. This supports the craft-specific distinction between an author's successful run and another person's independently repeatable run. It does not prove that Stitch & Scale provides complete instructions, checks gauge, or guarantees fit.

Source: [Craft Yarn Council — Reading Instructions & Other Basics](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/read_instructions.html).

## Research decision

The research changes the concept from “the result happened” to “another reviewer can exercise the same declared procedure from preserved inputs, steps, environment, expected output, exclusions, and reviewer evidence.” The fictional proof surface is `REPLICATION R-001 / INPUTS / STEPS / ENVIRONMENT / EXPECTED_OUTPUT / ACTUAL_OUTPUT / DELTA / REPLICATOR / CONDITIONS_CHANGED / REVIEWED / DISPOSITION`.
