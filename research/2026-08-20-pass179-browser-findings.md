# Pass 179 Browser / Source Findings — Assumption State

**Research question:** Which assumptions about yarn, gauge, size, audience, data, or operating conditions are silently driving a result, and which of them need to be named and reviewed before anyone relies on it?

**Selected angle:** `assumption-state` — An unstated assumption is an unreviewed input.

## NIST AI RMF 1.0

The NIST AI RMF identifies assumptions and related limitations about system purposes, uses, and risks as matters to document across an AI or product lifecycle. The pass-179 use is a fictional assumption card, not evidence that Stitch & Scale implements AI RMF, tracks assumptions, or provides formal risk assurance.

Source: [NIST AI RMF 1.0](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf).

## W3C Data Quality Vocabulary

W3C's Data Quality Vocabulary provides a framework for describing data quality so a user can judge fitness for purpose; it emphasizes quality measurements, annotations, policies, certificates, and provenance of quality metadata. The pass-179 use is to motivate explicit assumptions and reviewers around a fictional craft-business record, not to claim that Stitch & Scale implements DQV, quality metrics, provenance, or fitness assessment.

Source: [W3C Data Quality Vocabulary](https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dqv/).

## Craft Yarn Council yarn-weight guidance

Craft Yarn Council labels yarn-weight gauge ranges as guidelines and notes that a pattern's stated gauge should be followed, supporting the distinction between a convenient category assumption and a checked project-specific condition. The pass-179 use is limited to the fictional assumption card; it does not prove that Stitch & Scale computes yarn weight, gauge, fit, or pattern correctness.

Source: [Craft Yarn Council — Standard Yarn Weight System](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/yarn-weight-system).

## Research decision

The research changes the concept from “the value appears” to “the assumptions driving this value are named, tested, assigned, and reviewed.” The fictional proof surface is `ASSUMPTION A-001 / INPUT / VALUE / BASIS / IMPACTED_OUTPUT / CHECK_METHOD / OWNER / REVIEWED / STATUS / DISPOSITION`.

No current product capture or proof-registry record was supplied. No product claim may be made about assumption tracking, data quality, fitness for purpose, provenance, yarn-weight interpretation, gauge, fit, or correctness.
