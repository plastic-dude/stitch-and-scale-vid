# Pass 209 Wide Research — Decision Rationale State

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Director role:** Planning only; no video generation or upload  
**Research question:** How should a decision record the reasoning that connects applicable evidence to a bounded disposition, so another reviewer can inspect the basis without mistaking the decision for universal proof?

## Decision changed by research

Pass 208 tested whether qualified evidence applies to the exact claim, user, environment, scope, sample, version, and assumptions. Pass 209 adds a rationale record: identify the applicable evidence, explain the inference or reasoning that links it to the decision, state the alternatives or uncertainty considered, name the decision owner, and keep the disposition bounded. The fresh angle is `decision-rationale-state`, focused on the visible reasoning between evidence applicability and a decision, not merely the existence or applicability of the evidence.

The film will not claim that Stitch & Scale currently records rationale, enables audit, supports review, or makes decisions explainable. It will use original neutral cards labeled `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

## Source findings

### 1. GOV.UK — transparent evidence should let others check the reasoning

The UK Civil Service Quarterly describes the value of publishing supporting evidence and data so others can check and interrogate the analysis, consider alternative interpretations, bring further evidence, and challenge the reasoning. It also describes a transparency framework that asks for a clear problem diagnosis, evidence behind proposals, implementation or value-for-money evidence, and how the policy will be evaluated and adapted when evidence is limited. [1]

**Decision impact:** The visual record will separate `APPLICABLE_EVIDENCE`, `INFERENCE_OR_REASONING`, `ALTERNATIVES_CONSIDERED`, `UNCERTAINTY`, `DECISION_OWNER`, and `BOUNDED_DISPOSITION`. The decision will not be shown as self-justifying merely because evidence is attached.

### 2. NIST RMF — risk decisions sit within a repeatable, documented process

NIST describes the Risk Management Framework as a comprehensive, flexible, repeatable, and measurable seven-step process for managing information-security and privacy risk, with authorization and continuous monitoring as part of the process. This is security-risk guidance, not a Stitch & Scale product contract, and it does not establish that the app has risk authorization or monitoring features. [2]

**Decision impact:** The treatment will present rationale as a bounded planning record with owner, scope, conditions, and review trigger, not as a universal approval or a claim of automated authorization.

### 3. W3C — evaluation reports need scope, reviewers, methods, dates, results, and follow-up

W3C’s accessibility evaluation report template recommends identifying the product and purpose, scope and URLs, dates, reviewers and expertise, evaluation and validation tools, manual review process, detailed results, recommendations, and ongoing monitoring. It also notes that a website may change after the evaluation date. [3]

**Decision impact:** The rationale surface will keep evidence, method, reviewer, date/version, finding, limitation, recommendation, and follow-up distinct. The film will not claim WCAG conformance, universal accessibility, or product-wide coverage.

### 4. Craft Yarn Council — a decision depends on the instructions and checks actually performed

Craft Yarn Council advises reading the relevant pattern portion fully, doing what the instructions say, and using the specified yarn, needles, and pattern stitch for gauge. It describes gauge as essential to successful fit. [4]

**Decision impact:** Rationale will not collapse calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting. A decision about one checked material or instruction cannot be presented as proof for all craft gates.

## Audience and workflow relevance

The primary audience remains independent knitwear designers and pattern publishers coordinating calculations, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, test knitting, samples, swatches, revisions, tester feedback, submissions, and intended users. The planning hypothesis is that teams often retain an evidence item and a decision but lose the reasoning, alternatives, scope, or uncertainty that made the decision defensible. No current user study, incident report, product telemetry, or product-proof registry entry verifies this as a measured Stitch & Scale outcome.

David is a developer whose late mother knitted. He does not knit and must not be described as a knitter, knitwear designer, pattern designer, or technical editor. The film teaches rationale boundaries and does not claim personal craft practice.

## Platform, accessibility, and visual opportunity

The planned output remains a 9:16, 45-second mobile-first social explainer with destination platform unconfirmed. The visual sequence will use readable inserts for evidence ID, applicable evidence, inference or reasoning, alternatives considered, uncertainty, decision owner, scope, conditions, review trigger, bounded disposition, and follow-up. Captions must not cover hands, cards, labels, numbers, logos, controls, menus, or primary actions. Caption-on and caption-free review remain required. Exact safe-area and current caption behavior are not verified until a destination is named.

The visual opportunity is an applicable evidence card entering a rationale bridge. The bridge has a visible missing segment labeled `REASONING NOT RECORDED`, so the decision card remains held rather than jumping from evidence to approval. The emotional turn is inspectable confidence: show how you got there.

## Research limitations

GOV.UK transparency writing is policy and public-sector guidance and does not establish Stitch & Scale product behavior. NIST RMF concerns information-security and privacy risk management and does not establish an app authorization feature. W3C’s report template concerns accessibility evaluation reporting and does not establish product conformance.
