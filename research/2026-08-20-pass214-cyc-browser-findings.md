# Browser Findings — Craft Yarn Council — Pass 214 Research Support

**Source:** https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/industry.html  
**Accessed:** 2026-08-20  
**Retrieval:** Official Craft Yarn Council designer-guideline page, opened and reviewed in the browser.

## Evidence captured

The page says designers should reconfirm yarn and color selection with an editor or design director and check all ball bands to verify the same dye-lot number. It also says designers should confirm yarn availability and the requested sample size before beginning a project.

The page requires a gauge swatch and says the gauge swatch must match the project gauge; it warns that companies will reject a submission if the swatch does not match the stated project gauge. It separately asks for project instructions, materials and yarn amounts, measurements for each size, a schematic, and an invoice.

The page says company or publication-specific instructions also apply. Its guidance is therefore useful evidence of a craft-industry workflow boundary, not a universal substitution rule and not current product proof for Stitch & Scale.

## Decision relevance

This supports a fresh question about **substitution authorization and disclosure** rather than only substitution fit: a substitute may appear to match a yarn weight or gauge while the editor or design director’s confirmation, dye-lot record, availability check, swatch match, and disclosure status remain unrecorded. The proposed concept must preserve test knitting, calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, and schematic scale as separate human evidence gates.

## Boundaries

Do not claim that Stitch & Scale currently verifies yarn compatibility, detects substitutions, approves materials, checks dye lots, predicts availability, validates gauge, or replaces an editor, calculator, technical editor, test knitter, or publisher. Do not use real yarn labels, private submissions, contracts, or company names without rights clearance. Any illustrative card must say `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.
