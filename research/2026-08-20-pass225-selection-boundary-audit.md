## Selection decision

The selected angle is `dye-lot-state`. The fresh question is: **When a knitwear sample uses multiple skeins, what proves the material and dye-lot identity attached to the design rather than relying on a generic yarn name, a photograph, or memory?**

The concept is materially distinct from `export-identity-state`, which concerns whether a released file is the same reviewed artifact. It is narrower than `material-trace`: the pass focuses on the contradiction **same color is not the same lot** and the gap between a loose label and a project-linked lot record, without repeating broad material provenance. It does not claim material availability, substitution authorization, sample custody, care suitability, gauge, color continuity, fiber behavior, or publication approval.

The proposed proof surface is `MATERIAL M-001 / DESIGN_ID / SAMPLE_ID / MATERIAL_NAME / BRAND_OR_SOURCE / FIBER_OR_CONTENT / COLOR_NAME / COLOR_CODE / LOT_OR_DYE_LOT / LABEL_REFERENCE / QUANTITY / UNIT / ACQUIRED_AT / USED_FROM / SUBSTITUTE_FLAG / OBSERVED_DIFFERENCE / CHECKED_AT / SOURCE_OR_BASIS / REVIEWER / REVIEWED_AT / STATUS`.

The pass remains **blocked** because no current product-proof registry or approved capture verifies this exact surface, the destination platform is unconfirmed, and rights, narrator, captions, audio, privacy, and final acceptance evidence are incomplete. Do not generate or upload.
