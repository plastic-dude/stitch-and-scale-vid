## Fresh research question

When a craft workflow decision mixes recorded facts, assumptions, references, and human judgment, what evidence keeps the decision from being mistaken for a settled fact or approval?

This question is narrower than `record-purpose-state`, which concerns why a record exists and which workflow stage it represents. Pass 230 focuses on the **basis of a decision**: what was observed, what was derived, what was assumed, who decided, what remains open, and which human gate still applies.

## Research synthesis

CYC’s current guidance explicitly separates what a designer observes and records from later project materials and review: yarn/color selection, dye lot, sample size, notes, gauge, instructions, schematic, labels, invoice, and return shipping are not interchangeable evidence. [1] Its idea guidance separates idea, sample, acceptance, contract, and review. [2] Knitty separates technical editing, test knitting, charts, schematics, photographs, rights, and publication submission. [3] Atlassian’s DACI template provides a category example of documenting decision roles, but roles do not prove decision correctness. [4]

Together, these sources support a bounded question: **what was observed, what source or standard was consulted, what was derived, what was assumed, who decided, and what remains open?** A decision card should make its basis legible without presenting a rationale as a verified outcome.

The visual metaphor should be a fictional `DECISION` card that first reads `BASIS: UNKNOWN`, then separates `OBSERVATION`, `SOURCE`, `DERIVATION`, `ASSUMPTION`, `DECISION_OWNER`, `OPEN_QUESTION`, and `STATUS`. Separate human-gate cards remain visible for technical editing, calculation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, care review, test knitting, and publication approval. The final state remains `DECISION BASIS: UNVERIFIED` because no current Stitch & Scale decision surface is supplied.

## Decision

Select the new angle **`decision-basis-state`**. It is materially distinct from `record-purpose-state`, which concerns why a record exists and which workflow stage it represents. Pass 230 concerns the **evidence and roles beneath a craft workflow decision**, separating observation, source, derivation, assumption, decision owner, approver/gate, open question, and status. It is distinct from `source-basis-state`, `confidence-state`, `uncertainty-state`, `owner-state`, `review-basis-state`, `publication-boundary-state`, and the imported blocked concepts.

The proposed hook is: **“A decision is not its explanation until the basis is visible.”** The visual metaphor is a fictional `DECISION` card whose rationale opens into observation, source, derivation, assumption, owner, open question, and status. The final line is: **“Name what you saw, what you assumed, and who still needs to review it.”** These are bounded creative lines, not product claims.

The fictional proof surface, if a current product record is later supplied, must distinguish: `DECISION D-001 / DESIGN_ID / DECISION_PURPOSE / OBSERVATION / SOURCE_OR_REFERENCE / DERIVATION_OR_FORMULA / ASSUMPTION / DECISION_OWNER / APPROVER_OR_GATE / CONTRIBUTORS / INFORMED / OPEN_QUESTION / DECIDED_AT / STATUS / LIMITATIONS`. A decision-basis record does not prove the underlying observation, formula, technical correctness, test-knit success, publication approval, financial/tax treatment, or user outcome.
