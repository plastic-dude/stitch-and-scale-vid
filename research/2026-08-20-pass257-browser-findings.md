# Pass 257 Wide Research — Price Sensitivity State

**Research date:** 2026-08-20
**Pass:** 257
**Question:** When a projected price changes because one input is estimated, variable, or incomplete, what should an independent knitwear designer record before treating the number as a final business fact?

## Decision summary

The research changes the concept from a generic price or cost ledger to a sharper **input-sensitivity boundary**. A total can look precise while one material, labor, overhead, fee, volume, or sales assumption is estimated or moves independently. A trustworthy workflow should show which input changed, whether it is recorded or estimated, how the result moved, and what remains a designer decision.

The planned illustrative proof surface is a `PRICE SENSITIVITY` card with `OUTPUT`, `INPUT CHANGED`, `OLD VALUE`, `NEW VALUE`, `SOURCE`, `RECORDED / ESTIMATED / PLANNED`, `EFFECT ON RESULT`, `ASSUMPTION`, and `DECISION OWNER`. This is not a pricing recommendation, tax advice, financial forecast, or current Stitch & Scale UI. It will carry `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` and remain blocked.

## Audience and craft-workflow interpretation

For an independent designer, the pain is not simply “pricing is hard.” It is the false finality of a number whose most influential input is hidden. A sample, tech-editing fee, marketplace fee, photographer, labor estimate, yarn quantity, expected sales volume, or wholesale split can change the result without changing the label on the total. The most useful short is therefore a controlled reveal: one input moves, the output moves, and the card refuses to turn the result into a final decision.

The campaign keeps the standing founding-tester objective. It does not attach to a holiday, conference, shipping cutoff, seasonal sales event, or deadline. No urgency or market outcome is inferred from the research.

## Product-truth reconciliation

No current Stitch & Scale product-proof registry entry or approved non-private capture verifies a price-sensitivity record, input-delta view, source field, recorded-versus-estimated state, formula provenance, assumption label, or decision-owner control. Therefore the package is **blocked**. The paper card is not current product UI and must not show invented prices, currencies, fee rates, margins, revenue, adoption, savings, or profitability.

The concept must not claim that Stitch & Scale automatically prices work, predicts profitability, gives tax advice, chooses a price, reconciles transactions, or replaces an accountant. A recorded expense, derived estimate, planned cost, and designer assumption remain separate states. Technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting remain separate human evidence gates.

## Rights, visual opportunity, and asset decisions

Use an original paper card, one pencil, one neutral calculator-shaped prop with no numeric display, and one ruler only as a visual metaphor. Do not use a real accounting screen, payment processor, marketplace dashboard, customer record, invoice, tax document, private financial record, third-party spreadsheet, branded platform interface, or copyrighted music. If a current product capture is ever supplied, verify version, destination, privacy, owner, rights, and exact labels before using it.

The visual opportunity is a split card: the left side holds a stable `OUTPUT` label; the right side swaps only `INPUT CHANGED`. The output marker moves, then a red line reads `NOT FINAL — DECISION NEEDED`. This lets the audience see sensitivity without making a numeric claim.

## Platform, caption, audio, and acceptance decisions

Use a mobile-native 9:16 master under 45 seconds with one founder narrator, one microphone family, one room tone, and one paper-placement sound motif. TikTok’s current creative guidance supports vertical 9:16 framing, safe-zone awareness, early hooks, captions/text overlays, and clear CTAs, but current account-specific behavior must be rechecked before any approval.[7] YouTube’s caption guidance supports a human-reviewed subtitle track and caption editing.[6] Meta guidance could not be fully extracted in this run and is treated as an open platform check, not a claim.[8]

Captions may not cover the card, pencil, hands, labels, values, status, logo, buttons, menus, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions independently. The card must remain legible with audio muted and captions disabled. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported money claims, unlicensed audio, and dense overlays.

## Research conclusion

The evidence supports the materially distinct angle `price-sensitivity-state`: show that one changing or estimated input can move a derived price outcome, then preserve the human decision boundary. It does not authorize any Stitch & Scale finance feature, pricing recommendation, financial outcome, tax treatment, or exact formula. The package remains blocked until current product proof, rights, platform, caption, audio, and acceptance evidence exist.
