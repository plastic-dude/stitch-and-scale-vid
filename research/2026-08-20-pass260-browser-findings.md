# Pass 260 Wide Research — Evidence Consequence State

**Research date:** 2026-08-20
**Pass:** 260
**Question:** What decision can one piece of evidence actually support, and where must that evidence stop before a pattern, publication, accessibility, or product claim is made?

## Decision summary

The research changes the concept from generic evidence collection or test-knit coverage to a sharper **evidence-consequence boundary**. A calculation, technical edit, reader observation, test knit, accessibility evaluation, or product capture can support a bounded question, but none automatically proves every size, device, locale, user, publication outcome, or product capability.

The planned illustrative proof surface is an `EVIDENCE CONSEQUENCE` card with `EVIDENCE TYPE`, `OBSERVED / CALCULATED / REVIEWED`, `SCOPE`, `SAMPLE`, `QUESTION SUPPORTED`, `QUESTION NOT SUPPORTED`, `DECISION OWNER`, and `STATUS`. This is not a current Stitch & Scale evidence registry or product UI. It will carry `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` and remain blocked.

## Audience and craft-workflow interpretation

The audience pain is not lack of evidence in the abstract. It is the common shortcut from “someone checked something” to “the whole pattern or product is proven.” A technical edit, a test knit, a size sample, a reader report, a calculation, or an accessibility audit may be valuable while still being bounded by its question, scope, sample, method, and conditions.

The standing campaign objective remains recruiting founding testers through the free demo and early-access cohort. No event, seasonal deadline, competitor comparison, testimonial, urgency hook, adoption statement, or customer outcome is adopted. The research does not support a claim that any Stitch & Scale user experienced a false-completion event or that the product currently maps evidence consequences.

## Product-truth reconciliation

No current Stitch & Scale product-proof registry entry or approved non-private capture verifies an evidence-consequence record, evidence-type field, scope, sample, supported-question field, not-supported-question field, decision owner, or human status. Therefore the package is **blocked**. The paper card is not current product UI and must not be presented as a verified evidence registry.

The concept must not claim that Stitch & Scale automatically decides what evidence proves, replaces technical editors or test knitters, grades every size, validates accessibility, guarantees publication readiness, or turns one observation into universal accuracy. Calculation, record, technical edit, reader observation, accessibility evaluation, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, localization review, and test knitting remain separate evidence gates.

## Rights, visual opportunity, and asset decisions

Use an original paper card, one neutral pencil, one blank “observation” strip, and one blank “calculation” strip with no real pattern content, user data, reviewer name, size, result, or product label. Do not use a real pattern, chart, schematic, customer document, private review, tester message, accessibility report, third-party logo, or copyrighted music. If a current product capture is ever supplied, verify version, destination, privacy, owner, rights, evidence scope, sample, and exact labels before use.

The visual opportunity is a single evidence card split into two columns: `QUESTION SUPPORTED` and `QUESTION NOT SUPPORTED`. A pencil tries to drag one small observation across a thick boundary; the hand stops it and writes `DECISION OWNER: HUMAN`. The emotional turn is from proof accumulation to proof humility.

## Platform, caption, audio, and acceptance decisions

Use a mobile-native 9:16 master under 45 seconds with one founder narrator, one microphone family, one room tone, and one pencil-on-paper sound motif. Captions may not cover the card, columns, evidence labels, hands, scope, sample, buttons, menus, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions independently. The supported/not-supported distinction must remain understandable with audio muted and captions disabled. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported accuracy or capability claims, unlicensed audio, and dense overlays.

## Research conclusion

The evidence supports the materially distinct angle `evidence-consequence-state`: show that evidence is useful only when its scope and consequence are explicit, then stop before making a larger claim. It does not authorize a Stitch & Scale evidence registry, automatic QA judgment, universal pattern accuracy, accessibility conformance, publication readiness, or product capability claim. The package remains blocked until current product proof, rights, platform, caption, audio, and acceptance evidence exist.
