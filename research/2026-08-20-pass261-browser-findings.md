# Pass 261 Wide Research — Labor Record State

**Research date:** 2026-08-20
**Pass:** 261
**Question:** How should an independent designer distinguish completed, estimated, donated, and deferred work in a craft-business record without turning a short video into tax, wage, payroll, or profitability advice?

## Decision summary

The research changes the concept from broad hidden-labor visibility to a sharper **work-event state**. Labor can be visible while its record status is still unclear: a task may be completed, estimated, planned, donated, deferred, or unverified. A work event should preserve its task, date, source, role, and status without silently converting it into a wage, payroll liability, cost, price, profit, tax position, or settled business fact.

The planned illustrative proof surface is a `WORK EVENT` card with `DESIGN / TASK / ROLE / DATE / STATE / SOURCE / QUANTITY OR HOURS / COMPENSATION STATE / NOTES / DECISION OWNER`. This is not a current Stitch & Scale labor ledger or product UI. It will carry `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` and remain blocked.

## Audience and craft-business interpretation

The audience pain is not simply that labor is invisible. It is that a designer may remember that work happened while losing whether the event was completed, estimated, planned, donated, deferred, or still unverified. The business record should preserve the event and its source without silently rewriting a work note into an accounting conclusion. This supports honest business math while keeping payroll, tax, wage, profitability, and final-price decisions outside the film.

The standing campaign objective remains recruiting founding testers through the free demo and early-access cohort. No event, seasonal deadline, competitor comparison, testimonial, urgency hook, adoption statement, customer outcome, wage rate, currency, or profit result is adopted. The research does not support a claim that Stitch & Scale currently records labor, values unpaid work, calculates wages, or manages payroll.

## Product-truth reconciliation

No current Stitch & Scale product-proof registry entry or approved non-private capture verifies a work-event record, task, role, date, state, source, hours or quantity, compensation state, notes, or decision-owner surface. Therefore the package is **blocked**. The paper card is not current product UI and must not be presented as a labor ledger, payroll screen, tax record, price calculator, profitability analysis, or accounting system.

The concept must not claim that Stitch & Scale automatically values unpaid labor, calculates wages, determines compensation, handles payroll, gives tax advice, proves profitability, or sets final prices. Expense records, estimates, sales rollups, technical editing, test knitting, samples, publication work, and labor events remain distinct record or human-review gates.

## Rights, visual opportunity, and asset decisions

Use an original paper `WORK EVENT` card, one neutral pencil, and three blank state strips labelled `RECORDED`, `ESTIMATED`, and `PLANNED`. Do not use a real time sheet, invoice, payroll screen, customer record, private business document, currency, wage, tax form, third-party logo, or copyrighted music. If a current product capture is ever supplied, verify version, destination, privacy, owner, rights, exact labels, data source, and state semantics before use.

The visual opportunity is a single work card where a pencil tries to turn `ESTIMATED` into `RECORDED`; the hand stops it and adds `SOURCE: NOTE / DATE: REVIEW / COMPENSATION: NOT DECIDED`. The emotional turn is from “the work happened” to “the record still needs a state.”

## Platform, caption, audio, and acceptance decisions

Use a mobile-native 9:16 master under 45 seconds with one founder narrator, one microphone family, one room tone, and one pencil-on-paper sound motif. Captions may not cover the card, state strips, hands, task, date, source, notes, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions independently. The recorded/estimated/planned distinction must remain understandable with audio muted and captions disabled. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported finance claims, unlicensed audio, and dense overlays.

## Research conclusion

The evidence supports the materially distinct angle `labor-record-state`: show that visible work still needs a truthful event state and source before it becomes a business conclusion. It does not authorize a Stitch & Scale labor ledger, wage, payroll, tax, profitability, final-pricing, or compensation capability claim. The package remains blocked until current product proof, rights, platform, caption, audio, and acceptance evidence exist.
