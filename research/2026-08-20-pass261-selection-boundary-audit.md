## Selection result

`labor-record-state` was selected after refreshing the published branch and inspecting the 271 occupied ledger rows. No row used this exact slug. It is reserved once in pass 261 and does not reuse or rename any prior filename.

The fresh question was: **How should an independent designer distinguish completed, estimated, donated, and deferred work in a craft-business record without turning a short video into tax, wage, payroll, or profitability advice?** The research changed the concept from broad hidden-labor visibility to a visible work-event state with source, date, task, role, and compensation-state boundaries.

## Nearest prior and material difference

The nearest prior is `labor-visibility-state`, with adjacent `contribution-scope`, `price-basis`, `price-sensitivity-state`, `payment-state`, `cost-allocation-state`, `expense-state`, `work-scope`, and `sample-context`. Those concepts concern whether labor is visible, what a contribution means, how pricing is based, what payment state exists, how costs are allocated, what an expense record means, the scope of work, or a sample context. Pass 261 asks whether a specific work event is recorded, estimated, planned, donated, deferred, or unverified before a business conclusion is made.

| Dimension | Nearest prior territory | Pass 261 `labor-record-state` |
|---|---|---|
| Changed pain | Labor is hidden, misattributed, priced, paid, allocated, or scoped. | Work happened, but its record state and source are unclear. |
| Hook | Make labor visible or distinguish payment and price. | “Work happened is not a work record.” |
| Visual metaphor | Labor category, contribution, cost, payment, or scope card. | A `WORK EVENT` card where a pencil tries to change `ESTIMATED` into `RECORDED` and is stopped. |
| Proof surface | Labor visibility, contribution, cost, payment, or work scope fields. | `DESIGN / TASK / ROLE / DATE / STATE / SOURCE / QUANTITY OR HOURS / COMPENSATION STATE / NOTES / DECISION OWNER`. |
| Emotional turn | Hidden work gains visibility or a price/payment state gains context. | Remembered work becomes an explicit, bounded record without silently becoming a wage or accounting conclusion. |
| Final line | Context-specific prior line. | “Name the work state before you count the work.” |

The candidate is distinct from occupied `labor-visibility-state`, `contribution-scope`, `price-basis`, `price-sensitivity-state`, `payment-state`, `cost-allocation-state`, `expense-state`, `work-scope`, `sample-context`, `honest-price`, and `labor-evidence-state`. It is also distinct from pass 260 `evidence-consequence-state`, which concerns what a piece of evidence can support; pass 261 concerns the state and provenance of a work event before it becomes evidence or business math.

## Dramatic engine choice

**Subject:** A fictional `WORK EVENT` card with `RECORDED`, `ESTIMATED`, and `PLANNED` state strips.
**Desire:** Preserve what work happened without losing its status or source.
**Obstacle:** Memory, estimate, plan, donation, and completed record can be silently collapsed into one labor total.
**Stakes:** A designer may mistake a remembered task for a settled cost, wage, tax position, price input, or profitability result. The film must not claim this happened to a Stitch & Scale user.
**Change:** Move from visible work to explicit event state, source, date, and decision owner.
**Contradiction:** A work event can be real while its compensation, quantity, or business treatment remains undecided.
**Escalation:** Design; task; role; date; state; source; quantity/hours; compensation state; notes; owner.
**Final image:** `WORK EVENT → STATE → SOURCE → HUMAN DECISION`, with `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.
