# Pass 262 Wide Research — Publication Context State

**Research date:** 2026-08-20
**Pass:** 262
**Question:** What context must travel with a published pattern or export so a later reader can identify its source revision, format, locale, scope, last action, and known limitations without mistaking metadata for approval?

## Decision summary

The research changes the concept from generic publication readiness or export identity to a sharper **publication-context boundary**. A pattern can look published while remaining ambiguous if the reader cannot identify the source revision, format, locale, supported scope, last action, or known limitation. A schematic provides shape and measurement context, but it does not by itself prove fit or publication readiness. Preservation metadata offers a useful analogy: technical and action context should travel with a digital object, but preservation metadata is not a pattern standard or a Stitch & Scale feature proof.

The planned illustrative proof surface is a `PUBLICATION CONTEXT` card with `OBJECT / SOURCE REVISION / FORMAT / LOCALE / SCOPE / LAST ACTION / KNOWN LIMITATION / OWNER / STATUS`. This is not a current Stitch & Scale publication registry or product UI. It will carry `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI` and remain blocked.

## Audience and craft-workflow interpretation

The audience pain is not merely missing metadata. It is the moment a designer or reader sees a polished PDF, phone preview, or printed page and cannot tell which source revision, locale, format, scope, or known limitation it represents. The pattern’s visual surface may be correct while its context is missing. The record should travel with the object without silently becoming a release approval.

The standing campaign objective remains recruiting founding testers through the free demo and early-access cohort. No event, seasonal deadline, competitor comparison, testimonial, urgency hook, adoption statement, customer outcome, publication guarantee, or platform performance claim is adopted. Because Knitty’s current page could not be extracted, this note makes no Knitty-specific submission assertion.

## Product-truth reconciliation

No current Stitch & Scale product-proof registry entry or approved non-private capture verifies a publication-context record, object identity, source revision, format, locale, scope, last action, known limitation, owner, or status surface. Therefore the package is **blocked**. The paper card is not current product UI and must not be presented as a verified publication registry, release gate, PDF exporter, locale manager, fit checker, preservation system, or approval tool.

The concept must not claim that Stitch & Scale automatically attaches metadata, guarantees future readability, proves fit, validates every locale, makes a PDF accessible, makes a pattern publication-ready, or replaces physical print review, chart readability review, schematic-scale review, technical editing, or test knitting. Those remain distinct human evidence gates.

## Rights, visual opportunity, and asset decisions

Use an original paper `PUBLICATION CONTEXT` card, one neutral pencil, one blank export sheet, and one blank “known limitation” strip with no real pattern content, source revision, locale, customer data, file metadata, logo, or third-party media. Do not use a real PDF, chart, schematic, private export, customer document, platform screenshot, or copyrighted music. If a current product capture is ever supplied, verify version, destination, privacy, owner, rights, exact labels, locale behavior, and release state before use.

The visual opportunity is a polished blank export that receives a final paper band: `SOURCE REVISION: ? / FORMAT: ? / LOCALE: ? / SCOPE: ? / LAST ACTION: ? / KNOWN LIMITATION: ?`. A hand refuses to stamp `PUBLISHED` and writes `CONTEXT REQUIRED`. The emotional turn is from surface completion to context honesty.

## Platform, caption, audio, and acceptance decisions

Use a mobile-native 9:16 master under 45 seconds with one founder narrator, one microphone family, one room tone, and one pencil-on-paper sound motif. Captions may not cover the card, export sheet, context fields, hands, or CTA. Review caption-on and caption-free versions independently. The source revision, format, locale, scope, last action, known limitation, and `CONTEXT REQUIRED` boundary must remain understandable with audio muted and captions disabled. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, stale UI, unsupported publication or accessibility claims, unlicensed audio, and dense overlays.

## Research conclusion

The evidence supports the materially distinct angle `publication-context-state`: show that a published-looking pattern needs source, format, locale, scope, last action, and known-limitation context before a reader can responsibly identify what it is. It does not authorize a Stitch & Scale publication registry, export, preservation, locale, accessibility, fit, or release capability claim. The package remains blocked until current product proof, rights, platform, caption, audio, and acceptance evidence exist.
