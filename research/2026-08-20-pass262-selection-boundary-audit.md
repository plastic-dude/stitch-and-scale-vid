## Dramatic engine choice

**Subject:** A fictional `PUBLICATION CONTEXT` card attached to a blank export sheet.
**Desire:** Let a later reader identify what pattern or export they are looking at.
**Obstacle:** A polished surface can omit source revision, format, locale, scope, last action, and known limitation.
**Stakes:** A reader may mistake a preview or PDF for a final, universal, fit-checked, accessible, or current publication. The film must not claim this happened to a Stitch & Scale user.
**Change:** Move from a surface that looks complete to explicit context fields and `CONTEXT REQUIRED`.
**Contradiction:** Metadata can improve identification without being approval, accessibility conformance, fit proof, or publication readiness.
**Escalation:** Object; source revision; format; locale; scope; last action; known limitation; owner; status.
**Final image:** `OBJECT → CONTEXT → LIMITATION → HUMAN DECISION`, with `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.
