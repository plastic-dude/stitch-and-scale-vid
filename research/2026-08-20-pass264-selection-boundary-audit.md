## Dramatic engine choice

**Subject:** A fictional `OUTSIDER POSITION` card replacing a generic `FOUNDER STORY` card.
**Desire:** Participate in building a useful tool without claiming craft authority.
**Obstacle:** A family connection and a developer title can be misread as maker expertise.
**Stakes:** Designers and makers may be represented by someone who has not done their work; the film must not claim that this happened to a Stitch & Scale user or community.
**Change:** Move from origin-story credibility to explicit role, knowledge limit, reviewer relationship, and contribution request.
**Contradiction:** A founder can be motivated by a craft story while not owning the craft identity or the community’s authority.
**Escalation:** Role; motivation; what I know; what I do not know; who checks; what I am asking; status.
**Final image:** `ROLE → LIMIT → REVIEWER → ASK`, with `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.
