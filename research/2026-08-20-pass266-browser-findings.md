# Pass 266 Wide Research — Expense Source State

**Research date:** 2026-08-20
**Pass:** 266
**Question:** Which minimum fields make an independent designer’s expense event auditable and useful without implying tax treatment, profitability, or automatic reconciliation?

## Decision summary

The fresh question selects `expense-source-state`. Research supports a narrow business-record angle: a useful expense event should retain the underlying source, date, amount, currency/unit, category, design/project context, and evidence status instead of collapsing into a polished total. The film must distinguish a **recorded expense**, a **derived rollup**, an **estimate**, and **tax/accounting treatment**, and must not imply that Stitch & Scale reconciles bank accounts, determines deductibility, calculates profit, or replaces a bookkeeper or accountant.

The visual concept is an original expense card that starts as `TOTAL` and breaks into `EVENT / DATE / AMOUNT / CURRENCY / CATEGORY / PROJECT / SOURCE / STATUS`. It is illustrative, not current Stitch & Scale UI, and remains blocked because no current product-proof registry entry or approved capture verifies the exact expense source surface.

## Audience and campaign interpretation

For an independent knitwear designer, a material purchase, sample cost, tech-editing invoice, photography expense, marketplace fee, shipping charge, or test-knit payment can be useful only when its source and context remain visible. The audience pain is not “a dashboard lacks a total”; it is that a clean total may hide whether the item was recorded, estimated, derived, duplicated, assigned to the wrong design, or supported by a receipt or source note.

Assume the standing campaign objective is recruiting founding testers through the free demo and early-access cohort. Audience: independent knitwear designers and pattern publishers maintaining design, sample, testing, publication, and sales records. Form: organic 9:16 short for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts, 35–45 seconds. Tone: quiet, precise, practical, and suspicious of totals detached from their evidence. Brief tier: standard single-original-video planning package. No tax, accounting, profit, reconciliation, deductible, or financial-outcome claim is adopted.

## Product-truth reconciliation

The craft-business record model calls for stored events with date, amount/quantity, currency/unit, category, source note, and stable identifier, while derived rollups must remain visibly derived. This internal workflow guidance is not current product proof. No current product-proof registry entry or approved non-private capture verifies an expense event, source note, project link, supporting-document state, recorded/estimated/planned label, or rollup lineage surface. The paper card must carry `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.

The concept must not claim that Stitch & Scale automatically imports receipts, reconciles bank transactions, determines tax treatment, categorizes every expense, calculates profit, validates deductibility, or replaces a bookkeeper/accountant. It may invite designers to inspect whether a record retains its source and status.

## Rights, visual opportunity, and asset decisions

Use original paper cards, a blank receipt-shaped rectangle with no real merchant data, a pencil, and a neutral desk. Do not use a real receipt, invoice, bank statement, customer record, tax form, accounting software screenshot, financial account, personal identifier, third-party logo, or unlicensed music. If a current product capture is later supplied, verify version/date, exact labels, privacy, destination, rights, and whether the surface is a stored event, source attachment, rollup, estimate, or tax-related field.

Visual sequence: a large card reading `TOTAL` is split into `EVENT / DATE / AMOUNT / CURRENCY / CATEGORY / PROJECT / SOURCE / STATUS`. A smaller card reads `RECORDED`, another `DERIVED`, and a blank one reads `ESTIMATE — CHECK BASIS`. The emotional turn is from a comforting total to a traceable event.

## Platform, caption, audio, and acceptance decisions

Use one narrator family, one tabletop, one pencil-on-paper sound motif, and a 9:16 master under 45 seconds. Open on `A TOTAL IS NOT THE SOURCE` in the first second. Keep every card readable in the central safe region. Captions may not cover the cards, hands, receipt-shaped prop, numbers, or footer. Review caption-on, caption-free, and muted comprehension separately. Reject tax-form imagery, bank-feed animation, account balances, profit claims, reconciliation sounds, mixed voices, stale product captures, and unsupported accounting language.

## Research conclusion

The evidence supports a materially distinct angle about **source-carrying expense events**, not price basis, cost allocation, labor visibility, receipt identity, estimate status, or a P&L result. The package remains blocked until current product proof, rights, caption, audio, platform, and acceptance evidence exist.
