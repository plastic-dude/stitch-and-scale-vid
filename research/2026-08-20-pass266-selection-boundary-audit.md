## Dramatic engine choice

**Subject:** A large `TOTAL` card breaking into eight source-carrying fields.
**Desire:** Let a designer inspect what an expense represents before it becomes a summary.
**Obstacle:** A total looks authoritative while event source, date, category, project, and status remain hidden.
**Stakes:** A designer may misread a derived rollup or estimate as a recorded transaction or attach it to the wrong design. This is a workflow risk framing, not a claim of user harm.
**Change:** Move from summary-first bookkeeping to source-carrying record discipline.
**Contradiction:** A simple record can be more trustworthy when it preserves context instead of displaying more financial analysis.
**Escalation:** `TOTAL`; `EVENT`; `DATE`; `AMOUNT`; `CURRENCY`; `CATEGORY`; `PROJECT`; `SOURCE`; `STATUS`; `RECORDED / DERIVED / ESTIMATE`.
**Final image:** `KEEP THE SOURCE BESIDE THE EXPENSE.` with `ILLUSTRATIVE — NOT PRODUCT UI`.
