## Fresh research question

What platform-specific constraints must be checked before a 9:16 video can be treated as ready for vertical distribution, especially when the product proof contains small UI text?

## External evidence

Google Ads recommends 1080×1920 for vertical video and provides universal safe-zone references because overlays, calls to action, and buttons can cover important creative elements. [1] Meta says key creative elements, text, and logos must stay inside the safe zone; for 9:16 Reels/Stories ads it specifically advises keeping the bottom 40% free of text, logos, and other key elements when disclaimers are used. [2] TikTok’s current in-feed guidance recommends 9:16 at least 540×960, limits displayed ad captions to four lines, and says safe-zone size depends on dimension, caption length, and additional formats. [3]

## Current product reconciliation

The active privacy-by-default contract is a 45-second 9:16 vertical proof film. Its critical evidence is the Local only/Saved state and the local-storage warning. The product proof must therefore remain in a protected visual area, while any captions stay synchronized and outside the warning, buttons, fields, and other proof. The active contract is published and remains the only new-video contract under the single-flight rule. No second brief is created.

## Decision

Record one precise single-flight blocker: hold all new campaign briefing until the published privacy contract has been generated and independently reviewed. The platform findings are a future adaptation gate, not a replacement brief. A later derivative must use platform-specific safe-zone checks rather than crop the master and assume compliance.
