# Firing 217 Source Notes

## NIST uncertainty and evaluation limits

Sources: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/measure/ and https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/

NIST AI RMF emphasizes reliable measurement, documented methods and metrics, acceptable performance limits, independent assessors, and documentation of uncertainty and limitations. It recommends identifying what cannot be measured, assessing external validity, documenting risks and errors, and using independent review to mitigate bias and conflicts. Applied to V002, disagreement between valid independent Reviewer packets is itself evidence requiring resolution; it must not be silently averaged or resolved by recency.

## Independent assessment and role boundaries

NIST calls for separate testing or assessment teams, documented delegation, and clear human-AI oversight roles. A Reviewer disagreement must be handled by an authorized adjudication path, not by the Director, Generator, or owner acknowledgement. The original packets and all correction or adjudication records must remain immutable and linked.

## Firing-217 application

V002 remains `video-ready-for-review`, with no independent terminal Reviewer event, owner action, `REMAKE_REQUIRED` order, approved-for-generation brief, or publication failure. Exactly one blocker should require a conflict packet when valid independent assessments disagree, including both event IDs, exact provenance, dimensions in dispute, methods, evidence, and authorized resolution. Until then, the lane remains closed and no MP4 is authorized.
