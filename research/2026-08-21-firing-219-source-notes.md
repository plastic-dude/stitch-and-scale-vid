# Firing 219 Source Notes

## NIST AI RMF Manage and Govern

Sources: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/manage/ and https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/govern/

NIST Manage says risk responses should be prioritized, documented, assigned, monitored, and revisited through the AI lifecycle. It recommends documenting residual risks, maintaining accessible system and risk documentation, defining update/re-verification responsibilities, and using incident response and appeal/override processes with human adjudication. NIST Govern emphasizes clear roles, delegated authority, current documentation, change management, incident response plans, and periodic review.

## Google SRE incident lifecycle

Sources: https://sre.google/resources/practices-and-processes/incident-management-guide/ and https://sre.google/workbook/incident-response/

Google SRE describes incident response as coordinate, communicate, and control. It assigns explicit Incident Commander, Communications Lead, and Operations Lead roles, keeps a working record, escalates early, communicates consistently, and documents remediation and lessons after resolution. Actionable alerts should lead to a responsible response; non-actionable repeated alerts create noise. Closure should identify what was affected, what was done, who owns remaining actions, and what evidence confirms resolution.
