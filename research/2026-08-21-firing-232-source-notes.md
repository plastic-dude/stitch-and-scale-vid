# Firing 232 Source Notes

## NIST measurement consistency and independent assessment

Sources: https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/measure/, https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/, and https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ai/NIST.AI.100-1.pdf

NIST calls for objective, repeatable, scalable TEVV with documented metrics, methods, tools, limitations, uncertainty, and independent assessors. It recommends regularly assessing whether metrics and controls remain appropriate, documenting risks that cannot be measured, and using separate testing teams to support independent decisions and course correction. Applied here, a Reviewer’s seven scores should be tied to a stable rubric, calibration record, and explicit uncertainty rather than unexamined personal scale or drift.

## Calibration application

Calibration does not create a second terminal authority and does not permit averaging conflicting decisions. It is a measurement-quality control: the Reviewer should identify rubric version, calibration reference or anchor examples, any training or competency check, and whether the scoring process was performed independently. Material disagreement or evidence that the rubric was applied inconsistently is non-terminal until resolved by an authorized independent process.
