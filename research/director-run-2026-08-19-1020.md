## Wide-research result

The research pass consulted current knitwear workflow sources from Sister Mountain, Natalie in Stitches, and the School of SweetGeorgia. They describe grading as a barrier to publishing, spreadsheet workflows built from size charts, body measurements, ease, gauge, finished measurements, and stitch/row calculations, and the intimidation created by an unstructured blank spreadsheet. They also emphasize visualizing relationships, being explicit about what the numbers must do, and using fresh eyes or technical-editing conversations.

The research changed the creative decision in three ways. First, the story will show a concrete sequence of decisions rather than claim that Stitch & Scale magically solves grading. Second, the product proof will focus on one readable Spec Sheet surface rather than a dense full-screen table. Third, the emotional turn will respect the designer’s invisible labor and invite founding testers to audit the math rather than use hype or false certainty.
