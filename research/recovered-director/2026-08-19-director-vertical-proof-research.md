# Director Research — Vertical Proof and Caption Safety

**Research date:** 2026-08-19 UTC  
**Research question:** How should a vertical-first Stitch & Scale founding-tester cut adapt its duration, platform profile, and proof-safe caption design to current first-party platform rules without repeating the prior test-video obstruction?

## Findings

YouTube’s official Help Center states that, for standard channels, videos uploaded after October 15, 2024 with a square or vertical aspect ratio and a duration of up to three minutes are categorized as Shorts. The same guidance warns that a Short longer than one minute with an active third-party copyright claim may be blocked globally.[1]

Instagram’s official Help Center states that Reels can use an aspect ratio from 1.91:1 through 9:16, with a minimum frame rate of 30 FPS and a minimum resolution of 720 pixels.[2]

TikTok’s official Auction In-Feed Ads page was reachable but redirected to a resource page without exposing the specification text in the browser extraction. The first-party search result states that the safe-zone size depends on the dimension format, ad-caption length, and interactive add-ons. This is therefore a **Generator-side verification requirement**, not a Director-side assumption.[3]
