# Working Director Research — 2026-08-19

## Fresh question
How should a vertical-first Stitch & Scale tester-recruitment cut adapt its duration, platform profile, and proof-safe caption design to current first-party platform rules without repeating the prior test-video obstruction?

## Evidence captured so far

YouTube’s official Help page states that videos uploaded after October 15, 2024 with a square or vertical aspect ratio and a duration of up to three minutes are categorized as Shorts. It also warns that a Short over one minute with an active third-party copyright claim can be blocked globally. Source: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877?hl=en (accessed 2026-08-19).

## Initial decision impact

A short-form master can be designed as a 45–60 second 9:16 cut to keep the proof moment and tester invitation compact while avoiding unnecessary music-rights exposure beyond the one-minute threshold. The exact caption safe zone must still be declared from current TikTok and Instagram guidance and must preserve the UI/table proof area.

## Uncertainty

YouTube duration eligibility is clear from the first-party source. TikTok and Instagram safe-zone dimensions and platform-specific overlays still require first-party verification. No product UI claim is being inferred from platform research.


TikTok’s official Auction In-Feed Ads guidance was reachable but redirected to a resource page without exposing the specification text in the browser extraction. The search result and official URL establish that TikTok’s safe-zone size depends on the dimension format, ad-caption length, and interactive add-ons; this remains a platform-profile uncertainty that must be resolved by the Generator in its own current verification pass before export. Source: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads (accessed 2026-08-19; page redirected).

Instagram’s official Help Center states that Reels may use an aspect ratio from 1.91:1 to 9:16, with a minimum frame rate of 30 FPS and minimum resolution of 720 pixels. Source: https://help.instagram.com/1038071743007909 (accessed 2026-08-19).

## Revised decision impact

The distinct campaign should be a 9:16, 45–60 second tester-recruitment proof story with a platform matrix for YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels, and a TikTok derivative only after the Generator verifies the active TikTok ad/profile safe-zone file. The visual design must place the product proof in the upper-middle field and reserve a declared lower caption band, rather than relying on a full-screen table or generated labels.
