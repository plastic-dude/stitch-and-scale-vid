## Creative premise

The contradiction is simple: local ownership is not a magic shield. The product shows both the freedom and the responsibility. The film earns trust by refusing to hide the warning.

## Caption timing requirements

The Generator must produce a separate timed caption file from the exact spoken text. Captions must be two lines maximum and 34 characters per line maximum where possible. The caption-on review must demonstrate that no caption covers the header, product copy, warning, form field, logo, or primary action. If the sentence cannot fit safely, split the cue or redesign the shot; do not shrink the type until it becomes unreadable.

## Voice continuity requirements

Use one narrator family and one room-tone treatment for all six cues. No alternate takes with a different accent, age, microphone distance, or emotional register. Listen for doubled words, synthetic consonant artifacts, overlap, abrupt noise-floor changes, and music masking. A continuous transcript is not sufficient evidence of voice continuity.

## Prohibited additions

Do not add invented product UI, backup controls, cloud-sync states, prices, customer counts, accuracy percentages, revenue, testimonials, stock knitting footage, or captions that repeat the product text as decorative headlines. Do not use a generic “save time and scale your business” ending.
