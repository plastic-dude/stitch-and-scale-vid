## Performance direction

No fake legal authority, no courtroom voice, no dramatic accusation, no claims that a user was harmed, and no claim that the product currently clears rights. The pause after “different fields” is important: let the blank right column carry the contradiction before the narration names scope.

Do not add a second narrator, synthetic expert, creator testimonial, customer voice, legal voice, or platform voice. Do not improvise names, license numbers, URLs, product labels, customer outcomes, deadlines, or rights conclusions.
