Captions remain in a declared lane that never covers `CLAIM`, `FIELD`, `EVIDENCE_ID`, `SOURCE`, `VERSION`, `ACTIVITY`, `AGENT`, `REVIEWED`, `LINK_STATUS`, `LIMITATION`, or `DISPOSITION`, tables, numbers, labels, buttons, menus, hands, or primary actions. The sources inform evidence-model, responsibility, measurement-boundary, and production-review limits only; this script does not claim product provenance, traceability, assurance, or evidence linkage [903] [904] [905].
