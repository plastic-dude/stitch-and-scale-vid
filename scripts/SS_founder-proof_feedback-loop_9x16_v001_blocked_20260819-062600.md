## Spoken-word exclusions

Do not say “approved,” “resolved,” “correct,” “ready,” “release,” “validated,” “tested and passed,” “customers,” “trusted,” “complete,” or any unsupported capability, outcome, adoption, pricing, urgency, or accuracy statement. Do not imply that one feedback round replaces technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, or test knitting.
