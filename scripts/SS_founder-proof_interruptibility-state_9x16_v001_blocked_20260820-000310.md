## Caption map and accessibility

Captions contain the complete spoken dialogue and are reviewed against the workflow card. Meaningful non-speech cues may be labeled as `[stop click]` and `[review pending]` only when they carry information. Captions remain in a declared lane that never covers `RUN`, `CURRENT_STEP`, `PAUSE_REQUESTED`, `SAFE_STATE`, `SAVED`, `UNSAVED`, `RESUME_CONDITIONS`, `OWNER`, `REVIEWED`, `DISPOSITION`, tables, numbers, labels, buttons, menus, hands, or primary actions. Produce and review caption-on and caption-free masters. WCAG 2.2 requires captions for prerecorded synchronized media at Level A, but this script does not claim product conformance [873]. The W3C Pause, Stop, Hide guidance is used only to sharpen the distinction between a pause control and an inspectable workflow state [874].

## Audio plan

One narrator family, one voice, no mixed speakers. Use a clear dry recording with stable room tone. The start tone and stop click are original or cleared, quiet, and non-alarming. Hold silence while `SAFE_STATE` is undefined; use a neutral review tone, not a completion chime, alarm, urgency cue, or automation cue. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unlicensed music, or audio that implies autosave, recovery, AI oversight, or product conformance.
