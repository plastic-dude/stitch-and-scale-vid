Do not say “verified,” “certified,” “fact-checked,” “true,” “accurate,” “compliant,” “standard,” “proven,” “guaranteed,” “trusted,” or any unsupported capability, outcome, adoption, pricing, urgency, material, or evidence statement. The script may identify source types and a recheck need only; it must not imply the product verifies the result.
