## Caption map and accessibility

Captions contain the complete spoken dialogue and are reviewed against the transition rail. Meaningful non-speech cues may be labeled as `[status opened]`, `[transition gap]`, and `[review pending]` only when they carry information. Captions remain in a declared lane that never covers `CURRENT STATUS`, `STATUS_HISTORY`, `PRIOR_STATUS`, `EVENT`, `ACTOR`, `AT`, `REASON`, `AFFECTED_GATE`, `NEXT_STATUS`, `GAP`, `REVIEWED`, or `DISPOSITION`, tables, numbers, labels, buttons, menus, hands, or primary actions. Produce and review caption-on and caption-free masters. The sources inform logging, version-history, decision-record, and production-review boundaries only; this script does not claim product logging, audit, or governance behavior [900] [901] [902].

## Audio plan

One narrator family, one voice, no mixed speakers. Use a clear dry recording with stable room tone. Current-status and fictional-transition ticks are original or cleared and restrained. Hold silence at `GAP: EVENT-02`; use a neutral review tone, not an audit alarm, success chime, approval fanfare, urgency cue, or security-monitoring cue. Reject mixed voices, doubled words, overlaps, room-tone jumps, unlicensed music, or audio that implies product capability.

## Alternatives

If motion reduces legibility, use a static status-transition rail with current status, prior state, event, actor/time, reason, affected gate, missing event, next state, review, and disposition centered. If a real product capture is later supplied, do not insert it automatically; first record exact labels, version/date, rights, and changed claims, then rerun Director review. If the example uses a readiness status for a pattern, preserve calculation, technical editing, physical print review, chart readability, schematic scale, and test knitting as distinct human evidence gates; status history does not replace any of them.
